### Champion of Valeia, Goddess of Health

**Name:** **Elysia Greenbloom**

**Race:** **Half-Elf**

**Class:** **Cleric (Life Domain)**  
_Elysia is a devout healer and protector, her divine connection to Valeia manifests in her ability to mend wounds, cure ailments, and ensure the well-being of all those under her care. She wields the power of life itself to bring health and vitality to the world, striving to be a beacon of hope and renewal wherever she goes._

---

### Background and Lore:

Elysia Greenbloom was born to a human mother and an elven father in a small village nestled at the edge of a vast, verdant forest. From an early age, she showed an innate connection to nature and the living energy that flows through the world. As a child, she often played in the meadows, talking to the creatures of the forest and helping heal small animals with the herbs and flowers she gathered.

When she came of age, Elysia became an apprentice to a traveling healer who recognized her gift for restoring life and health. She was taught not only the arts of medicine and herbalism, but also the divine rituals of healing and protection. During a particularly harrowing journey, Elysia encountered a terrible plague sweeping through a city. Despite the overwhelming odds, she was able to save hundreds of lives with her gifts, proving herself a true servant of Valeia, goddess of health.

In her most trying moment, Valeia herself appeared to Elysia, acknowledging her dedication and heart for the well-being of others. The goddess granted Elysia a divine blessing, marking her as a champion of health, capable of channeling Valeia's healing power to an even greater degree.

Now, Elysia travels the world, spreading health and vitality wherever she goes, aiding the sick, protecting the weak, and offering her expertise to those in need. Her mission is to restore and preserve life, using the divine gift of healing to confront the forces of decay and death that threaten to consume the world.

### Appearance:

Elysia stands at a modest 5'8", with the graceful, delicate features of her elven heritage, complemented by the warm, earth-toned skin of her human side. Her long hair, a mix of soft brown and golden blonde, is often woven into intricate braids, symbolizing her connection to nature. Her eyes, a vibrant green, reflect the vitality of the world she works to protect.

She wears simple, yet elegant robes made from natural fibers, with a green-and-gold color scheme that represents both life and the divine favor of Valeia. A delicate vine pattern adorns the hems of her garments, symbolizing growth and healing. Around her neck, she wears a pendant shaped like a blooming flower, a symbol of Valeia's presence in her life.

Elysia carries a beautifully crafted staff, adorned with green gemstones and vine-like motifs, which she uses to channel her divine magic. At her side, she keeps a small pouch of healing herbs and salves, as well as a few vials of holy water.

### Personality:

Elysia is gentle, compassionate, and nurturing, always willing to lend a hand to those in need. Her heart is full of love and empathy, and she feels a deep connection to all living things. She is often described as a calming presence, radiating warmth and hope even in the darkest of times. While she exudes a serene and peaceful aura, she also has a fierce protective instinct when it comes to those under her care.

Despite her gentle demeanor, Elysia is unyielding in her resolve to heal and protect. She is driven by an unwavering belief that every life has value, and that health is the foundation of all things. She abhors sickness, disease, and suffering, and will go to great lengths to fight against forces that bring harm to the innocent.

Elysia believes that the natural world and the divine work in tandem to maintain balance, and she is ever-watchful for ways to mend broken ecosystems, cure diseases, and promote well-being. She has a deep respect for those who devote themselves to the preservation of life, and she is quick to offer aid to fellow healers, warriors, or adventurers, always with the hope of restoring and maintaining the vitality of those around her.

### Abilities:

- **Channel Divinity: Valeia's Blessing:** Elysia can call upon the power of Valeia to heal the most grievous of wounds. When she uses her Channel Divinity, she can restore a large amount of hit points to one creature within 30 feet, as if the goddess herself were mending their wounds. The healing is so powerful it can even cure diseases or remove poisons affecting the target.
- **Aura of Vitality:** Elysia radiates an aura that promotes health and vitality. Allies within a 10-foot radius of her gain additional hit points when they start their turn within her aura, as the divine energy of Valeia accelerates their natural healing processes.
- **Cleansing Touch:** Elysia can use her action to touch a creature and remove one disease or condition affecting it. This includes common afflictions such as blindness, paralysis, poison, or even curses. This ability allows Elysia to be a powerful force against the forces of decay and death.
- **Healer’s Radiance:** Elysia has the ability to channel Valeia's healing magic into radiant energy that can heal or protect others from harm. Once per long rest, she can release a burst of radiant healing energy in a 30-foot radius, restoring hit points to allies while damaging enemies that are harmed by radiant energy.
- **Divine Health:** Elysia’s bond with Valeia grants her resistance to diseases and poisons, as her very being is imbued with divine vitality. She is immune to any disease and gains advantage on saving throws against poison.
- **Aura of Restoration:** At higher levels, Elysia’s connection to Valeia strengthens, allowing her to grant even greater healing power. Allies within 30 feet of her who are reduced to 0 hit points are automatically stabilized, and they have advantage on death saving throws. Additionally, they gain temporary hit points when they regain consciousness.
- **Lay on Hands:** As a part of her clerical training, Elysia has mastered the ability to lay hands on others and heal their wounds. She can restore a set number of hit points per day (determined by her level), and she can also use this ability to cure poison or disease.
- **Healing Touch of the Wild:** Elysia is connected to the natural world and can draw strength from the earth itself. Once per short rest, she can use her healing touch to plant a seed in the ground, which grows into a small, vibrant plant that heals those who rest nearby. This magical plant offers additional healing benefits to anyone who spends a short rest near it.

### Motivation and Goals:

Elysia’s primary goal is to bring healing and health wherever she goes. She seeks to eliminate suffering, cure disease, and protect the vulnerable from harm. She has taken it upon herself to ensure that no one faces the horrors of death or decay without a fighting chance.

Her divine mission also extends to the preservation of the natural world, as Valeia's health is intrinsically tied to the health of the earth itself. Elysia strives to protect sacred groves, heal the wounded creatures of the world, and restore balance to ecosystems that have been corrupted or destroyed.

Elysia is also deeply invested in preventing the spread of plagues and diseases. She spends much of her time traveling to places where sickness threatens entire communities, offering her healing services to those who need it most. She believes that everyone deserves a chance at life and health, and she will do anything in her power to preserve that gift.

### Roleplay Tips:

- Elysia is always looking for ways to help others, whether it’s by providing healing, offering a listening ear, or using her knowledge to advise others on maintaining good health.
- She has an overwhelming empathy for others and often goes out of her way to comfort or tend to the wounded, sick, or emotionally distressed.
- Elysia may be seen as overly protective, particularly when it comes to people who are in need of healing. Her tendency to care for others can sometimes lead her to take on too much responsibility, but her unwavering compassion makes her a trusted ally and a steady presence in times of crisis.
- She is often found meditating, connecting with the divine energy of Valeia, or offering prayers in quiet moments, seeking guidance on how best to fulfill her mission.
