### Champion of Zehir, God of Affliction

**Name:** **Valthrax Bloodvenom**

**Race:** **Tiefling (variant)**

**Class:** **Warlock (The Fiend)**  
_Valthrax's pact with Zehir, the god of affliction, manifests in his mastery over poisonous magic, disease, and the power to curse his enemies, spreading affliction in both body and mind._

---

### Background and Lore:

Valthrax Bloodvenom was born under a cursed omen in the swamps of a forgotten kingdom. His parents were highborn aristocrats who sought power through forbidden rituals, seeking to control the forces of illness and decay to further their influence. When their pact with an eldritch being turned sour, they were stricken by a plague that consumed their bodies, leaving them as hollow shells of the people they once were. Valthrax, however, was untouched by the affliction, instead emerging stronger, with his skin marked by faint dark veins that resembled creeping vines of poison.

From a young age, Valthrax was drawn to the dark arts. He came into contact with Zehir, the god of affliction, whose dominion over disease, poison, and suffering resonated with him. Zehir saw potential in Valthrax's survival and ruthlessness, offering him a pact to become a champion of affliction. In exchange, Valthrax would wield Zehir’s power to spread sickness, corruption, and suffering, not only to others but also to further his own growth and mastery over the forces that once destroyed his family.

Valthrax was entrusted with spreading affliction in the form of curses, poisons, and diseases. He now wanders the world, both a harbinger of suffering and a twisted healer, as he offers affliction to those who cross his path, using his power to shape the fate of those who dare to defy him or seek his blessings.

### Appearance:

Valthrax stands at 6'2" with a lithe but muscular build, his movements as smooth and deliberate as a predator's. His skin is a deep crimson, with thin, black, vine-like veins crawling beneath the surface, constantly shifting and pulsing as if alive. His eyes glow a sickly yellow, and his long, black hair is streaked with silver, further accentuating his eldritch connection. His horns curl back from his forehead in jagged, snake-like spirals, reminiscent of serpents—a mark of Zehir’s influence. His nails are long and sharp, tinted greenish-black, reminiscent of poisoned blades.

He wears a dark cloak, stitched with arcane symbols, and adorned with twisted silver charms that rattle like bones as he moves. In his hands, he wields a staff, crowned with the skull of a serpent, from which tendrils of dark energy seem to emanate. His presence is chilling, and those who stand too close often feel the air grow colder, as if disease itself lingers around him.

### Personality:

Valthrax is calculating, cold, and merciless, with an air of detached cruelty. He believes that affliction, suffering, and decay are natural forces of the world, and that they have their place in the balance of life. While not particularly sadistic, he views pain and illness as necessary tools for growth, whether it be for individuals or societies. His presence can induce discomfort, but it is always purposeful. Valthrax rarely engages in pointless cruelty but is willing to do whatever it takes to achieve his goals—be it through manipulation, poison, or spreading disease.

He is not swayed by pity or compassion, and while he might help those who align with his vision, his aid often comes at a price. He values survival above all else, believing that only the strong and cunning are worthy of existence. Valthrax is also highly strategic, preferring subtlety and patience in his dealings, waiting for the right moment to strike with the venom of his poison or the torment of a curse.

### Abilities:

- **Poisonous Magic:** Valthrax can cast powerful poison and disease-based spells, his magic infused with the essence of Zehir’s dominion. Spells like _Poison Spray_, _Cloudkill_, _Bestow Curse_, and _Contagion_ are all part of his repertoire, and he can make these effects more potent and lasting than normal.
    
- **Venomous Touch:** Valthrax can imbue his touch with toxic energy. On a successful hit, his enemies are poisoned, suffering ongoing damage or debilitating effects as the poison courses through their veins.
    
- **Aura of Affliction:** Valthrax emanates an aura of sickness and decay. Enemies within a certain radius of him suffer disadvantage on saving throws against disease and poison effects. Additionally, any creature that starts its turn within this aura might be affected by a random affliction, such as nausea, weakness, or the onset of a curse.
    
- **Serpent’s Kiss (Pact Boon):** Valthrax’s pact with Zehir allows him to draw power from venom and suffering. He can summon a serpent-like familiar imbued with poison, which can deliver venomous bites or spread the disease of Zehir to those it touches.
    
- **Plaguebearer’s Blessing:** Valthrax can channel Zehir's power to bestow the blessing (or curse) of affliction upon others. He can curse enemies to slowly waste away or bless allies with resilience against disease and poison, though such blessings often come at a price, such as binding them to Zehir’s will.
    
- **Aura of Death’s Embrace:** As a champion of affliction, Valthrax can invoke a chilling aura that saps the life force of those around him. Enemies that remain too close for long become weaker, their vitality drained as they are suffused with the effects of Zehir’s domain.
    
- **Master of Disease:** Valthrax has unparalleled control over the spread of diseases, both magical and natural. He can unleash plagues upon cities or individuals, causing them to suffer from wasting sickness or mind-affecting afflictions. His diseases are often magical in nature, resistant to ordinary cures and requiring divine intervention to fully cure.
    
- **Serpent’s Revenge:** When struck by an enemy in combat, Valthrax can invoke Zehir’s wrath to deal damage to the attacker in the form of toxic energy, as a reflection of the serpent god’s power. This power can manifest as a poison spray or a poisonous aura surrounding him in retaliation.
    

### Motivation and Goals:

Valthrax Bloodvenom’s ultimate goal is to spread the influence of Zehir far and wide, bringing suffering and affliction to those who resist or oppose the natural cycle of disease, decay, and death. He sees himself as a necessary force in the world—a harbinger of change, one who removes the weak and encourages growth through pain and hardship. He seeks to create a world where only the strong, cunning, and resilient survive and thrive, purified through suffering.

Valthrax's methods may seem cruel, but in his mind, they are justified. He serves Zehir not out of blind devotion, but because he believes that affliction is the greatest teacher—offering a path to true power through survival and adaptation.
