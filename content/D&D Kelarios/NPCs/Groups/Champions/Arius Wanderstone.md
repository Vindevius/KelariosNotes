### Champion of Topolus, God of Location

**Name:** **Arius Wanderstone**

**Race:** **Human**

**Class:** **Ranger (Explorer, Horizon Walker Subclass)**  
_Arius Wanderstone is a steadfast and skilled explorer whose divine bond with Topolus, the god of location, grants him the ability to traverse the world's landscapes with unparalleled precision and understanding. He is a seeker of knowledge about the world's boundaries, its hidden paths, and the nature of reality itself, always in search of the perfect place to uncover the mysteries of existence._

---

### Background and Lore:

Arius Wanderstone was born in a remote village on the edge of an expansive mountain range. His early life was marked by a fascination with maps, charts, and the wilderness surrounding his home. As a young boy, he would often wander the nearby forests and hills, learning to navigate by the stars and by the land itself, developing an uncanny sense of direction.

When he reached adulthood, Arius left his home to pursue a life of exploration. He traveled across seas, through jungles, and scaled towering peaks, documenting his journeys in carefully drawn maps. During one of his journeys, he stumbled upon an ancient, forgotten temple hidden in a remote canyon. Inside, he discovered a shrine dedicated to Topolus, the god of location. Upon his touch, the god imbued him with divine power, marking him as a champion of locations and boundaries.

Since that moment, Arius has been granted divine insight into the nature of places and movement. He can sense the flow of energy that connects all spaces, and he understands the significance of every step, every path, and every destination. With Topolus’s blessing, Arius can navigate any terrain effortlessly, find hidden places, and even manipulate the very concept of space itself to shift between distant locations. His purpose is to seek out new horizons, explore lost worlds, and ensure that the balance of locations in the world remains intact.

Now, Arius roams the world as a champion of Topolus, uncovering the secrets of the land and the planes, protecting sacred locations, and ensuring that the flow of space and location is unbroken. He is both a wanderer and a guardian, constantly moving between places yet always mindful of the deeper meaning behind every location.

### Appearance:

Arius is a tall, broad-shouldered man with a rugged appearance, honed by years of travel and adventure. His weathered skin is sun-kissed and carries the marks of countless journeys. His dark brown hair is often kept short and practical, and his piercing gray eyes seem to constantly scan the horizon, always seeking the next destination. His expression is calm and thoughtful, exuding both wisdom and a quiet confidence.

He wears practical clothing suited for exploration: sturdy boots, a leather vest, and a cloak that blends in with the environment, helping him stay inconspicuous when needed. A long, intricate map of his travels is always rolled up and fastened at his waist, and at his side, he carries a compass, a symbol of his devotion to Topolus. He also wears an amulet shaped like a map of the world, with shifting landmarks that are constantly changing and adapting, reflecting the god's ever-changing influence on the world.

His weapon of choice is a finely crafted longbow, symbolizing his connection to both the land and the horizon. He also carries a shortsword for close combat, though he prefers to keep his distance and rely on his tracking and environmental knowledge.

### Personality:

Arius is calm, methodical, and deeply introspective. He has an innate understanding of the world's geography, and he holds a reverence for the natural balance of locations, both in the material plane and the planes beyond. He tends to be a quiet presence, preferring to observe and listen rather than speak. However, when he does speak, it is often in measured, thoughtful words that carry weight and insight.

He is deeply dedicated to exploration and discovery, always seeking to understand the world’s boundaries—whether that means charting unknown territories or uncovering the hidden significance of sacred and forgotten locations. Arius values the importance of place, believing that each location holds power and meaning, from the bustling cities to the desolate ruins. He is always mindful of the interconnectedness of the world and sees himself as a caretaker of places and the secrets they hold.

Arius has a profound sense of duty when it comes to preserving the sanctity of places, whether that means guarding sacred sites or ensuring that certain locations are not exploited for evil purposes. His devotion to Topolus drives him to protect the places of the world, as he believes that they are not merely locations—they are part of the essence of the world itself.

Despite his intense dedication to his god and his mission, Arius is not without his lighthearted moments. He has a deep appreciation for the beauty of the world and the stories of those he meets. Though he spends most of his time traveling alone, he values companionship on his journeys, seeing the bonds between people as another vital part of the world's tapestry.

### Abilities:

- **Channel Divinity: Topolus’s Path:** Arius can use his divine connection to Topolus to instantly know the best and most efficient path to his destination, whether it is a physical location or a plane of existence. This ability grants him the ability to travel through difficult terrain with ease, ignore difficult environmental effects (like wind, fog, or heavy rain), and even find shortcuts that others cannot.
- **Horizon Walker’s Portal:** Arius can open a portal to a location he has previously visited, enabling him to travel between distant places in the blink of an eye. Once per long rest, Arius can create a one-way portal to a location he knows, allowing him and up to six others to step through and arrive instantly.
- **Sense Location:** With his connection to Topolus, Arius can sense the presence of certain locations or objects within a certain radius, including hidden places, magical wards, and even planes of existence. This ability grants him a sort of "map vision," allowing him to sense the layout of his surroundings and navigate with ease, even in unfamiliar locations.
- **Location Mastery:** Arius can memorize and recall any location he has visited. He can instantly know detailed information about any place he has previously been, including the layout, important landmarks, hazards, and any special properties it may have. This includes both physical places and abstract locations such as certain planes of existence.
- **Divine Mark of the Location:** Topolus has marked Arius with his divine blessing. Once per day, Arius can designate a location to be protected. This location becomes a place of power where allies gain temporary hit points each time they rest there, and any hostile creatures within its vicinity must succeed on a saving throw to avoid disadvantage on attacks against Arius and his allies.
- **Endless Horizon:** Arius can call upon the divine power of Topolus to extend his knowledge and perception of the world’s boundaries. For a short duration, he gains the ability to move without leaving a trace, ignore difficult terrain entirely, and pass through walls or natural obstacles, representing his ability to traverse all locations without restriction.
- **Guardian of Places:** Arius can call upon his bond with Topolus to animate and protect sacred or significant locations. Once per long rest, he can imbue a location with divine energy, causing it to protect and defend itself from invaders. This might manifest as shifting terrain, barriers of energy, or the manifestation of ethereal guardians to protect the area.

### Motivation and Goals:

Arius Wanderstone's primary goal is to explore, protect, and understand the locations that shape the world. He seeks to uncover forgotten places, preserve sacred sites, and ensure that the natural order of locations remains unbroken. His devotion to Topolus drives him to explore the unknown, map the uncharted, and protect the sacred places of the world from corruption or exploitation.

His ultimate aim is to achieve a greater understanding of how the planes of existence and the material world are interwoven. He hopes that by unlocking the secrets of locations, he can protect the boundaries between realms and ensure that no place is ever lost or forgotten.

Arius also wishes to connect with other explorers and protectors of places, believing that the shared wisdom of travelers and guardians can create a better world. Though he is a solitary figure, his travels often take him into the company of those who share his desire to explore and safeguard the world, and he is always eager to share his knowledge with those who seek it.

### Roleplay Tips:

- Arius is quiet and reflective, preferring to listen rather than speak, especially when exploring new places or when facing challenges in unfamiliar locations.
- He is extremely observant of his surroundings, constantly analyzing the terrain, the structures, and the layout of any place he visits.
- He is deeply respectful of nature, history, and the significance of locations, and will often go out of his way to protect places from harm.
- When encountering a new place, Arius is likely to take notes or create a mental map, marking out important landmarks and assessing the location’s energy.
- Arius is always searching for new locations, whether they be forgotten ruins, sacred grounds, or unexplored realms, and can become driven to uncover hidden secrets if he senses there’s something important to be found.
