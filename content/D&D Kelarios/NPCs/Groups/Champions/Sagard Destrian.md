**Champion of Ruichasm, God of Destruction**

**Name:** Sagard Destrian  
**Race:** Human  
**Class:** Paladin (Oathbreaker)

---

**Background and Appearance:**  
Sagard Destrian is a towering, grim figure with a hardened face, marked by deep scars that reflect his brutal journey. His once-pristine armor is battered and tarnished, a symbol of his broken oath and descent into the service of Ruichasm, the god of destruction. His deep-set eyes are a stormy gray, and his expression is often one of simmering rage, reflecting the violent fury that has taken hold of him. His dark, unkempt hair cascades around his shoulders, a symbol of his abandonment of the discipline and order that once defined him.

Sagard wears the remnants of his paladin’s regalia—cracked and shattered plates of armor adorned with insignias that now seem hollow. His sword, once a shining beacon of his oath, is now blackened, its blade twisted and jagged, as if it were forged from the very essence of ruin itself. It crackles with destructive energy, a tool of devastation that only serves to reinforce the violent power Ruichasm has bestowed upon him.

His cloak is a deep black, stitched with runes that glow faintly in the presence of destruction. Sagard's physical presence is imposing, exuding a feeling of raw energy, as though the world around him is perpetually on the verge of collapse when he is near.

---

**Personality:**  
Sagard is a man consumed by wrath and destruction, shaped by his fall from grace. Once a noble and just paladin, he was torn from the path of righteousness by the devastating loss of his family, which he believes was orchestrated by divine forces beyond his control. This loss triggered his vow of vengeance, leading him to abandon his Oath of Devotion and embrace the destructive power of Ruichasm.

Sagard’s sense of justice has twisted into something darker and more merciless. He believes that the world must be broken down, that only through destruction can true balance and rebirth emerge. His views are nihilistic—he believes that life’s creation and the gods’ interference have led to a world that must be torn apart before it can ever be remade. His thirst for vengeance drives him, and he sees the suffering he causes as necessary to achieve the god of destruction's will.

While he is driven by fury, there is a lingering sadness in his heart, a trace of the man he used to be. He still struggles with the memory of his lost family and the honor he once held. Deep down, there is a part of him that longs for redemption but believes it can only be attained through the utter obliteration of the forces that govern the world.

---

**Abilities and Role:**  
As the champion of Ruichasm, Sagard draws his power from the god of destruction, using his paladin abilities in a twisted and violent way. His powers focus on obliteration, disintegration, and the overwhelming force of chaos. Through his pact with Ruichasm, he has abandoned the ideals of his previous oath in exchange for the destructive might to break down the world and its defenses.

Some of his notable abilities include:
- **Aura of Ruin**: Sagard emanates an aura of destruction that weakens the integrity of everything within range. Buildings crack, weapons lose their sharpness, and the wills of his enemies falter in the face of overwhelming despair. Allies within this aura grow bolder and more destructive, fueled by the god of destruction's power.
- **Destructive Smite**: Sagard’s smites are imbued with Ruichasm's devastating energy. When he strikes with his sword, the blow causes explosive damage that tears apart armor, flesh, and bone alike. His smites also leave lingering destruction in the environment, turning the ground beneath his feet into cracked earth or rubble.
- **Breaking Oath**: Sagard’s fall from grace as a paladin manifests in his ability to corrupt the magic of divine oaths. He can break oaths and holy bonds, rendering magical protections useless and causing any sacred spells or protections to unravel. He can also curse others by forcing them to abandon their own vows or promises.
- **Wave of Devastation**: Sagard channels the full wrath of Ruichasm, summoning a wave of destructive energy that crushes everything in its path. The wave decimates enemies, shattering defenses and collapsing structures. It leaves nothing but ruin in its wake, and those caught in it are torn apart by raw force.
- **Aura of Destruction**: As a champion of Ruichasm, Sagard can create an aura of destruction that enhances his offensive power. His attacks deal additional force damage, and those who strike him are met with the same destructive energy, causing their attacks to backfire or disintegrate upon impact.
- **Champion's Vengeance**: Sagard can channel the vengeance he feels for his family’s death, summoning the destructive wrath of Ruichasm to empower his strikes. With this ability, he gains immense strength and speed, delivering devastating blows that cause lasting damage to enemies and the environment alike.
- **Extermination's Blessing**: Ruichasm’s destructive blessing grants Sagard the ability to bring ruin to the battlefield, targeting weak points and focusing his attacks on areas that are most vulnerable. This power allows him to bring down foes, both physical and divine, with terrifying precision.

---

**Story and Motivation:**  
Sagard Destrian was once a paladin of great renown, a man who upheld the ideals of justice and devotion with unwavering dedication. However, his life was shattered when his wife and child were killed in a raid that seemed all too orchestrated by the gods themselves. The gods' indifference to his suffering drove him into madness, and he began to question their divine mandates.

In his grief, Sagard abandoned his oath of devotion, embracing a new path of destruction that led him to Ruichasm, the god of devastation. He saw Ruichasm as the only god who understood the true nature of the world—an endless cycle of creation and destruction. Ruichasm offered Sagard the power to destroy everything that had ever been dear to him and, in doing so, provide the world with the purification it needed. In return for his unwavering service, Ruichasm granted him terrible power—enough to tear down kingdoms, crush gods, and bring about the end of the world as it stood.

Sagard's current motivation is simple: vengeance, destruction, and the belief that the world must be torn apart to allow for true rebirth. He believes that through the destruction of the world, the gods will be forced to finally acknowledge the suffering they have wrought, and only then can something truly new emerge from the ashes. He is a force of unrelenting vengeance, seeking to undo everything he once fought to protect.

---

**Motivation and Goals:**

- To avenge the death of his family and bring about the destruction of those responsible, including the gods who have forsaken him.
- To serve Ruichasm in the belief that destruction is the only way to bring true change and purification to the world.
- To tear down civilizations, corrupt holy places, and break the systems of order and justice that once upheld him.
- To spread the message of Ruichasm—creation leads to destruction, and destruction leads to renewal.
- To become a harbinger of total annihilation, proving that the gods and the world itself can be reduced to nothing.

---

**Weapons and Tools:**

- **The Ruinblade**: A massive, jagged longsword that crackles with destructive energy. Forged from the remnants of a once-glorious weapon and imbued with Ruichasm’s raw power, it is capable of cleaving through both flesh and stone with ease. Its strikes cause explosions of force that devastate the area around it.
    
- **Cloak of Desolation**: A tattered black cloak that constantly emanates an aura of decay and despair. It shields Sagard from divine magic, preventing it from healing him and turning any attempts to restore life into moments of agony.
    
- **Sigil of Ruin**: A bloodstained sigil, once a holy symbol of Sagard’s order, now corrupted by the god of destruction. It serves as a conduit for Ruichasm’s destructive power, allowing Sagard to call down devastating forces on his enemies, especially in times of vengeance.
    

---

**Legacy and Influence:**  
Sagard Destrian is a harbinger of ruin, a living testament to the destructive will of Ruichasm. His presence on the battlefield is one of terror, as entire cities are reduced to rubble in his wake. His power is not just one of physical destruction but of ideological collapse—he destroys beliefs, promises, and oaths, sowing discord and turning allies into enemies. With every act of devastation, he grows closer to Ruichasm’s ultimate goal: the complete annihilation of the world as it is known, so that a new age of ruin and rebirth can rise from the ashes.
