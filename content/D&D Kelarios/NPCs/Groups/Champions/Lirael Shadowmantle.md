### Champion of the Raven Queen, Goddess of Death

**Name:** **Lirael Shadowmantle**

**Race:** **Shadar-kai** (a race closely tied to the Shadowfell, often seen as emissaries of the Raven Queen)

**Class:** **Paladin (Oath of the Grave)**  
_Lirael is a servant of the Raven Queen, dedicated to the delicate balance between life and death, ensuring that souls are shepherded to their rightful place and that death is met with dignity and reverence._

---

### Background and Lore:

Lirael Shadowmantle was born in the Shadowfell, the gloomy and desolate realm that mirrors the material world. She was orphaned at a young age, her family lost to the eternal darkness of the Shadowfell's corruption. It was then that the Raven Queen, goddess of death, took notice of her. In a vision, the Raven Queen appeared to Lirael and called her into service, offering her the mantle of a champion—a paladin who would serve as a guide to the dead, a protector of souls, and a guardian of the veil between life and death.

Lirael accepted the Raven Queen's offer, drawn by the promise of purpose in the eternal dance of life and death. She was trained in the art of death's touch, learning the ways of necrotic magic and the oaths of guardianship. As a champion of the Raven Queen, Lirael now walks the worlds, silently guiding lost souls, hunting down those who defile death or cheat their fate, and ensuring that the flow of life and death remains unbroken. She seeks to uphold the Raven Queen's ideals, respecting the sanctity of death and the inevitable passage of all souls, even as she dispatches those who would disrupt that balance.

Lirael's sacred charge is to confront the forces of undeath, guiding restless spirits, and ensuring that those who should pass are allowed to do so, while punishing those who cling to life in unnatural ways. She wields her connection to the Raven Queen's power to remind mortals of the fragility of life and the importance of accepting their inevitable end with honor.

### Appearance:

Lirael stands at 5'9", her lithe yet strong frame draped in a flowing cloak of deep black feathers, with hints of silver threading through them like ethereal strands of starlight. Her skin is pale with a faint grayish tint, an inheritance from the Shadowfell's bleakness, while her dark eyes are filled with the wisdom and sorrow of countless souls she's guided. Her long, black hair is streaked with silver and falls in waves around her shoulders, and her sharp features are framed by a soft yet somber expression.

She wears a mask of dark metal shaped like a raven's beak, a gift from the Raven Queen herself, which she wears in battle to conceal her identity. Her armor is light and sleek, resembling a blend of shadow and feathers, offering her both mobility and protection. In her hand, she wields a staff of twisted black bone, topped with a raven's skull, from which the faint glow of souls seems to emanate. Her presence exudes an aura of tranquility and inevitability, as though death itself walks alongside her.

### Personality:

Lirael is calm, stoic, and wise beyond her years. She understands the inevitability of death and sees it not as an enemy, but as a natural part of existence. She does not view death as something to be feared but rather as a peaceful release, and she is resolute in her duty to ensure that souls are properly guided to their afterlife.

She is respectful of those who accept death with dignity but is uncompromising when it comes to those who defy it. Lirael has little patience for undead or those who seek immortality at the cost of others. Her sense of justice is unshakable, and she will go to great lengths to see that the dead are honored and that the natural order is preserved.

Though she is often quiet and introspective, she is not without compassion. She mourns the loss of those she has loved, and her empathy for the grieving is boundless. She offers solace and guidance to those struggling with loss, though she never strays from the understanding that death is a part of the cycle that must be embraced. In battle, she is swift and deadly, her strikes bringing finality to those who need to face death, and she can be both comforting and terrifying to those she encounters.

### Abilities:

- **Raven Queen’s Blessing:** Lirael has been granted the ability to command the dead and commune with spirits. She can speak to the recently deceased, offering them comfort, guidance, or instructions, and can even summon ethereal raven spirits to assist her in her duties, aiding in both combat and spiritual tasks.
- **Channel Divinity: Final Rest:** Lirael can use her Channel Divinity to release the souls of the dead, bringing peace to those who have passed. She can lay to rest restless spirits, sending them on to their afterlife or stopping undead from rising. This power also allows her to turn undead or command them to be still, halting their defilement of death.
- **Oath of the Grave (Paladin Subclass):** Lirael has taken an oath that binds her to the Raven Queen’s will. Her abilities focus on defending the natural order of life and death, laying judgment upon those who defy it. She can call down the Raven Queen’s power to deliver divine necrotic damage, curse enemies with the weight of death, or protect the living from the corrupting touch of undeath.
- **Soulstealer’s Strike:** Lirael's weapon strikes with necrotic energy, draining life force from her enemies. When she deals damage, a portion of the life force is siphoned from her enemies and transferred to her, healing her and empowering her strikes. This is especially potent against undead, as they cannot resist the death she commands.
- **The Raven’s Shadow:** Lirael can transform into a shadowy raven or summon a swarm of ravens to scout, gather information, or attack enemies. These ravens are manifestations of the Raven Queen’s will, and they can obscure the battlefield, making it difficult for her enemies to track or hit her.
- **Aura of Death’s Embrace:** Lirael exudes an aura that affects all creatures in the vicinity. Enemies within this aura suffer disadvantage on saving throws against necrotic damage, and if they drop to 0 hit points, they are immediately consumed by shadow and passed on to their final resting place. Allies within her aura gain resistance to necrotic damage and can heal from wounds by standing near her.
- **The Raven Queen’s Mark:** Lirael can place a mark on a creature, sealing its fate. This mark causes the creature to suffer additional necrotic damage whenever it is hit. The mark can also track the creature's soul, preventing it from escaping the natural flow of death.
- **Wings of the Raven:** As a manifestation of her divine connection to the Raven Queen, Lirael can grow shadowy wings that allow her to fly. The wings are not just for travel—they can also be used in combat to shield her from harm, as they are imbued with the Raven Queen’s divine protection.

### Motivation and Goals:

Lirael's primary goal is to serve the Raven Queen and ensure that the cycle of life and death is respected and maintained. She is on a mission to destroy any who would disrupt this natural balance, such as necromancers, those who practice forbidden magics, or entities that seek to alter death's course. She seeks to guide souls to their rightful resting place and to provide comfort to those suffering from loss.

Her ultimate goal is to uphold the Raven Queen's vision of death not as an end but as a necessary transition. She fights against undeath, knowing that it is a perversion of the natural order. Lirael strives to remind mortals that death is not to be feared, but embraced, for it is through death that one may find peace.
