**Champion of Eriseris, God of Wilderness**

**Name:** Ewart the Wildman  
**Race:** Tiefling (Red)  
**Class:** Druid (Circle of the Land - Wilderness)

---

**Background and Appearance:**  
Ewart the Wildman is a striking figure, his red skin contrasting sharply with the vibrant greens, browns, and blues of the wilderness he so deeply cherishes. With horns that curl upward like the branches of ancient trees, Ewart has the unmistakable appearance of someone both blessed and cursed by the forces of nature. His eyes gleam a bright amber, and his face often holds a wild, untamed look, reflecting his deep connection to Eriseris, the god of wilderness. His hair is a mass of wild, untamed curls that appear to shift like leaves in the wind, and he often wears a cloak made of interwoven vines and animal pelts, woven by his own hand.

His body is covered with intricate tattoos—natural patterns representing the animals, plants, and seasons that shape his world. A large tattoo of a stag’s antlers wraps around his shoulder and neck, symbolizing his connection to the beasts of the wild. He carries a gnarled staff crafted from an ancient tree branch, decorated with feathers and stones, a symbol of his bond with nature.

Despite his fearsome appearance, Ewart exudes an aura of calm and quiet strength. He is often found walking barefoot through the forest or standing atop a cliff, his eyes gazing out over the sprawling wilderness that he protects.

---

**Personality:**  
Ewart is a creature of the wild, deeply in tune with the rhythms of nature and the land. While he may appear wild and untamed, he is also wise, thoughtful, and patient. He has an inherent understanding of the natural world, and a deep empathy for all living things. To Ewart, every animal, every plant, and every breeze has its own spirit and purpose. His bond with Eriseris is reflected in his constant drive to protect and nurture the wilderness, seeing it as a source of life and balance for all beings.

Ewart often speaks in metaphor and imagery, using nature to explain his thoughts. He is slow to anger, but when he is pushed too far, his wrath can be as fierce as a storm or as unforgiving as a wildfire. He is deeply protective of the natural world and is quick to defend it from those who seek to harm it—whether through greed, ignorance, or malice.

Despite his seriousness when it comes to nature’s protection, Ewart has a gentle side. He enjoys the companionship of animals and fellow druids, and his heart swells with joy when he sees the wilderness thriving and untouched by destructive forces. He is drawn to those who share his respect for nature and will offer guidance to those who seek to understand the wilderness’ intricate balance.

---

**Abilities and Role:**  
As the champion of Eriseris, Ewart is granted profound powers that enable him to command the forces of the wild, influence the land, and communicate with animals and plants. His druidic powers are especially attuned to the raw, untamed elements of nature, such as the wilderness, the wild beasts, and the weather.

Some of his notable abilities include:
- **Wild Shape:**  
    Ewart’s connection with Eriseris allows him to assume the forms of various animals that live in the wild, from fierce predators to swift runners, and even creatures of the sky. His favored forms include a mighty stag, a swift fox, and a great eagle, each allowing him to move through the wilderness with the ease and grace of the animals he reveres.
- **Nature’s Wrath:**  
    As a champion of the wilderness, Ewart can call upon nature to assist him in battle. He can cause the earth to tremble beneath his feet, summon thorny vines to entangle his enemies, and call upon the fury of storms or wildfires to devastate those who threaten the land. His connection to Eriseris amplifies these powers, allowing him to strike with greater force when defending the wilds.
- **Animal Companion (Eriseris' Gift):**  
    Ewart is never without the companionship of a powerful animal ally, a gift from Eriseris himself. This animal companion could be a great wolf, a cunning hawk, or a mighty bear—each with unique abilities that assist Ewart in his mission to protect the wilderness. The bond between Ewart and his companion is unbreakable, and they fight as one.
- **Nature’s Healing:**  
    Ewart has a deep, intuitive understanding of the healing properties of plants and the restorative powers of nature. He can channel this knowledge to heal wounds, cure diseases, and revitalize the land itself. His healing spells often involve drawing upon the natural energy of the earth, using it to restore balance to the wounded or suffering.
- **Commune with Nature:**  
    Ewart has the ability to commune with nature itself. He can ask questions of the land, the trees, the wind, and the creatures of the world. This divine connection allows him to learn about the surrounding environment, the movements of animals, and even gain insight into natural disasters or shifts in the balance of the wilderness. This ability allows him to uncover hidden truths about the world and foresee threats to the natural order.
- **Eriseris' Blessing:**  
    Eriseris has gifted Ewart with the power to manipulate the environment around him. He can cause plants to grow and flourish, summoning roots and vines to form barriers, or even coax flowers and trees into life to aid him in his endeavors. This power also allows him to calm the wildest of storms or wild animals, invoking the primal forces of nature in a controlled way.
- **Guardian of the Wilds:**  
    Carrying the divine mantle of Eriseris, Ewart is a tireless protector of the wilderness. He can shield the land from destruction, using his power to bolster the resilience of the earth, animals, and plants. He can protect forests from fire, heal blighted lands, and encourage the growth of new life even in the face of adversity. His presence acts as a barrier to unnatural forces that seek to ravage the natural world.
- **Fury of the Earth (Channel Divinity):**  
    As a Champion of Eriseris, Ewart can channel the fury of the wilderness into a devastating display of power. This ability allows him to call upon the primal forces of nature, creating shockwaves, earthquakes, or violent storms that can scatter enemies, break down barriers, and restore the balance of the earth.

---

**Story and Motivation:**  
Ewart the Wildman grew up in a small, isolated village nestled in the heart of a vast forest. His family lived in harmony with the land, using the gifts of the wilderness to sustain themselves. From a young age, Ewart was drawn to the wilds, spending hours exploring the forests, studying the animals, and listening to the winds. His parents taught him the ways of the land, showing him how to survive in the wilderness, but also how to live in balance with it.

One day, while wandering deep in the forest, Ewart encountered a mighty spirit of the wild—Eriseris, the god of wilderness. Eriseris recognized the young tiefling's innate connection to the land and the wild creatures, and saw in him a worthy champion. The god of wilderness bestowed upon Ewart the divine gift of druidic power, marking him as a protector of nature.

Ewart’s devotion to Eriseris has since driven him to protect the wilderness at all costs. He has become a fierce defender of the forests, mountains, and plains that make up the natural world. Whether it’s defending a sacred grove from poachers, stopping a group of miners from tearing into the earth, or healing a poisoned river, Ewart’s mission is clear: protect the wilds, preserve the balance, and ensure that the land continues to thrive.

---

**Motivation and Goals:**

- To protect the wilderness from encroachment, exploitation, and destruction.
- To nurture the land and ensure the survival of endangered species and ecosystems.
- To prevent unnatural forces, such as dark magic or corrupting influences, from polluting the natural world.
- To spread knowledge of the balance between humans and nature, teaching others to live in harmony with the earth.
- To restore damaged or corrupted lands, returning them to their original, thriving state.

---

**Weapons and Tools:**

- **Wildwood Staff:** A staff carved from the heartwood of an ancient tree, imbued with the raw power of nature. This staff allows Ewart to channel his druidic powers, manipulating the earth, plants, and animals with ease.
- **Fur of the Wild:** A cloak woven from the fur and pelts of animals Ewart has befriended or protected. It grants him protection from the elements and allows him to blend seamlessly into the wilderness, becoming one with the land.
- **Totem of Eriseris:** A carved totem representing the essence of Eriseris, given to Ewart by the god. It serves as a symbol of his divine connection and grants him enhanced abilities to commune with nature and command the elements of the wilderness.

---

**Legacy and Influence:**  
Ewart the Wildman is known as a protector of the wilderness and a champion of nature's untamed beauty. His name is whispered among druids and rangers, and his feats of bravery in the name of nature are legendary. Wherever the wilds are threatened, Ewart can be found, ready to strike back with the fury of the earth itself. His influence spreads far and wide, teaching people that true strength comes from living in harmony with the land, not from controlling it.
