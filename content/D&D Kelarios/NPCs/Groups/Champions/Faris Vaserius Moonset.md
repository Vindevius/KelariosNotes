### Champion of Numeda, Goddess of Strength

**Name:** **Faris Vaserius Moonset**

**Race:** **Half-Elf**

**Class:** **Fighter (Rune Knight Subclass)**

---

### Background and Lore:

Faris Vaserius Moonset was born under strange circumstances, a child caught between the light of the stars and the shadows of forgotten realms. The product of a union between his mortal father, Kenzo Dere Moonset, a skilled blacksmith, and his mysterious mother, Diana, known to him only as Zia, who runs the tavern known as the **Wolves Den**, Faris grew up unaware of the divine nature of his mother. To him, Zia is simply his enigmatic and fiercely protective mother, who always had an air of mystery surrounding her. Little did he know that Zia is actually **Diana**, the mistress and goddess of shadow, who is always around, watching over her son while keeping her true identity hidden.

Raised by his father, Faris was taught the art of smithing, honing his physical strength and craftsmanship from a young age. Kenzo, a humble yet gifted smith, imbued his son with the importance of perseverance, resilience, and the value of hard labor. But there was more to Faris than met the eye. Though he was a natural with the forge, an innate magic began to stir within him, granting him unusual powers over shadows—traits inherited from his mother. This sorcerous gift, while subtle, sometimes made him feel like an outsider, torn between the solid world of steel and the fluid mystery of the arcane.

As Faris grew older, he began to notice that there was more to his lineage than his father ever told him. His mother’s connections seemed to reach far beyond the tavern, and he often had strange dreams of immense strength and divine figures. It wasn’t until he was 25 that he encountered **Numeda**, the goddess of strength. In a moment of peril, where Faris found himself facing an opponent far beyond his abilities, the goddess of strength appeared before him. In this moment of vulnerability, Numeda offered Faris a divine pact: to serve her and become her champion, harnessing the power of his heritage to embody strength in all its forms.

Since then, Faris has trained under Numeda’s divine guidance, learning to channel the full breadth of his power. His bond with the goddess has unlocked his true potential, pushing his physical capabilities to extraordinary levels. He became a **Rune Knight**, marking his body with ancient runes that channel the primordial power of strength, endurance, and resilience, while his mother's shadow magic manifests in subtle ways, allowing him to manipulate the shadows and dark energies around him.

Now, at 28 years old, Faris serves as Numeda’s Champion, a living embodiment of her strength, carrying the legacy of his mother’s shadow magic while embracing the divine power of might and fortitude granted to him by the goddess. Though he continues to search for the truth of his mother’s identity, Faris knows that his destiny lies in fighting for justice, honor, and the preservation of the strength that flows through him.

---

### Appearance:

Faris stands tall at 6'3", with a solid, muscular build that reflects his life of hard work and physical training. His olive-toned skin and sharp, angular features are a clear blend of his elven and human heritage. His dark brown hair, streaked with silver at the temples, falls just past his shoulders in unruly waves. His eyes are a striking shade of amber, sometimes appearing like pools of molten gold when he is channeling his divine power, and his gaze often carries a quiet intensity.

Faris wears a mix of practical armor and clothing adorned with runic markings that glow faintly in the dark, symbols that represent his pact with Numeda and his mastery of the Rune Knight arts. His attire is often a combination of practical, weathered leather and intricately crafted metal, indicative of his smithing roots. Around his neck, he wears a simple, black chain with a small pendant, a remnant of his mother’s influence. When he taps into his shadow magic, the shadows around him seem to twist and dance at his command, almost as if they recognize the bloodline from which he hails.

---

### Personality:

Faris is a man of few words, preferring actions to speak for him. His childhood in a humble blacksmith’s shop shaped him into a hard-working, dedicated individual, one who believes in the importance of effort and perseverance. However, beneath his stoic demeanor lies a complex soul filled with questions about his true heritage. He feels the constant pull between the strength of his father’s legacy—rooted in the tangible world of crafting and smithing—and the otherworldly, shadowy power granted by his mysterious mother.

Though he doesn’t know the full truth about his mother, Faris has a deep-seated loyalty to her, sensing that she has always been watching over him in some way. He respects the goddess Numeda with a quiet reverence, and he is deeply committed to fulfilling his role as her champion, constantly striving to push the limits of his physical strength and endurance while learning how to balance the shadowy magic within him.

He is deeply protective of those he cares about, and although he may come off as cold and detached to strangers, those who have earned his trust know him as a stalwart and reliable ally. Faris is a firm believer in justice and honor, but also in the inherent strength of individuals to overcome their own obstacles. His goals are simple: to forge his own destiny, discover the truth about his mother, and use his divine strength to protect the weak and preserve peace.

Faris can sometimes be overwhelmed by the competing forces of his dual heritage: the mortal world of his father’s smithing and the divine influence of his mother. However, he is learning to balance these aspects of his identity, and in doing so, he has begun to understand that true strength comes from understanding one’s roots and embracing both light and shadow.

### Abilities:

- **Channel Divinity: Numeda’s Might:** Faris can call upon Numeda’s divine strength, temporarily increasing his physical capabilities to superhuman levels. For 1 minute, he gains advantage on all Strength-based checks and saving throws, and his melee attacks deal additional radiant damage, as though imbued by the goddess herself.
    
- **Rune Knight: Runes of Strength:** Faris has inscribed powerful runes across his body, which enhance his physical prowess. These runes grant him the ability to:
    
    - **Rune of Power:** Gain a bonus to Strength-based attack rolls and damage.
    - **Rune of Endurance:** Gain resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage from non-magical attacks.
    - **Rune of Vitality:** Once per short rest, Faris can regain a portion of hit points equal to his Fighter level after a battle, representing the enduring nature of his divine strength.
- **Shadowmeld (Innate Ability):** Inherited from his mother’s shadow magic, Faris can blend into the shadows, becoming nearly invisible in dim light or darkness for up to 1 minute. During this time, he can move stealthily without leaving a trace, gaining advantage on Stealth checks. He can also use this ability to emerge from the shadows to strike with surprise.
    
- **Divine Resilience:** Faris can tap into Numeda’s divine power to heal his wounds and recover from fatigue. Once per long rest, Faris can use a bonus action to expend a spell slot (if he has any available) to regain hit points equal to 2d10 + his Fighter level.
    
- **Shadowstrike:** Once per day, Faris can imbue his weapon with shadow magic, allowing him to strike with both physical and magical force. For the next minute, his attacks deal additional necrotic damage and can bypass resistance to non-magical attacks. After striking with this ability, he can use his bonus action to return to the shadows, repositioning himself within 30 feet.
    
- **Unyielding Strength:** As a champion of Numeda, Faris has an aura of physical resilience. Allies within 10 feet of him gain a bonus to Strength saving throws, and he can use his reaction to grant advantage on a Strength check or saving throw for an ally within range.
    

---

### Motivation and Goals:

Faris is driven by a sense of duty to Numeda, eager to demonstrate his strength in the service of justice and protection. However, his personal goal is to uncover the truth of his mother’s identity and what divine blood truly flows through his veins. He believes that understanding his mother’s connection to the divine will help him reconcile the conflicting aspects of his heritage and ultimately unlock his full potential.

He seeks to prove that his strength is not merely physical, but a force of will and determination that transcends his origins. As a champion of Numeda, Faris is also committed to protecting those who cannot protect themselves, seeing his role as one of both strength and sacrifice. He hopes to one day reconcile the shadowy and divine aspects of his nature, allowing him to forge his own path forward.

---

### Roleplay Tips:

- Faris is loyal and dependable, but he struggles with feelings of inadequacy due to the mystery surrounding his mother. Play him as someone who is constantly searching for clarity about his origins while trying to focus on his role as Numeda's champion.
- His bond with his father, Kenzo, should be a source of pride, and his interactions with others should reflect the discipline, strength, and resilience he learned from his father’s smithing.
- He may have moments of introspection where he reflects on the powers he inherited from both his father and his mother, grappling with the nature of his identity and the expectations placed upon him.
