### Champion of Zorvia, Goddess of Force

**Name:** **Kaelen Stormhammer**

**Race:** **Goliath**

**Class:** **Fighter (Battle Master archetype)**  
_Kaelen is a mighty warrior, molded by the intense pressure of his deity's power. His physical prowess and unmatched force of will make him a true servant of Zorvia, embodying the raw force and strength that her goddess represents. His mastery in combat allows him to strike with the devastating force of nature itself._

---

### Background and Lore:

Kaelen Stormhammer hails from the high mountain peaks where his tribe, known for their strength and connection to the elemental forces of the earth, dwells. Growing up in a harsh environment, Kaelen learned from a young age that only through strength and resolve could one survive. His people revered the power of nature and the primal forces that shape the world, but Kaelen always felt a strange pull toward something more—something beyond the natural world. This longing led him to an encounter with Zorvia, the goddess of force, who appeared to him in a vision of cosmic power.

Zorvia revealed herself to Kaelen as a force beyond the natural world, a deity that embodies raw power and unyielding will. She is the cosmic force that shapes reality through sheer strength, whether it be the force that causes stars to burn or the power that makes mountains crumble. In this vision, Kaelen was told that his strength was not just for survival, but for enforcing the will of the gods in the material world. He was chosen to be a champion, a conduit of Zorvia's destructive and creative force, able to command the very power that shaped existence.

Now, Kaelen wields his formidable strength as both a weapon and a shield, crushing those who oppose him with the fury of Zorvia’s will. His devotion to the goddess of force drives him to constantly push the limits of his strength, seeking to impose order on chaos and to reshape the world with the unstoppable force of his being.

### Appearance:

Kaelen stands at 7 feet tall, his body a mountain of muscle and sinew. His goliath heritage is evident in his broad, solid frame, and his skin, a grayish stone hue, bears faint tribal tattoos, marking his heritage and his connection to the earth. His eyes are a piercing blue, glowing faintly with the power of the divine force he channels. His long hair, a mix of black and silver, flows down his back, adding to his imposing figure.

He wears armor forged from the bones of ancient creatures, tempered by magic and bound with the power of Zorvia. His armor is heavy, but Kaelen moves with the ease of a seasoned warrior, his every step resounding like a force of nature. At his waist, a massive warhammer, known as "Tempest’s Call," is ever present. The weapon crackles with energy, the hammerhead constantly vibrating with the force of Zorvia's power. When Kaelen swings it, the very air around him seems to tremble.

### Personality:

Kaelen is a being of immense willpower, driven by an unwavering belief in the power of force and strength to shape the world. He is single-minded in his purpose and utterly relentless in his pursuit of his goals. His demeanor is calm and stoic, but beneath his reserved exterior is a fury that can be unleashed in the heat of battle. Kaelen has little patience for weakness, whether in others or in himself. He believes that only through force—whether of the body, will, or spirit—can true change occur.

While he is often seen as intimidating and direct, Kaelen has a deep respect for those who show strength in other forms. He admires individuals who overcome personal adversity or harness their own power in ways that shape the world. However, he has little tolerance for those who seek to manipulate or deceive others. To Kaelen, only the purest forms of power matter—those that can be felt, seen, and understood.

Kaelen’s bond with Zorvia is one of mutual respect. He does not see her as a master who demands obedience, but as a force that guides him to manifest the true potential of strength in the world. To him, Zorvia is not just a goddess, but a cosmic principle of power and order that must be embraced.

### Abilities:

- **Force of the Mountain:** Kaelen can channel the power of Zorvia into his physical strikes, allowing him to increase his strength and deliver devastating blows. Once per short rest, he can use this ability to gain an additional bonus to damage rolls with melee weapons, and his attacks can push enemies back, as though struck by an earthquake. This effect can knock creatures back several feet or make them fall prone if they fail a Strength saving throw.
- **Channel Divinity: Zorvia's Wrath:** Kaelen can use his Channel Divinity to unleash a devastating burst of force in a 30-foot cone in front of him. Creatures within the area must make a Strength saving throw or take thunder damage and be knocked prone. On a successful save, they take half damage and are not knocked prone. This ability reflects Zorvia’s divine power of pure destructive force.
- **Unyielding Will:** Kaelen’s connection to Zorvia gives him incredible resilience. He has advantage on saving throws against being pushed, knocked prone, or restrained. Additionally, once per long rest, Kaelen can use his action to remove one condition that would prevent him from acting (e.g., frightened, paralyzed) as the divine force within him forces his will to overcome any barrier.
- **Battle Master Maneuvers:** As a Battle Master Fighter, Kaelen uses a variety of combat maneuvers that reflect Zorvia's power. He can push enemies back, trip them, and disarm them, channeling the goddess's force into precise, devastating strikes. He also has superiority dice, which he can use to enhance these maneuvers, making them even more potent.
- **Titan’s Stance:** Kaelen’s stature and strength allow him to assume a powerful stance, giving him resistance to damage from creatures smaller than him. When in this stance, he can also use a bonus action to push a creature within 5 feet of him, as though he is a living embodiment of a boulder rolling down a mountain. The creature must make a Strength saving throw or be knocked back 10 feet.
- **Forceful Strikes:** Kaelen’s attacks with his warhammer, Tempest’s Call, deal additional thunder damage, and the weapon creates shockwaves that resonate throughout the battlefield. On a critical hit, his strike causes a shockwave that can knock all creatures within 10 feet of the target back and deal additional thunder damage.
- **Divine Resilience:** Zorvia’s power bolsters Kaelen’s health. He gains temporary hit points whenever he successfully lands a critical hit or uses his Force of the Mountain ability. These temporary hit points reflect the divine protection granted by Zorvia’s unyielding force, making him even more difficult to bring down in combat.

### Motivation and Goals:

Kaelen’s ultimate goal is to embody Zorvia’s will in the material world, using his strength to protect the weak, crush his enemies, and bring order through force. He believes that true power is found in one's ability to shape the world with their might—whether that means defending those who cannot defend themselves or destroying the forces that threaten to disrupt the balance.

He seeks to become a living testament to Zorvia’s ideals, pushing himself to his physical and mental limits, and proving that strength can both create and destroy. Kaelen's journey is one of self-improvement and understanding the true meaning of power. While he may often be found on the frontlines of battle, he is also constantly training and honing his skills, seeking to refine his connection to Zorvia and learn how to wield her divine force with greater precision.

Kaelen also harbors a deep desire to prove that strength is not only a tool for domination but also a force for establishing order, protecting the innocent, and achieving personal growth. He believes that the world is shaped by those who have the strength to shape it, and he strives to be the instrument of that change.

### Roleplay Tips:

- Kaelen's stoic nature can sometimes make him appear as cold and distant, but his loyalty and dedication to Zorvia and those he protects are unwavering.
- When interacting with others, he may question their strength or resolve, offering advice on how to build physical and mental resilience.
- Kaelen has a deep respect for combat and sees it as an art form. He may enjoy teaching others the discipline of martial prowess or sparring with fellow warriors to test their skills.
- Kaelen is driven by a need to prove himself, and this could lead him to take on dangerous challenges or seek out mighty opponents to test his strength.
