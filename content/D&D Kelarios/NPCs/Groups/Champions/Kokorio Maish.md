**Champion of Neglamel, God of Neglect**

**Name:** Kokorio Maish  
**Race:** Green Dragonborn  
**Class:** Warlock (The Undying Patron)

---

**Background and Appearance:**  
Kokorio Maish is a striking green dragonborn with scales that shimmer with an unsettling, acidic hue, a constant reminder of his bond with Neglamel, the god of neglect. His eyes are pale and hollow, glowing faintly with a sickly green light that suggests the forgotten and the forsaken. His horns curve backward along his skull like twisted branches, and his long, whip-like tail is marked by faint scars from past hardships. Kokorio stands tall, but his posture is often hunched as if weighed down by an invisible burden, reflecting his inner turmoil and the neglect he has endured.

He wears simple, tattered robes that speak to his disdain for comfort and material wealth. His clothing is weathered, and his cloak is often draped carelessly over his shoulders. Around his neck, he wears an amulet—a broken and tarnished relic that once was an emblem of a past long forgotten, now serving as a reminder of his bond with Neglamel. His fingers are often stained with ink, as he keeps a journal to record the neglected and forgotten things he encounters.

---

**Personality:**  
Kokorio is a quiet and brooding figure, someone whose life has been marred by neglect and abandonment. He is deeply introspective and often speaks in a detached manner, as though the world and its concerns are distant, irrelevant things. His relationship with Neglamel has taught him that abandonment is a natural part of existence, and he accepts it with an eerie calmness. However, beneath this detached demeanor lies a twisted sense of purpose.

Kokorio is deeply cynical about love, care, and connection, seeing them as fleeting and ultimately meaningless. He believes that neglect is the true force that governs the world—things left unattended inevitably rot, fade, and die. Despite his cold exterior, he is driven by a strange desire to see others suffer the same abandonment that he has felt, often manipulating people into neglecting their responsibilities, relationships, and self-worth.

His emotions are often dormant, though flashes of bitter rage emerge when he perceives something that, in his eyes, should have been neglected but wasn’t. He believes that neglect, in all its forms, is a rightful, karmic punishment for those who think they can escape its reach.

---

**Abilities and Role:**  
As the champion of Neglamel, Kokorio is a vessel for the divine influence of neglect, abandonment, and decay. His powers are tied to the concepts of forsakenness, indifference, and the slow, creeping erosion of all things. As a **Warlock of The Undying**, Kokorio’s abilities focus on manipulation, decay, and the endurance of neglect over time. He draws his power from his pact with Neglamel, the god of neglect, to bring destruction through inattention and to drain the vitality from both things and people.

Some of his notable abilities include:

- **Pact of the Undying**: Kokorio's connection to Neglamel allows him to avoid death, gaining resilience from the neglect of the living and the dead. He is often able to come back from the brink of death, driven by the refusal to be truly forgotten.
    
- **Aura of Indifference**: Kokorio can create a palpable aura of neglect around him, where things and people seem to lose the will to act. People under this aura become lethargic and unmotivated, and objects lose their shine, their upkeep becoming burdensome or even impossible.
    
- **Disruption of Care**: Kokorio can force relationships, systems, and bonds to slowly decay by infusing them with his god’s neglect. Allies may find their trust in each other faltering, their bonds weakening, and their motivations waning. This power can cause friendships and alliances to unravel.
    
- **Decay of Attention**: Kokorio has the ability to make things fall apart simply by withholding attention. Structures may crumble, plants wither, and wounds worsen without care, as if all forms of preservation have been abandoned. He can summon this power to break down materials, and it’s particularly effective against those who refuse to care for their environment.
    
- **Vessel of the Forgotten**: Kokorio can absorb the life force of the neglected, draining the vitality of those who have been abandoned or ignored. This allows him to heal from the suffering of others, turning their pain into his strength.
    
- **Spellcasting (Hexblade-like abilities)**: Kokorio can channel the essence of neglect through his pact magic, casting spells that weaken, corrupt, and erase, rather than build or heal. His most prominent spells focus on causing lingering pain, paralysis from indifference, and inflicting statuses that induce apathy or lethargy.
    

---

**Story and Motivation:**  
Kokorio was born into a world that never wanted him. His parents, both prominent figures in their respective clans, saw him as a mistake, a burden that would never amount to anything. They abandoned him early in his life, leaving him in the care of a distant relative who, too, grew tired of him. Left to wander and fend for himself, Kokorio learned that no one cared, no one remembered him, and no one would ever offer him a second glance.

Eventually, he found himself wandering the wastelands between cities, surviving off scraps and the indifference of the world. There, in the shadow of the forsaken ruins, he met a figure cloaked in darkness—Neglamel, the god of neglect. The god spoke of the inevitability of neglect and abandonment and offered Kokorio a choice: embrace his abandonment and become his champion or live forever as a forgotten soul. Kokorio, who had already come to terms with being neglected, accepted.

Now, as Kokorio Maish, he serves Neglamel, spreading his god’s influence in subtle and disturbing ways. He manipulates situations where people’s desires, hopes, and affections fade away. He watches from the shadows as relationships deteriorate, businesses fail, and kingdoms crumble, all due to the neglect that is inherent in the world.

Kokorio’s primary motivation is to expose the futility of care and responsibility, revealing that all things are destined to be forgotten or ignored in time. He seeks to show people that nothing matters if it is not given proper attention, and everything—eventually—becomes a victim of neglect. While he may manipulate others into abandoning their tasks or their passions, his ultimate goal is not to harm them directly but to make them realize that neglect is not just a personal failing but the true nature of existence.

---

**Motivation and Goals:**

- To spread the influence of Neglamel by sowing apathy, neglect, and abandonment in all aspects of life.
- To manipulate relationships and situations so that they fail due to the absence of care or attention.
- To show people the inevitable nature of neglect and help them come to terms with their own abandonment.
- To challenge the belief in care, love, and responsibility by revealing their ultimate futility in the grand scheme of things.
- To expand Neglamel's influence over all things that are forgotten or forsaken, including people, places, and ideas.

---

**Weapons and Tools:**

- **The Forgotten Blade**: A sword forged from the remains of neglected things—a blade that is tarnished and rusted, yet sharp and deadly. It represents Kokorio’s disdain for beauty and perfection, and when it strikes, it causes a lasting sense of loss in its victims.
    
- **Amulet of the Forsaken**: This twisted, broken amulet is a symbol of Kokorio’s bond with Neglamel. It grants him further control over decay and neglect and allows him to draw power from abandoned places and forsaken creatures.
    
- **Book of the Neglected**: A weathered journal where Kokorio records the slow erosion of the world, from broken promises to forgotten vows. The book can be used to scribe dark rituals or act as a source of magic, especially when Kokorio calls upon the forsaken knowledge contained within it.
    

---

**Legacy and Influence:**  
Kokorio Maish, the champion of Neglamel, leaves behind a legacy of silence, decay, and forgotten dreams. Wherever he goes, he sows seeds of neglect and apathy, turning once-healthy relationships and thriving communities into hollow shells of their former selves. Though he seeks to spread Neglamel's will, his methods are subtle and insidious, weaving neglect into the fabric of reality itself until nothing is left untouched by the goddess’s influence. Through him, Neglamel’s vision of a world forgotten takes shape—one where no one remembers, no one cares, and all things are eventually lost to the void of neglect.
