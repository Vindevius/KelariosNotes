### Champion of Asmodeus, God of Dominion

**Name:** **Varek Valrath**

**Race:** **Tiefling (Variant)**  
_Varek Valrath is a Tiefling, born with the infernal blood of Asmodeus himself. His horns curve slightly upward, his tail is long and whip-like, and his crimson skin bears intricate, arcane runes that glow faintly with the essence of dominion._

**Class:** **Paladin (Oath of Conquest)**  
_Varek is a Paladin dedicated to Asmodeus, sworn to uphold the principles of control, obedience, and subjugation. His Oath of Conquest aligns perfectly with the ideals of his divine patron, as he seeks to impose order, crush resistance, and bend others to his will._

---

### Background and Lore:

Varek Valrath was born into the infernal realms, a child of a mortal who made a desperate pact with Asmodeus. His mother, once a high-ranking noble, traded her soul for power, giving birth to a son who carried the blood of a devil. Growing up amidst the ruthlessness of the Nine Hells, Varek quickly learned the art of manipulation, power, and control. However, it wasn’t just power for power’s sake—Varek was taught the higher calling of Dominion, to rule not only over others, but over his own fate as well.

At a young age, Varek began to rise in prominence among the devils, eventually becoming a favored champion of Asmodeus himself. Recognizing his immense potential, Asmodeus bestowed upon him the divine gift of the **Oath of Conquest**, a path that focuses on overwhelming enemies, claiming dominion over all, and establishing an iron-fisted rule.

### Appearance:

Varek stands tall at 6’5”, his imposing figure enhanced by the regal armor he wears—blackened steel etched with golden sigils that reflect the authority of his lord. His crimson skin and glowing infernal eyes give him an otherworldly presence, while his horns, sharp and slightly curved upward, symbolize his connection to the infernal realm. A long, whip-like tail twitches with authority, and a flowing cloak emblazoned with Asmodeus's symbol—a red infernal pentagram—trails behind him like a banner of dominion.

### Personality:

Varek is cold, calculating, and absolutely unwavering in his devotion to Asmodeus. He sees the world as a place where only the strong should lead, and those who do not bend to authority are weak and deserve to be crushed beneath the weight of his power. His philosophy is simple: to rule is divine, and to serve is to be subjugated. Varek treats loyalty as a weapon—those who serve him will be rewarded, but betrayal is met with swift retribution.

While ruthless, Varek is not without a sense of duty. His devotion to his god is absolute, and he sees his role as Asmodeus's champion as one of both privilege and responsibility. He does not see himself as a tyrant but as a necessary force for imposing order on a chaotic world.

### Abilities:

- **Divine Smite (Conquest):** As a Paladin of the Oath of Conquest, Varek channels the power of Asmodeus to smite foes with blinding, overwhelming force. His Divine Smite inflicts not just radiant damage but also forces the target to feel the crushing weight of divine law, often leaving them stunned or frightened.
- **Aura of Conquest:** Varek exudes an aura that inspires fear and despair in those around him. Enemies within a certain radius of him are filled with dread, unable to fight as effectively in the face of his overwhelming dominance.
- **Scornful Rebuke:** When struck by an enemy, Varek can use his connection to his infernal patron to retaliate with an infernal burst of energy. The enemy is burned by the force of his wrath, suffering damage as their soul is tainted by the dark power of Asmodeus.
- **Channel Divinity – Guided Strike:** With his divine connection, Varek can call upon Asmodeus for strength in battle, making his strikes deadly precise and almost impossible to avoid, ensuring that his enemies feel the full force of his might.
- **Infernal Legacy:** As a Tiefling, Varek has innate access to infernal magic, granting him abilities like **Hellish Rebuke** and **Darkness**. These powers are a reminder of his infernal heritage, allowing him to manipulate shadows and unleash the fires of Hell itself.
- **Unyielding Presence:** Varek’s presence is commanding—enemies hesitate to act against him due to his sheer aura of control. He can assert his dominion over weaker wills, bending them to his will through sheer force of personality and presence.

### Motivation and Goals:

Varek seeks to expand the influence of Asmodeus into the mortal realms, bringing order and control to those who do not recognize the value of dominion. He believes that only under Asmodeus’s rule can true peace be achieved—through total obedience and the elimination of chaos. His ultimate goal is to solidify his position as Asmodeus's right hand, perhaps even becoming the ruler of the Nine Hells one day, should the Lord of the Hells ever fall.
