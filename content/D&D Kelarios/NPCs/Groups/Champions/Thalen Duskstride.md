### Champion of Exsoria, Goddess of Exile

**Name:** **Thalen Duskstride**

**Race:** **Tiefling** (A being cast out from his family and community, marked by his infernal heritage and driven by a deep desire for redemption, but often left to wander in exile)

**Class:** **Ranger (Hunter archetype)**  
_Thalen is a lone wanderer, forced into exile after his community cast him out. He serves Exsoria, the goddess of exile, embracing the solitude and isolation that Exsoria embodies, and seeks to protect those who have been rejected or ostracized by the world._

---

### Background and Lore:

Thalen Duskstride was born into a small, secluded tiefling village in the shadow of a great city. Though his family was bound by love and duty, Thalen's infernal heritage—the result of a distant ancestor's pact with devils—set him apart. As a child, Thalen was a quiet, introspective soul, more at ease in the forests surrounding the village than in the bustling streets of the settlement. Yet, his infernal nature was a constant source of tension within his community, which looked upon him with suspicion, despite his good deeds.

One day, after a terrible incident where Thalen's powers were unintentionally triggered, his village elders cast him out, declaring that he was a danger to the safety of their people. Exiled and alone, Thalen wandered for years, broken by the loss of his family, his friends, and his home. It was during this time that he encountered Exsoria, the goddess of exile, who appeared to him as a mysterious, ever-distant figure, offering him solace and strength.

Exsoria revealed that exile was not always a punishment but could also be a path to self-realization. Thalen, through the goddess's guidance, found solace in the wilderness, embracing the harshness and beauty of isolation. He came to see his exile not as a curse, but as a calling. He became the living embodiment of Exsoria’s will, a protector of those who had been unjustly cast out, and a champion for the forgotten and forsaken.

Now, Thalen roams the world, wandering from place to place, always guided by Exsoria’s quiet whisper. He seeks to shield those who have been exiled, cast aside, or left to fend for themselves, helping them find strength in their solitude and guiding them to safety in a world that often forgets them.

### Appearance:

Thalen stands at 6'1", with a lean, wiry build honed from years of survival in the wilderness. His dark red skin, a clear reminder of his infernal ancestry, contrasts sharply with his wild, unkempt black hair, which falls around his sharp features. His eyes are a piercing amber, glowing faintly in the darkness, giving him an otherworldly presence. A pair of curved horns, black and smooth, curl back from his temples, marking him as a tiefling. His long tail swishes with an almost predatory grace.

He wears a simple cloak of muted browns and greens, made of animal pelts and weathered fabric, perfect for blending into the forests and mountains. A leather strap holds a variety of survival tools—flint, a bedroll, and a small blade—while a longbow made from dark wood is slung across his back. At his waist hangs a dagger forged from obsidian, its dark edge catching the light as he moves. Thalen’s movements are smooth and deliberate, the grace of a predator stalking through the wilds, yet there is an air of sadness in his every step—a reflection of the burden he carries as an eternal wanderer.

### Personality:

Thalen is quiet and reserved, often keeping to himself and avoiding unnecessary social interactions. His exile has shaped him into someone who is comfortable with solitude but deeply compassionate toward those who have been wrongfully cast aside by society. He has a fierce sense of loyalty to those who have been rejected, and he will go to great lengths to protect them, even if it means putting himself in harm's way.

While he is a skilled and ruthless hunter, he also believes in the importance of mercy. He has seen the pain of rejection and isolation firsthand and would never subject anyone to the same fate unless absolutely necessary. Thalen's sense of justice is tempered by a sense of understanding and empathy, knowing that everyone, regardless of their past, has the capacity to change.

His connection to Exsoria has made him pragmatic and patient. He believes that exile offers an opportunity for growth, and while he may mourn the loss of his past life, he has come to accept that his fate is his own. He remains hopeful, even in the darkest of times, and quietly seeks a day when he might finally feel at peace with his own exile.

### Abilities:

- **Exile's Embrace:** Thalen can call upon the power of Exsoria to shield others from harm. This power allows him to create an aura of isolation around a creature, making them harder to track or detect by others. Those within this aura feel an empowering sense of solitude, gaining temporary hit points and resistance to being charmed or frightened.
- **Channel Divinity: Path of the Forsaken:** Thalen can invoke Exsoria’s divine power to either protect a creature in exile or banish an enemy from the mortal plane. When protecting an exiled individual, Thalen can grant them temporary sanctuary, making them untouchable by enemies for a short time. When banishing an enemy, he can use his Channel Divinity to cast a creature away, forcing them into a temporary exile on another plane, making them vulnerable to attacks for a short duration before they return.
- **Hunter’s Mark (Exile's Pursuit):** Thalen’s hunter’s mark takes on a different flavor; when he marks a target, it is not just for tracking purposes. His mark signifies a being that has been cast out or is in some way isolated. The target becomes weaker in the presence of Thalen's divine powers, and Thalen gains the ability to manipulate the environment to ensure that the target is cornered or driven into a trap.
- **Exiled Strikes:** Thalen’s attacks with ranged or melee weapons have a unique edge. His precision and skill are enhanced when dealing with targets who have been outcast or marginalized. He can deal additional damage to those who are wounded, isolated, or on the run, representing the goddess's wrath against those who turn their back on others.
- **Mark of the Forgotten:** Thalen can place an ethereal mark upon an individual, symbolizing their exile from the natural order or society. This mark causes the individual to become more vulnerable to Thalen's attacks, as he channels Exsoria’s power to expose the isolation and rejection they suffer. It also allows him to track the marked creature across distances, no matter where they may try to hide.
- **Ranger’s Bond of the Outcast:** Thalen has a deep bond with creatures that dwell in isolation or have been outcast from their communities. He is particularly adept at understanding their nature and can communicate with them in a rudimentary form. He also gains proficiency in survival checks related to the wilderness, aiding him in finding those in need or identifying others who have suffered from exile.
- **Pathfinder’s Resilience:** Exile has forged Thalen into an unbreakable figure. He has resistance to exhaustion and can go for long periods without food or rest. His connection to the wilderness makes him immune to many diseases and curses, symbolizing his ability to endure, no matter the circumstances of his exile.

### Motivation and Goals:

Thalen's primary motivation is to ensure that those who have been unjustly exiled or rejected are protected, guided, and shown that they are not alone. He seeks to right the wrongs of the world by helping the outcasts, the forsaken, and the cast-asides find peace, safety, and redemption. Through his service to Exsoria, Thalen aims to bring balance to a world that often casts people out without second thought, believing that exile can be a pathway to growth and strength rather than punishment.

Though he will never truly escape his own exile, Thalen's ultimate goal is to find peace within himself and to protect those who cannot protect themselves, all the while carrying the weight of Exsoria’s will with a quiet strength. He hopes that one day, his service will bring him the closure he seeks, or perhaps even the end of his eternal wandering.
