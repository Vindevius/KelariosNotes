### **The Silver Pact of Shadows**

By accepting this coin, you pledge yourself to Diana, Mistress of Shadows, and align your fate with her will and the honor of the Shadows. This agreement binds you to her divine purpose and grants you the blessings of her shadowed power.

---

### **The Coin of Shadows**

1. **Design**:
    - **The Wolf**: Symbolizing loyalty, instinct, and the unbreakable bond of the pack.
    - **The Circle with a Line**: The mark of the Shadows, representing the duality of chaos and order within shadow’s embrace.
2. **Medium of Bond**:
    - Possession of the coin signifies your acceptance of Diana’s authority.
    - Speaking her name while holding the coin invokes her presence.

---

### **Terms of the Pact**

#### **I. Boons Granted**

As a Shadow, you are gifted Diana’s blessings:
- **Stage 1: Shadow’s Boon (lvl 3)**
	- **Effect**: You gain the following blessings from Diana.
		- **Shadow’s Grace**: The ability to meld with shadows, becoming unseen or intangible within their depths.
		- **Loyalty of the Wolf**: Wolves and shadow-aligned creatures recognize you as kin and offer their aid in times of need.
		- **Protection of the Pack**: You are granted safe haven in any Shadow-run tavern or bastion.
- **Stage 2: Shadow’s Warning (lvl 6)**
	- **Effect**: Your shadow becomes a sentinel, attuned to the presence of danger.
	    - When a threat approaches, your shadow moves unnaturally to warn you, granting **advantage on Perception checks** to detect hidden dangers, traps, or ambushes.
	    - This boon emphasizes heightened awareness in low-light or shadowed environments.
- **Stage 3: Shadow’s Scout (lvl 10)**
	- **Effect**: You gain the ability to detach your shadow and send it scouting ahead.
	    - **Duration**: Up to 1 hour, or until you dismiss it.
	    - While detached, your shadow appears as if you are without one and can:
	        - **Scout**: Move up to 60 feet per round (with a range of 1 mile).
	        - **Perceive**: Detect enemies, objects, or environmental features using your Perception skill.
	        - **Interact**: Look for hidden mechanisms or track a creature’s trail.
	        - **Communicate**: Offer advice in Diana’s voice or deliver sarcastic quips that reflect your subconscious.
    - **Limit**: Your shadow cannot interact with physical objects or attack, but it can pass through barriers and convey visual information.
- **Stage 4: Shadow’s Absence (lvl 14)**
	- **Effect**: At the apex of your connection, your shadow becomes a versatile companion that can seamlessly blend into your environment.
	    - **Camouflage**: While your shadow is detached or active, it grants you **advantage on Stealth checks**, as your lack of a shadow makes you harder to detect.
	    - **Guardian**: If an enemy attacks while your shadow is scouting, it instinctively returns to defend you, imposing **disadvantage** on one attack per round.
	    - **Awakened Intelligence**: Your shadow becomes fully sentient, capable of engaging in complex strategies or commentary, reflecting your thoughts and personality.

#### **II. Duties and Expectations**

- **Code of Honor**: You must uphold the values of the Shadows: care for the forgotten, protect the oppressed, and maintain loyalty to the pack.
- **Service to the Mistress**: Carry out missions and tasks in Diana’s name, whether they involve aiding the orphaned, toppling tyrants, or defending the Shadowfell’s sanctity.
- **Tavern Demiplane Access**: A copper coin must be offered to use the demiplane connecting Shadow-run taverns unless on a mission or in an emergency. Even during missions, this offering is expected.
- **Offerings**: Leave offerings for orphans to honor Diana and gain favor.

#### **III. Invocation and Guidance**

- To seek Diana’s counsel, place the coin in your palm and whisper, “Diana, Mistress of Shadows, guide me.” Your shadow will awaken, becoming an entity through which Diana communicates.
- Offerings left in Shadow-run taverns attract black wolves, known as the **Spawn of the Shadow Wolf**, symbolizing Diana’s favor. These wolves serve as silent watchers and guardians.

#### **IV. Duration and Forfeiture**

- This pact remains active until:
    - You voluntarily return the coin to the Shadow Council and they accept it.
    - Diana herself releases you.
    - You betray the Shadows, causing the coin to melt and mark you for death.

#### **V. Penalty for Betrayal**

- **Shadow’s Bane**: Betrayal severs all boons and curses the betrayer, rendering them visible and vulnerable in all shadows.
- **Wolves’ Hunt**: The betrayer is hunted by wolves, led by the Spawn of the Shadow Wolf. If wolves are nearby when the betrayal occurs, they will howl and begin the hunt immediately.
- **Possible Curses Before Betrayal**:
	- Shadows consistently trip you or move things slightly off where they were before.
	- Disadvantage on all Dexterity Checks and Saving Throws
	- Umbraki randomly appear when the person is alone and get advantage on attacks since the person is marked with a black dot on their hand and will be hunted down till they gain the goddesses forgiveness.

### **The Shadow Council’s Seal**

This coin binds you to the Mistress of Shadows and her divine will. Once activated, the coin’s wolf engraving will glow silver when you are in proximity to fellow Shadows, signifying your place in the pack.

By holding this coin, you pledge to serve the Shadows, honor Diana, and uphold the code of the pack. May the Mistress guide your path in darkness.
