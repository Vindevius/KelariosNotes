
### Diana, the Mistress of Shadows

## The Shadow Council
- Voice of the Shadows: The Shadow (Avatar of Diana/Derek)
	- Delivering Diana's decrees and guiding the council.
- High Shadowborn: Zanryn Helevaris (Half-Elf Assassin)
	- Enforces loyalty and discipline, oversees the Shadowborn organization.
- Keeper of Secrets: Asterin Thorn (Wood Elf Druid of the Moon)
	- Manages intelligence, demiplane operations, and covert information networks.
- Master of Arms: Atlas the Mighty (High Elf Barbarian)
	- Oversees training, armament production, and bastion defenses.
- Tavern Matron: Zia Moonset (Avatar of Diana)
	- Symbol of family and loyalty, fostering morale and caring for orphans.

## The Shadow Lords
- Staderia: **Helah Scar** _(Halfling Druid)_:
	- Renowned for her cunning and ability to manipulate natural shadows.
- Wraithmoor: **Reese Rock** _(Red Dragonborn Rogue)_:
	- Operates with ruthless efficiency, specializing in high-stakes espionage.
- Western City States: **Xander Black** _(Drow Tavern Enforcer)_:
	- Keeps order in Shadow-run taverns and bastions with a firm hand.
- Valsia: **Nogna Afarir** _(Gnome Warlock)_:
	- Master of arcane secrets and leader in magical strategies.
- Alvoria: **Veyla Nightthorn** _(Dwarf Cleric of the Shadows)_:
	- Healer and spiritual guide for her region.
- Wuryn Kingdom: **Amara Vesperine Rothwood** _(Shadow Elf Rogue/Cleric)_:
	- Balances faith in Diana with surgical precision on missions.
- Archaios: **Jarek Flintfoot** _(Dwarf Artificer)_:
	- Operates a hub for Shadow technology and magical innovations.
- Archia: **Syra Hollowstep** _(Human Bard)_:
	- Specializes in espionage and manipulation through music and charm.
- Kyria: **Edith "Eddy" Drakemantle** _(Bronze Dragonborn Paladin)_:
	- Combines loyalty and strength to protect the weak. Also a member of the Council of Stars.
- Veteria: **Rathos Embermane** _(Fire Genasi Fighter)_:
	- Commanding and tactical, known for leading revolts.
- Xeloria Sea: **Klimwir Galba** _(Gnome Monk)_:
	- An elusive martial artist and philosopher of the Shadows’ code.
- Snobira: **Fenril Duskwind** _(Aarakocra Monk)_:
	- Controls mountainous regions and aerial operations.
- Eastern Shadar: **Liora Quicksilver** _(Halfling Rogue/Swashbuckler)_:
	- Leads operations in a bustling port city.
- Western Shadar: **Lyric Veylore** _(Elf Rogue/Enchanter)_:
	- An expert in enchanted disguises and infiltration.
- Kayos: **Ceylin Whisperwind** _(Half-Elf Wizard)_:
	- Master of teleportation and planar magic.
- Diadera: **Brelon Shadeclaw** _(Tabaxi Ranger)_:
	- Expert in wilderness survival and infiltration.
- Kraken Shores: **Mara Duskveil** _(Tiefling Sorcerer)_:
	- Oversees a coastal city and specializes in smuggling operations. Directly reports to Eris (Avatar of Diana).
- Western Uzeron: **Selric Frostshade** _(White Dragonborn Fighter)_:
	- Leader in colder, remote territories.
- Eastern Uzeron: **Tara Starveil** _(Half-Elf Druid)_:
	- Protects wilderness bastions and guides Shadow Pups.
- South Zomera: **Quorin Talonshade** _(Human Paladin)_:
	- Maintains order in a tumultuous border region.
- North Zomera: **Tyvar Darkstrider** _(Half-Orc Rogue)_:
	- Known for brutal efficiency and unwavering loyalty.
