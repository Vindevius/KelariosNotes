### **1. Leadership Tier: The Apex of the Shadows**

#### **The Mistress of Shadows (Diana)**

- **Role**: Supreme leader and divine guide of the Shadows, overseeing all operations and upholding their values.
- **Power**: Final authority on major decisions, revolutions, and member appointments.

#### **The Shadow Council**

- **Role**: The ruling body beneath Diana, responsible for high-level decisions and the organization’s direction.
- **Members**:
    - **The Shadow (Avatar of Diana)**: Voice of the Shadows, delivering Diana's decrees and guiding the council.
    - **Zanryn Helevaris** _(High Shadowborn, Half-Elf Assassin)_: Enforces loyalty and discipline, oversees the Shadowborn organization.
    - **Asterin Thorn** _(Keeper of Secrets, Wood Elf Druid)_: Manages intelligence, demiplane operations, and covert information networks.
    - **Atlas the Mighty** _(Master of Arms, High Elf Barbarian)_: Oversees training, armament production, and bastion defenses.
    - **Zia Moonset** _(Tavern Matron, Avatar of Diana)_: Symbol of family and loyalty, fostering morale and caring for orphans.

### **3. Operative Tier: The Shadow Pack**

#### **Elite Shadows**

- **Alastrina the Silent** _(Leader of the Enforcers)_: Stealthy, efficient, and deadly. Alastrina commands the enforcers who protect taverns and execute Diana’s justice.

#### **The Shadowborn**

- **Role**: An elite unit of assassins and paladins of vengeance reporting to the High Shadowborn.
- **Description**:
    - Dressed in unique black studded leather and cloaks that snuff out nearby light, their faces hidden in shadows.
    - Known for unparalleled stealth and lethal precision.
    - Accompany wolves to hunt down betrayers, ensuring justice is swift and final.

#### **Standard Operatives**

- **Ranks**:
    - **Shadow Fang**: Senior operatives with high levels of field expertise.
    - **Shadow Claw**: Mid-level operatives completing standard missions.
    - **Shadow Pup (Trainee)**: Junior operatives in advanced training.

### **5. Youth Tier: The Shadow Pups**

- **Role**: Orphans raised in Shadow-run keeps and orphanages.
- **Training**:
    - Childhood: Education, basic survival skills, and moral lessons.
    - Adolescence: Advanced training in stealth, combat, and Shadow philosophy.
- **Unique Features**:
    - Wolves (often called “puppies”) are a constant presence, fostering bonds between children and Diana’s sacred creatures.
- Given the choice to formally join the Shadows upon adulthood at the age of 16.
