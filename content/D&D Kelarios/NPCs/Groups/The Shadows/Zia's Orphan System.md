### **Zia Moonset’s System for Raising Orphans**

#### **1. The Shadow Haven Network**

- **Orphanages and Keeps:** The Shadows maintain a network of orphanages and fortified keeps around the world. Each is designed to provide safety, warmth, and education, often disguised as ordinary establishments to avoid suspicion. They are located near Shadow-run Taverns.
- **Taverns as Transition Hubs:** Taverns like the Wolves’ Den serve as intermediary locations where orphans can be cared for temporarily before being assigned to a Shadow Haven.
- **Guardianship Council:** Each Shadow Haven is led by a team of carefully chosen Shadows with a strong maternal or paternal nature, modeled after Zia's own protective instincts. They are led by a cleric.

---

#### **2. The Pack Philosophy**

- **Family Values:** Orphans are raised as part of a "pack," fostering a sense of belonging. They are taught to view fellow Shadows as siblings and Zia as a guiding mother figure.
- **Wolves as Companions:** Wolves are integral to the orphanages, serving as protectors, companions, and teachers. Young children often call them “puppies,” creating a bond of trust and safety.
- **Code of Loyalty:** From a young age, children learn the core tenets of the Shadows—family, loyalty, protection of the weak, and honoring commitments.

---

#### **3. Education and Training**

- **Moral Education:** Storytelling, songs, and rituals are used to teach the values of the Shadows. Zia ensures these lessons are imbued with love and understanding.
- **Life Skills:** Children are trained in practical skills like cooking, crafting, and self-defense to prepare them for life, whether they join the Shadows or not.
- **Combat and Stealth Training:** Starting in adolescence, those who show interest and aptitude are trained in the ways of the Shadows—stealth, reconnaissance, and ethical combat.
- **Cultural Diversity:** Since the Shadows operate globally, children are taught languages, customs, and cultural practices to instill respect for all people.

---

#### **4. Personalized Care**

- **Assigned Mentors:** Each child is paired with a Shadow mentor who acts as a surrogate parent, ensuring every child receives individual attention.
	- Alastrina the Silent is [[Jude Moonset]]'s mentor.
- **Emotional Support:** Zia emphasizes the importance of mental and emotional well-being. Shadows skilled in healing and counseling are assigned to the orphanages.
- **Adoption Within the Shadows:** Orphans are often “adopted” by Shadows who want to build a family, ensuring every child feels wanted and loved. Retired Shadows are more likely to adopt.

---

#### **5. Shadow Wolves’ Protection**

- **Wolves as Guardians:** Every Shadow Haven is watched over by a pack of wolves, which serve as both protectors and companions. Wolves are particularly attuned to the children, becoming their playmates and defenders.
- **Sacred Bond:** Children learn to treat wolves as sacred beings and allies, deepening their connection to Diana and her teachings.

---

#### **6. Transition to Shadowhood**

- **Voluntary Membership:** As the children grow, they are given the choice to join the Shadows. Those who choose other paths are supported with resources and guidance.
- **Rites of Passage:** For those who join, a coming-of-age ceremony is held where they receive a silver coin and pledge themselves to Diana.

---

#### **7. Zia’s Role**

- **Maternal Oversight:** Zia personally visits Shadow Havens, ensuring their standards reflect her love and care.
- **Festivals and Gatherings:** She organizes events where all the orphans and Shadows can come together, celebrating their bond as one family.
- **Intervention and Protection:** Zia will fiercely defend her "children" from any threats, often calling on the full power of the Shadows to protect them.
