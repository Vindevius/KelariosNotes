### **Zarvok the Stormwarden**

_Blue Dragonborn, Captain of the Guard / Protector of the Guild, CR 15_

**Armor Class** 20 (Dragonborn Armor + Shield)  
**Hit Points** 210 (20d10 + 100)  
**Speed** 40 ft.

**STR** 22 (+6)  
**DEX** 16 (+3)  
**CON** 20 (+5)  
**INT** 14 (+2)  
**WIS** 18 (+4)  
**CHA** 16 (+3)

**Saving Throws** Str +11, Dex +8, Con +10, Wis +9, Cha +8  
**Skills** Athletics +11, Intimidation +8, Perception +9, Survival +9  
**Damage Resistances** Lightning, Thunder  
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 19  
**Languages** Common, Draconic, Celestial

**Challenge** 15 (13,000 XP)

---

**TRAITS**

- **Storm's Fury:** Zarvok can channel the energy of storms into his attacks. When he hits a creature with his _Thunderstrike Halberd_, he can deal an additional 14 (4d6) lightning damage.
- **Lightning Absorption:** Zarvok has resistance to lightning damage. When he takes lightning damage, he regains hit points equal to half the damage taken (rounded down).
- **Thunderous Roar (Recharge 5-6):** As a bonus action, Zarvok can unleash a thunderous roar that echoes in a 30-foot radius around him. All creatures within range must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or take 18 (4d8) thunder damage and be deafened for 1 minute. On a successful save, the creature takes half damage and isn’t deafened.
- **Electric Aura:** Zarvok radiates an aura of crackling energy. Any creature that starts its turn within 10 feet of Zarvok takes 10 (3d6) lightning damage.

---

**ACTIONS**

- **Multiattack:** Zarvok makes three attacks: one with his _Thunderstrike Halberd_ and two with his claws.
- **Thunderstrike Halberd (Melee Weapon Attack):** +11 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 19 (2d10 + 6) slashing damage plus 14 (4d6) lightning damage. On a critical hit, the target must make a DC 19 Constitution saving throw or be stunned until the end of Zarvok’s next turn.
- **Claws (Melee Weapon Attack):** +11 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 12 (2d6 + 6) slashing damage.
- **Thunderstrike Wave (Recharge 5-6):** Zarvok slams his halberd into the ground, sending a shockwave of thunder and lightning. All creatures within a 30-foot cone must make a DC 19 Constitution saving throw or take 28 (8d6) thunder damage and 28 (8d6) lightning damage, or half as much on a successful save.
- **Guardian's Shield (Recharge 6):** Zarvok creates a magical shield around himself and one ally within 30 feet, granting both +2 to AC for 1 minute. This ability also grants both creatures immunity to lightning and thunder damage for the duration.

---

**BONUS ACTIONS**

- **Protective Stance:** As a bonus action, Zarvok can choose an ally within 30 feet of him. The ally gains resistance to all damage until the start of Zarvok’s next turn.
- **Electrified Strike:** Zarvok can choose to supercharge his next melee attack with lightning. The attack deals an additional 14 (4d6) lightning damage and forces the target to make a DC 19 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the target is knocked prone.

---

**REACTIONS**

- **Lightning Riposte (Recharge 5-6):** When Zarvok is hit by a melee attack, he can use his reaction to strike back with a blast of lightning. The attacker takes 20 (6d6) lightning damage, and the creature must make a DC 19 Dexterity saving throw or be stunned until the end of Zarvok's next turn.
- **Guardian’s Vow (1/Day):** When an ally within 30 feet of Zarvok is hit by an attack, Zarvok can use his reaction to intercept the blow, redirecting the damage to himself. Zarvok takes the damage instead of the ally, and the attack does not affect the original target.

---

**LEGENDARY ACTIONS**  
Zarvok can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Zarvok regains spent legendary actions at the start of his turn.

- **Thunderstrike Slam (Costs 2 Actions):** Zarvok makes a melee attack with his _Thunderstrike Halberd_. On a hit, the target also takes 10 (3d6) thunder damage and must make a DC 19 Strength saving throw or be knocked back 20 feet and knocked prone.
- **Lightning Lash (Costs 2 Actions):** Zarvok releases a bolt of lightning at a creature he can see within 60 feet. The creature must make a DC 19 Dexterity saving throw or take 16 (4d6) lightning damage, or half as much on a successful save.
- **Commanding Roar (Costs 3 Actions):** Zarvok lets out a booming roar that commands his allies. All allies within 60 feet of Zarvok can immediately take one weapon attack as a reaction.

---

**SPELLCASTING**  
Zarvok has innate draconic magic. He can cast the following spells, requiring no material components:

- **At will:** _Detect Magic, Thunderwave_
- **1/day each:** _Lightning Bolt, Call Lightning, Counterspell_

---

**Description:**  
Zarvok the Stormwarden is a stalwart protector of Xarathian, wielding both his formidable physical prowess and draconic magic to shield his sovereign from any who would dare to defy him. Clad in lightning-etched armor and wielding a halberd that crackles with storm energy, Zarvok is a terrifying force on the battlefield. His unwavering loyalty to Xarathian is matched only by his merciless dedication to those who would betray the guild. As the Captain of the Guard, Zarvok serves as both a commander and an impenetrable wall, ensuring that any threat to his leader is quickly extinguished.
