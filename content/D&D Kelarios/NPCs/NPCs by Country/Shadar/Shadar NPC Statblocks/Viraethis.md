### **Viraethis the Stormcaller**

_Aarakocra, High Advisor / Weather Manipulator, CR 10_

**Armor Class** 17 (Ceremonial Robes)  
**Hit Points** 112 (15d8 + 45)  
**Speed** 30 ft., fly 60 ft.

**STR** 10 (+0)  
**DEX** 18 (+4)  
**CON** 16 (+3)  
**INT** 20 (+5)  
**WIS** 18 (+4)  
**CHA** 16 (+3)

**Saving Throws** Dex +8, Wis +8, Cha +7  
**Skills** Insight +8, Nature +9, Persuasion +7, Religion +9  
**Damage Resistances** Lightning  
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14  
**Languages** Common, Draconic, Aarakocra

**Challenge** 10 (5,900 XP)

---

**TRAITS**

- **Aarakocra Flight:** Viraethis has a flying speed of 60 feet. She can fly in difficult terrain, including within storm clouds.
- **Stormcaller’s Gift:** Viraethis can manipulate the weather in a 1-mile radius around her. While near Xarathian (within 100 feet), her control over the storm is enhanced. She can use the following abilities:
    - **Summon Storm (Recharge 5-6):** Viraethis can call forth a storm in a 60-foot radius centered on her. The storm lasts for 10 minutes, and any creature within the storm’s area must succeed on a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw or take 35 (10d6) lightning damage, or half as much on a successful save.
    - **Calm Storm (1/Day):** As an action, Viraethis can use her power to calm a storm within a 5-mile radius. This instantly ends any weather-based effects, including magical ones, within the radius.
    - **Lightning Rod (Recharge 5-6):** Viraethis can channel a bolt of lightning into a single creature within 120 feet. The target must make a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw or take 27 (6d8) lightning damage, or half as much on a successful save.
- **Harbinger of the Winds:** Viraethis has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks related to weather patterns, wind, and air currents.
- **Wind Spirit (1/Short Rest):** Viraethis can invoke the aid of a wind spirit. As a bonus action, she can summon the wind spirit, granting her a flying speed of 90 feet for 1 minute. She also has advantage on all saving throws against being grappled or restrained while the spirit is present.

---

**ACTIONS**

- **Multiattack:** Viraethis makes two attacks with her Arcane Lightning Staff.
- **Arcane Lightning Staff (Melee Weapon Attack):** +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 12 (2d6 + 4) lightning damage.
- **Thunderstrike (Recharge 5-6):** Viraethis channels a powerful thunderstorm through her staff. All creatures within 20 feet of her must make a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or take 28 (8d6) thunder damage, or half as much on a successful save.
- **Lightning Cascade (Recharge 6):** Viraethis releases a chain of lightning that arcs to 3 different creatures within 60 feet of her. Each target must make a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw or take 18 (4d8) lightning damage, or half as much on a successful save.
- **Summon Tempest (1/Day):** Viraethis calls forth a massive storm in a 100-foot radius around her. The storm lasts for 1 minute. All creatures in the area must make a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw or take 56 (16d6) lightning and thunder damage, or half as much on a successful save. The storm reduces visibility in the area to 10 feet, and creatures who fail their saving throws are also deafened for 1 minute.
- **Gale Burst (1/Short Rest):** Viraethis summons a blast of wind that pushes enemies away. Each creature within a 15-foot cone must make a DC 17 Strength saving throw or be pushed 20 feet away from her and take 14 (4d6) bludgeoning damage. A creature that succeeds on the saving throw takes half damage and isn't pushed.

---

**BONUS ACTIONS**

- **Wind Step:** As a bonus action, Viraethis can move up to 30 feet without provoking opportunity attacks. This movement must be in a straight line and can pass through difficult terrain.

---

**REACTIONS**

- **Stormshield (1/Day):** When Viraethis or an ally within 30 feet of her is hit by a melee or ranged attack, she can use her reaction to create a barrier of wind and lightning, reducing the damage by 22 (5d8) and dealing 10 (3d6) lightning damage to the attacker.

---

**LEGENDARY ACTIONS**  
Viraethis can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Viraethis regains spent legendary actions at the start of her turn.

- **Call of the Storm (Costs 2 Actions):** Viraethis summons a bolt of lightning from the sky to strike a creature within 120 feet. The target must make a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw or take 15 (3d6) lightning damage, or half as much on a successful save.
- **Wind Slash (Costs 1 Action):** Viraethis makes a melee attack with her Arcane Lightning Staff. On a hit, the target takes an additional 7 (2d6) thunder damage and must succeed on a DC 16 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.
- **Eye of the Storm (Costs 3 Actions):** Viraethis invokes the calm center of the storm, granting herself advantage on all attack rolls, ability checks, and saving throws for 1 round. In addition, creatures within 10 feet of her have disadvantage on attack rolls against her.

---

**Description:**  
Viraethis the Stormcaller is a mystical and revered figure in Xarathian’s guild, not only for her ability to manipulate the weather but also for her profound connection to the forces of nature. Her ethereal blue and silver feathers shimmer with magic, and her presence radiates wisdom and calm. As one of Xarathian’s most trusted advisors, she guides his decisions with a deep understanding of the storm and its will. Her devotion to the Blue Greatwyrm is unwavering, believing that Xarathian heralds a new era of dominion over the skies and the elements. With her power to summon and control the tempest, Viraethis ensures that no enemy can withstand the fury of the storm or the will of Xarathian.
