### Xarathian the Sovereign

_Gargantuan Dragon (Chromatic, Blue), Chaotic Evil_

---

**Armor Class:** 23 (natural armor)  
**Initiative:** +2  
**Hit Points:** 533 (26d20 + 260)  
**Speed:** 60 ft., Burrow 60 ft., Fly 120 ft., Swim 60 ft.

---

**STR** 30 (+10)  
**DEX** 14 (+2)  
**CON** 30 (+10)  
**INT** 21 (+5)  
**WIS** 20 (+5)  
**CHA** 26 (+8)

---

**Saving Throws:** CON +18, WIS +13, CHA +16
**Skills:** _Intimidation_ +16, _Perception_ +21, _Stealth_ +10
**Damage Immunities:** Lightning; Charmed, Frightened, Poisoned
**Senses:** Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 31
**Languages:** Common, Draconic
**Challenge Rating:** 27 (XP 105,000 or Mythic Encounter; PB +8)

---

### **Traits**

**Chromatic Awakening (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).**  
If Xarathian is reduced to 0 hit points, his current hit point total instead resets to 425 hit points. He recharges his Breath Weapon and regains any expended uses of Legendary Resistance. For 1 hour after this, Xarathian can use his Mythic Actions, and all damage from his Breath Weapon is enhanced. Award an additional 105,000 XP (210,000 XP total) for defeating Xarathian after his Chromatic Awakening.

**Legendary Resistance (4/Day).**  
If Xarathian fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.

**Unusual Nature.**  
Xarathian doesn't require food or drink, but he hoards vast quantities of diamonds, which he uses for resurrections and rewarding loyalty.

**Hoarding Diamonds (Passive).**  
Xarathian’s hoard includes vast amounts of diamonds that he uses for his personal power and to reward loyal followers. When someone shows him great loyalty, he grants them magical items crafted from his hoard.

---

### **Actions**

**Multiattack.**  
Xarathian makes three attacks: one _Bite_, one _Claw_, and one _Tail_ attack.

**Bite.**  
_Melee Weapon Attack:_ +18 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target.  
_Hit:_ 28 (3d10 + 10) piercing damage plus 15 (3d12) force damage.

**Claw.**  
_Melee Weapon Attack:_ +18 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target.  
_Hit:_ 26 (3d8 + 10) slashing damage.  
If the target is a Huge or smaller creature, it is grappled (escape DC 22) and restrained until this grapple ends. Xarathian can only grapple one creature at a time.

**Tail.**  
_Melee Weapon Attack:_ +18 to hit, reach 20 ft., one target.  
_Hit:_ 26 (3d8 + 10) bludgeoning damage.  
If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 26 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

**Breath Weapon (Recharge 5–6).**  
Xarathian exhales a 300-foot cone of lightning energy. Each creature in the area must make a DC 26 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 78 (12d12) lightning damage, or half as much on a successful save.

**Thunderous Shockwave (Recharge 5-6).**  
As a bonus action, Xarathian channels lightning through his maw, creating a shockwave in a 60-foot radius. Each creature in the area must make a DC 26 Constitution saving throw or take 35 (6d10) thunder damage and be pushed back 15 feet. Creatures that succeed on their saving throw take half damage and are not pushed back.

---

### **Legendary Actions**

Xarathian can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature’s turn. Xarathian regains spent legendary actions at the start of his turn.
**Claw Attack.**  
Xarathian makes one _Claw_ attack.
**Tail Sweep (Costs 2 Actions).**  
Xarathian sweeps his tail in a 15-foot radius around him. Each creature in the area must succeed on a DC 26 Dexterity saving throw or take 26 (3d8 + 10) bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone.
**Arcane Spear (Costs 3 Actions).**  
Xarathian creates four spears of magical force. Each spear targets a creature of Xarathian’s choice within 120 feet, dealing 12 (1d8 + 8) force damage to the target.

---

### **Mythic Actions**

If Xarathian's Chromatic Awakening has activated in the last hour, he can use the following Mythic Actions as part of his legendary actions.
**Bite.**  
Xarathian makes one _Bite_ attack.
**Chromatic Flare (Costs 2 Actions).**  
Xarathian flares with elemental energy, causing each creature within a 60-foot radius sphere centered on him to succeed on a DC 26 Dexterity saving throw or take 22 (5d8) lightning damage.

---

### **Lair Actions**

On initiative count 20 (losing initiative ties), Xarathian can take a lair action to cause one of the following effects. He can’t use the same effect two rounds in a row.
1. **Lightning Surge.**  
    A bolt of lightning strikes the ground within 120 feet of Xarathian. Each creature within 10 feet of the point where the lightning strikes must make a DC 26 Dexterity saving throw or take 30 (5d10) lightning damage, or half as much on a successful save.
2. **Diamond Wall.**  
    A wall of diamonds, 30 feet long, 20 feet high, and 5 feet thick, forms within 120 feet of Xarathian. The wall is impervious to damage, and creatures cannot pass through it for 1 minute.
3. **Command Loyalty.**  
    Xarathian can choose one creature within 60 feet that can hear him. That creature must succeed on a DC 26 Wisdom saving throw or be charmed by Xarathian for 1 hour. The creature will fight for him, remaining under his sway unless harmed by Xarathian’s allies.

---

### **Legendary Rewards**

Xarathian rewards loyalty with powerful magic items. His most trusted followers might receive unique artifacts that augment their powers, often crafted from his diamond hoard or imbued with his storm-touched energy.

### **Description**

Xarathian the Sovereign is an awe-inspiring, gargantuan dragon with a commanding presence that reflects his title as the leader of the Guild of the Sovereign. His scales shimmer with a dark metallic sheen, a mix of deep sapphire and obsidian hues, with jagged edges that give him a menacing, regal appearance. His eyes gleam like precious diamonds—cold and calculating, yet betraying a hint of deep wisdom and ancient power.

Xarathian's immense wings are regal, stretching far beyond what most mortals can comprehend. They resemble the wings of an ancient wyvern, with an iridescent glow that crackles with electricity, hinting at the vast arcane power he wields. His massive, coiled tail drags behind him, leaving deep impressions in the ground, and it seems capable of snapping with terrifying force.

As the ruler of his guild, Xarathian’s personality is as sharp and unyielding as his form. He is a master tactician, preferring to manipulate events from the shadows and rewarding loyalty with immense power, often bestowing his followers with powerful artifacts or magical items in exchange for service. However, disobedience is met with swift and merciless punishment—Xarathian does not tolerate failure. His hoard consists not only of wealth and artifacts but of diamonds—precious gems that he keeps for one singular purpose: resurrection. He believes that only those who have proven their worth should be allowed to live, and he hoards these gems in case he needs to bring back loyal members of his guild or those he deems necessary for his schemes.

Xarathian is fiercely intelligent, with a sharp mind capable of weaving complex plans and setting intricate traps for both enemies and allies alike. He holds grudges deeply, and those who cross him find themselves facing not only his wrath but the full might of his guild and resources. While he can be charming in his dealings with those he respects, his cold demeanor and calculating nature can make him seem distant and intimidating. He speaks with authority, his voice like a deep rumble that echoes with the weight of centuries of draconic power.

With an overwhelming sense of self-worth, Xarathian considers himself the true sovereign of his domain, and those who fail to recognize his supremacy are quickly crushed under his clawed feet. The Sovereign is not just a leader; he is a force of nature, a being whose influence can stretch to every corner of the world.

In combat, Xarathian is a nearly unstoppable force. His breath weapon—an explosive torrent of destructive energy—can obliterate entire battlefields, and his vast magical abilities further enhance his potency. With his immense size, superior strength, and tactical brilliance, Xarathian is a foe that strikes fear into all who hear his name. Only those with deep resolve and unyielding loyalty can stand a chance of earning his favor—or escaping his wrath.
