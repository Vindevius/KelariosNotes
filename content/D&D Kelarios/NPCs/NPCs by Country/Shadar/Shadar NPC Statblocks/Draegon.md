### **Draegon the Wyrmblade**

_Human (Draconic Heritage), Guild Champion / Duelist, CR 11_

**Armor Class** 19 (Dragonbone Armor, Shield of the Wyrmblade)  
**Hit Points** 150 (20d8 + 60)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

**STR** 20 (+5)  
**DEX** 16 (+3)  
**CON** 18 (+4)  
**INT** 12 (+1)  
**WIS** 14 (+2)  
**CHA** 16 (+3)

**Saving Throws** Str +9, Con +8, Cha +7  
**Skills** Athletics +9, Intimidation +7, Perception +6, Survival +6  
**Damage Resistances** Lightning  
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 16  
**Languages** Common, Draconic

**Challenge** 11 (7,200 XP)

---

**TRAITS**

- **Draconic Heritage (Lightning Resistance):** Draegon has innate resistance to lightning damage due to his draconic heritage, which makes him more resistant to electrical-based attacks.
- **Arcane Blade (Stormstrike):** Draegon’s weapon is imbued with arcane power, allowing him to channel electrical energy through his strikes. Once per turn, when Draegon hits a creature with his weapon, he can deal an additional 1d8 lightning damage. This ability also gives him advantage on attacks against creatures vulnerable to lightning.
- **Champion’s Will:** Draegon is unshakable in his loyalty to Xarathian. If he is reduced to 0 hit points while fighting in defense of Xarathian, Draegon can use his reaction to immediately make a single melee attack with advantage before falling unconscious. He can use this feature once per long rest.
- **Wyrmblade’s Fury:** As an action, Draegon can channel the storm energy within his blade, causing a burst of electric energy to spread in a 15-foot radius around him. All creatures in range must make a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw or take 3d6 lightning damage, or half on a successful save. Draegon can use this ability once per short rest.

---

**ACTIONS**

- **Multiattack:** Draegon makes three attacks with his blade.
- **Stormblade Strike (Melee Weapon Attack):** +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 17 (2d6 + 5) slashing damage plus 6 (1d8) lightning damage.
- **Lightning Surge (Recharge 5–6):** Draegon can release a wave of lightning in a 30-foot cone. Each creature in the cone must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw, taking 40 (8d8) lightning damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.
- **Thunderous Cleave (Recharge 5–6):** Draegon swings his blade in a wide arc, releasing a pulse of storm energy. All creatures in a 20-foot line must make a DC 16 Strength saving throw or take 26 (4d8 + 4) lightning slashing damage, or half as much on a successful save.
- **Challenge of the Sovereign (Recharge 6):** Draegon can challenge a creature to single combat in defense of Xarathian’s will. The target must succeed on a DC 17 Wisdom saving throw or be forced to focus all its attacks on Draegon for 1 minute. During this time, Draegon gains +2 AC and advantage on attack rolls against the target.
- **Arcane Shield (1/Day):** Draegon can invoke the storm energy within his blade to form an arcane shield around him. This shield grants Draegon temporary hit points equal to 3d8 + his Constitution modifier. These temporary hit points last for 1 minute or until depleted.

---

**BONUS ACTIONS**

- **Shielded Strike:** As a bonus action, Draegon can use his shield to gain +2 AC against one attack that would hit him, provided the shield is not already used for that turn. He can use this ability a number of times per day equal to his Constitution modifier (minimum of once).

---

**REACTIONS**

- **Storm Dodge:** When a creature within 10 feet of Draegon makes a ranged attack against him, Draegon can use his reaction to summon a burst of lightning, forcing the attacker to make a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the attacker takes 10 (2d8) lightning damage and the attack misses.
- **Counterstrike:** When a creature hits Draegon with a melee attack, Draegon can use his reaction to make a melee weapon attack with his blade against the creature.

---

**LEGENDARY ACTIONS**  
Draegon can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Draegon regains spent legendary actions at the start of his turn.

- **Storm Strike (Costs 2 Actions):** Draegon makes one Stormblade Strike with an additional 1d8 lightning damage.
- **Thunderous Cleave (Costs 3 Actions):** Draegon uses his Thunderous Cleave attack as a legendary action.

---

**Description:**  
Draegon, the Wyrmblade, is a fierce champion of Xarathian and a duelist of unparalleled skill. His draconic heritage and unwavering loyalty to the Blue Greatwyrm make him a formidable opponent on the battlefield. Clad in dark blue armor adorned with dragonbone pauldrons, Draegon wields his arcane-infused blade with deadly precision. As a master of lightning-based combat, he channels the storm’s fury into his strikes, making him both an offensive powerhouse and a resilient defender. Draegon is utterly devoted to Xarathian, viewing any threat to his lord as a personal challenge to his honor, and he will stop at nothing to ensure the success of his guild and the protection of his sovereign.
