### **Talonis the Truthseeker**

_Tiefling, Spymaster / Information Broker, CR 8_

**Armor Class** 15 (Natural Armor)  
**Hit Points** 105 (14d8 + 42)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

**STR** 10 (+0)  
**DEX** 16 (+3)  
**CON** 14 (+2)  
**INT** 20 (+5)  
**WIS** 14 (+2)  
**CHA** 18 (+4)

**Saving Throws** Dex +7, Int +9, Cha +8  
**Skills** Deception +12, Insight +6, Investigation +9, Perception +6, Persuasion +8, Stealth +7  
**Damage Resistances** Psychic  
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 16  
**Languages** Common, Infernal, Elvish, Thieves' Cant

**Challenge** 8 (3,900 XP)

---

**TRAITS**

- **Devil’s Sight:** Talonis can see in magical and non-magical darkness out to 120 feet.
- **Master of Deception:** Talonis has advantage on all Charisma (Deception) checks.
- **Shadow Manipulation:** As an action, Talonis can cast _Shadow Step_ (teleport up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space that he can see, provided it is in dim light or darkness) or _Pass Without Trace_ (self and up to 8 allies within 30 feet, leaving no trace of their passage for 1 hour). These abilities don't require spell slots and can be used once per short or long rest.
- **Lie Detection:** Talonis has the ability to sense when someone is lying. As a bonus action, Talonis can choose one creature within 30 feet of him that is speaking. That creature must succeed on a DC 16 Charisma saving throw or be compelled to reveal the truth. If the creature fails the saving throw, Talonis can ask one question and the creature must respond truthfully. This effect lasts for 1 minute.
- **Storm’s Influence (Recharge 5-6):** While within a storm or a zone of magical darkness (such as Xarathian’s domain), Talonis can use his powers to manipulate shadows and eavesdrop on any conversation within 120 feet. He can hear all conversations in the area as though he were present, even if they occur in total darkness. This effect lasts for 10 minutes and can be used once per long rest.

---

**ACTIONS**

- **Multiattack:** Talonis makes two attacks with his _Dagger of Deception_.
- **Dagger of Deception (Melee Weapon Attack):** +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 9 (2d6 + 3) piercing damage. On a critical hit, the target must make a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or become paralyzed until the end of Talonis' next turn.
- **Psychic Manipulation (Recharge 5-6):** Talonis uses his powers to warp the mind of a target. One creature within 60 feet must make a DC 16 Intelligence saving throw or take 21 (6d6) psychic damage and be frightened for 1 minute. A creature that succeeds on the saving throw takes half damage and is not frightened.
- **Shadow Manipulation (1/Day):** Talonis can envelop himself in shadows, becoming invisible and intangible for up to 1 minute. While in this state, he can move through creatures and objects as if they were difficult terrain. Any creature that touches him or attempts to attack him while he is in this form must succeed on a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw or take 10 (3d6) necrotic damage.
- **Dark Bargain (1/Day):** Talonis offers a tempting bargain to a creature within 30 feet. The creature must succeed on a DC 16 Charisma saving throw or become charmed by him for 1 hour. While charmed, the creature is compelled to follow Talonis' suggestions and commands to the best of its ability. Once a creature has resisted this effect, it cannot be affected by it again for 24 hours.

---

**BONUS ACTIONS**

- **Cunning Action:** Talonis can take a bonus action to Dash, Disengage, or Hide.
- **Subtle Blade (1/Short Rest):** Talonis can use his bonus action to make a melee attack with his dagger. If the attack hits, the target must make a DC 16 Constitution saving throw or become poisoned for 1 minute. While poisoned in this way, the creature has disadvantage on attack rolls and saving throws against being charmed or frightened.

---

**REACTIONS**

- **Evasive Maneuver:** When Talonis is hit by an attack, he can use his reaction to impose disadvantage on the attack roll if he is in dim light or darkness.
- **Counter-Intelligence (1/Short Rest):** When Talonis is targeted by a spell or ability that requires a saving throw, he can use his reaction to impose disadvantage on the caster's attack roll or saving throw.

---

**LEGENDARY ACTIONS**  
Talonis can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Talonis regains spent legendary actions at the start of his turn.

- **Shadow Step (Costs 1 Action):** Talonis teleports up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space that he can see.
- **Whisper in the Shadows (Costs 2 Actions):** Talonis uses his knowledge of lies and secrets to confuse a creature. A creature within 60 feet must make a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or be incapacitated until the end of Talonis' next turn.
- **Psychic Blade (Costs 3 Actions):** Talonis makes a melee attack with his _Dagger of Deception_. On a hit, the target must succeed on a DC 16 Intelligence saving throw or take 18 (4d8) psychic damage and be stunned until the end of Talonis' next turn.

---

**Description:**  
Talonis the Truthseeker is a master of espionage, manipulation, and deceit. His fiery red skin and curling horns give him an intimidating appearance, while his glowing yellow eyes reflect the razor-sharp mind he uses to gather secrets. Working behind the scenes, Talonis ensures that Xarathian’s rule remains unchallenged, using every tool at his disposal to uncover the hidden truths of his enemies. His ability to blend into the shadows and manipulate the fabric of lies and perception makes him an invaluable asset to the guild, but his ruthlessness in punishing betrayal ensures that loyalty is never taken lightly. Talonis is a master of psychological warfare, and when combined with his ability to move like a shadow, few are safe from his manipulations.
