### **Kaltorix the Stormbinder**

_Dragonborn (Bronze), Guild Enforcer / General, CR 13_

**Armor Class** 21 (Spiked Armor, Shield of the Stormbinder)  
**Hit Points** 175 (18d10 + 72)  
**Speed** 40 ft.

**STR** 22 (+6)  
**DEX** 14 (+2)  
**CON** 18 (+4)  
**INT** 14 (+2)  
**WIS** 16 (+3)  
**CHA** 18 (+4)

**Saving Throws** Str +11, Con +9, Wis +8, Cha +9  
**Skills** Athletics +11, Intimidation +9, Perception +8, Insight +8  
**Damage Resistances** Lightning  
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 18  
**Languages** Common, Draconic

**Challenge** 13 (10,000 XP)

---

**TRAITS**

- **Draconic Ancestry (Lightning Resistance):** Kaltorix has resistance to lightning damage due to his draconic heritage, granting him an edge against electrical attacks.
- **Stormbinder's Will:** Kaltorix is a master of storm magic. As a bonus action, Kaltorix can choose to charge his halberd with lightning, giving him the ability to channel the storm's fury into his melee attacks. On a successful melee hit with his halberd, Kaltorix can deal an additional 2d8 lightning damage and force the target to make a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw or become stunned until the end of their next turn.
- **Aura of Command:** Kaltorix exudes an aura of authority and discipline. Allies within 30 feet of him gain advantage on saving throws against being frightened and gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls.
- **Legendary Resistance (2/Day):** If Kaltorix fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead, up to 2 times per day.
- **Unyielding Loyalty:** Kaltorix’s loyalty to Xarathian is unshakeable. Once per day, when he is reduced to half his hit points or less, Kaltorix can use his reaction to gain temporary hit points equal to his Charisma modifier + 10. These temporary hit points last for 1 minute.

---

**ACTIONS**

- **Multiattack:** Kaltorix makes three attacks with his halberd.
- **Halberd of the Storm (Melee Weapon Attack):** +11 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 19 (2d8 + 6) slashing damage plus 8 (2d8) lightning damage.
- **Thunderous Cleave (Recharge 5–6):** Kaltorix swings his halberd in a wide arc, releasing a shockwave of lightning energy. All creatures in a 20-foot line must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw or take 42 (8d8) lightning damage, or half as much on a successful save.
- **Storm of Wrath (Recharge 5–6):** As an action, Kaltorix summons a violent thunderstorm in a 30-foot radius centered on him. Each creature in that area must make a DC 18 Constitution saving throw or take 35 (10d6) thunder and lightning damage, or half as much on a successful save. The storm lasts for 1 minute and is difficult terrain for creatures that aren't Kaltorix's allies.
- **Eye of the Storm (Recharge 6):** Kaltorix channels the fury of the storm into a 60-foot radius centered on himself. All enemies in range must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw or take 45 (10d8) lightning damage, or half as much on a successful save.
- **Chain Lightning (1/Day):** Kaltorix targets a creature within 120 feet. The target must make a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw, taking 66 (12d8) lightning damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one. This lightning arcs to one additional target within 30 feet of the original target, dealing the same damage.

---

**BONUS ACTIONS**

- **Thunderous Strike:** As a bonus action, Kaltorix can charge his halberd with lightning energy, causing his next melee attack to deal an additional 2d6 lightning damage and force the target to make a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or be stunned for 1 turn.

---

**REACTIONS**

- **Deflecting Storm:** When Kaltorix is hit by a ranged attack, he can use his reaction to deflect the attack with a burst of lightning energy. The attacker must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw or take 20 (4d8) lightning damage, or half on a successful save.
- **Storm’s Grasp:** When a creature moves within 10 feet of Kaltorix, he can use his reaction to make a melee attack with his halberd against that creature.

---

**LEGENDARY ACTIONS**  
Kaltorix can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Kaltorix regains spent legendary actions at the start of his turn.

- **Stormstrike (Costs 2 Actions):** Kaltorix makes one melee attack with his halberd, dealing an additional 1d8 lightning damage.
- **Stormstep (Costs 3 Actions):** Kaltorix moves up to 30 feet without provoking opportunity attacks, leaving behind a trail of lightning that deals 4d6 lightning damage to any creature in its path.
- **Summon Stormcloud (Costs 3 Actions):** Kaltorix summons a stormcloud above a 20-foot radius area, causing a flash of lightning that forces all creatures in the area to make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw or take 24 (6d6) lightning damage, or half as much on a successful save.

---

**Description:**  
Kaltorix the Stormbinder is a force of nature in both mind and body. His towering bronze form and spiked armor signify his role as the unyielding enforcer of Xarathian’s guild. Armed with a halberd that crackles with raw lightning power, Kaltorix is the embodiment of discipline and strength. His presence on the battlefield is a testament to his mastery over storms, as he can summon tempests to crush his enemies with the fury of nature itself. Though stoic and intimidating, Kaltorix’s devotion to Xarathian is absolute, and he is unwavering in his pursuit of order and unity within the guild. Any sign of betrayal is met with swift and brutal retribution under his watchful gaze.
