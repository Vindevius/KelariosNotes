### **Serithra the Chosen**

_Dragonborn (Blue), High Priestess of Xarathian, CR 10_

**Armor Class** 18 (ceremonial robes, magical protection)  
**Hit Points** 112 (15d8 + 45)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

**STR** 16 (+3)  
**DEX** 14 (+2)  
**CON** 16 (+3)  
**INT** 12 (+1)  
**WIS** 20 (+5)  
**CHA** 18 (+4)

**Saving Throws** Wis +9, Cha +8  
**Skills** Insight +9, Medicine +9, Persuasion +8, Religion +5  
**Damage Resistances** Lightning, Necrotic  
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 15  
**Languages** Draconic, Celestial, Common

**Challenge** 10 (5,900 XP)

---

**TRAITS**

- **Divine Power (Xarathian’s Blessing):** Serithra can channel the divine power of Xarathian, granting her the ability to cast the _Call Lightning_ spell at will (spell level 3). When casting _Call Lightning_ or any lightning-based spell, she deals an additional 1d6 lightning damage.
- **Aura of Devotion:** Serithra exudes an aura of sacred protection. All friendly creatures within 10 feet of her have advantage on saving throws against being frightened or charmed.
- **Sacred Healing:** As an action, Serithra can channel divine energy to heal herself or an ally within 30 feet. The target regains 4d8 + 5 hit points. This ability can be used three times per long rest.
- **Arcane Tattoos:** Serithra’s arcane tattoos grant her resistance to one damaging spell effect of her choice once per day. When she is targeted by a damaging spell, she may use her reaction to invoke the tattoos, granting her resistance to the damage of that spell.

---

**ACTIONS**

- **Multiattack:** Serithra makes two attacks, one with her staff and one with a divine lightning strike.
- **Staff of the Sovereign (Melee Weapon Attack):** +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 14 (2d6 + 5) bludgeoning damage plus 4 (1d8) lightning damage.
- **Divine Lightning Strike (Recharge 5–6):** Serithra calls down a bolt of divine lightning from Xarathian. All creatures within a 20-foot line must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 40 (12d6) lightning damage, or half as much on a successful one.
- **Channel Divine Fury (Recharge 6):** Serithra channels Xarathian’s wrath, summoning a storm of divine lightning that covers a 30-foot radius centered on a point she can see within 60 feet. Each creature in the area must make a DC 17 Constitution saving throw, taking 50 (10d10) lightning damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.
- **Divine Word (1/Day):** Serithra speaks a command in the voice of Xarathian, forcing all creatures of her choice within a 60-foot radius to make a DC 17 Charisma saving throw. Creatures that fail the saving throw are stunned for 1 minute.

---

**BONUS ACTIONS**

- **Blessed Shield:** Serithra may use her bonus action to grant a creature within 30 feet a shield of radiant energy, granting them +2 AC for 1 minute. This ability can be used three times per long rest.

---

### **Legendary Actions (3/Turn)**

Serithra can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time, and only at the end of another creature's turn. Serithra regains spent legendary actions at the start of her turn.

- **Channel Divine Lightning (Costs 2 Actions):** Serithra calls down a bolt of lightning in a 20-foot line. Each creature in the line must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw, taking 22 (6d6) lightning damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.
- **Divine Word (Costs 3 Actions):** Serithra speaks a command in the voice of Xarathian, forcing one creature of her choice within 60 feet to make a DC 17 Charisma saving throw. On a failed save, the creature is charmed for 1 minute.

---

**REACTIONS**

- **Arcane Ward (Recharge 5–6):** When Serithra is targeted by a spell, she can use her reaction to create a ward that absorbs damage. The ward reduces the damage taken by 20 points. The ward lasts until the end of her next turn.

---

**Description:**  
Serithra, the High Priestess of Xarathian, is a devout servant of the Blue Greatwyrm and a powerful wielder of divine magic. As the Keeper of Xarathian's Faith, she preaches discipline, loyalty, and devotion, ensuring the followers of the Blue Draconic Sovereign uphold their sacred vows. Her abilities include commanding divine lightning and storms, healing her allies, and protecting them with sacred magic. Though calm and peaceful in demeanor, Serithra is unyielding when it comes to betrayal, unleashing devastating power upon those who defy the will of Xarathian.
