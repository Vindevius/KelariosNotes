### **Kerriss Volthara the Diamond Hand**

_Half-Elf, Master of Artifacts and Magic Item Steward, CR 9_

**Armor Class** 16 (Magical Wardrobe)  
**Hit Points** 112 (15d8 + 45)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

**STR** 10 (+0)  
**DEX** 14 (+2)  
**CON** 16 (+3)  
**INT** 20 (+5)  
**WIS** 14 (+2)  
**CHA** 18 (+4)

**Saving Throws** Int +9, Wis +6, Cha +8  
**Skills** Arcana +13, History +9, Insight +6, Investigation +9, Persuasion +8  
**Damage Resistances** Psychic, Force  
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12  
**Languages** Common, Elvish, Draconic, Celestial

**Challenge** 9 (5,000 XP)

---

**TRAITS**

- **Arcane Attunement:** Kerriss can attune to any magical item with a mere touch, bypassing the normal attunement process. Once per day, she can reassign the attunement of a magical item to herself or another creature as an action, no matter the item’s usual requirements.
- **Master of Artifacts:** Kerriss can use her deep knowledge of magical items to identify and understand their true nature. As an action, she can cast _Identify_ on any magic item, learning its properties and any curses it may have.
- **Diamond’s Ward (Recharge 5-6):** Kerriss is protected by a mystical barrier woven from the enchanted diamonds she guards. When she takes damage, she can use her reaction to reduce the damage by 15 (3d10), reflecting the energy into the attacker. This ability can be used once per short or long rest.
- **Loyalty to the Sovereign:** Kerriss gains advantage on all Wisdom (Insight) checks made to detect deception or gauge the loyalty of others, particularly when those conversations involve Xarathian or his vision.

---

**ACTIONS**

- **Multiattack:** Kerriss makes two attacks with her _Diamond Blade_ or uses one of her spells.
- **Diamond Blade (Melee Weapon Attack):** +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 10 (2d6 + 3) slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) radiant damage. On a critical hit, the target must make a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or become blinded for 1 minute.
- **Arcane Blast (Recharge 5-6):** Kerriss releases a burst of arcane energy from her hands. Each creature in a 30-foot cone must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw, taking 28 (8d6) force damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.
- **Diamond Ward (1/Day):** Kerriss can cast _Shield_ as a reaction, increasing her AC by 5 for 1 round and preventing magic missile damage entirely.
- **Master’s Touch (1/Day):** Kerriss may imbue one magic item she is attuned to with a unique effect. For 1 hour, the item grants an additional +1 bonus to attack rolls, damage rolls, or saving throws, or can cast a 1st-level spell (such as _Mage Armor_ or _Shield_) once per long rest.

---

**BONUS ACTIONS**

- **Empowered Focus:** As a bonus action, Kerriss can choose one creature within 30 feet of her. That creature has disadvantage on any saving throws against Kerriss’ spells or magical effects until the end of her next turn.
- **Diamond Shard (Recharge 5-6):** Kerriss throws a shard of enchanted diamond at a creature she can see within 30 feet. The target must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw or take 14 (4d6) piercing damage and be stunned until the end of Kerriss' next turn. On a successful save, the target takes half damage and is not stunned.

---

**REACTIONS**

- **Diamond Counter (1/Short Rest):** When Kerriss is targeted by a melee attack, she can use her reaction to deflect the attack with the power of her enchanted diamonds, forcing the attacker to reroll their attack with disadvantage.
- **Arcane Reflection (1/Short Rest):** When Kerriss is hit by a damaging spell, she can use her reaction to reflect the spell's energy back at the caster, forcing the caster to make a DC 17 Intelligence saving throw. On a failed save, the caster takes half the damage that Kerriss would have taken. On a successful save, the caster takes no damage.

---

**LEGENDARY ACTIONS**  
Kerriss can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Kerriss regains spent legendary actions at the start of her turn.

- **Arcane Focus (Costs 1 Action):** Kerriss gains advantage on the next Intelligence-based attack roll or saving throw she makes before the end of her next turn.
- **Magic Item Rebalance (Costs 2 Actions):** Kerriss swaps the attunement of one magical item with another creature or herself. This counts as a short rest for the item, allowing it to recharge any abilities that have a recharge timer.
- **Diamond Strike (Costs 3 Actions):** Kerriss makes a melee attack with her _Diamond Blade_, dealing 12 (2d6 + 3) slashing damage plus 10 (3d6) radiant damage. The target must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or be blinded for 1 minute.

---

**SPELLCASTING**  
Kerriss is a 9th-level spellcaster. Her spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save DC 17, +9 to hit with spell attacks). She has the following spells prepared:

- **Cantrips (at will):** _Prestidigitation, Mage Hand, Detect Magic, Ray of Frost_
- **1st level (4 slots):** _Mage Armor, Shield, Detect Magic, Identify_
- **2nd level (3 slots):** _Mirror Image, Misty Step, Lesser Restoration_
- **3rd level (3 slots):** _Counterspell, Dispel Magic, Fly_
- **4th level (3 slots):** _Greater Invisibility, Polymorph_
- **5th level (2 slots):** _Cone of Cold, Wall of Force_
- **6th level (1 slot):** _Globe of Invulnerability_

---

**Description:**  
Kerriss Volthara, the Diamond Hand, is the master of magical items within the guild, her piercing violet eyes ever-watchful for those who would seek to misappropriate the treasures of Xarathian. Her sharp mind and calculating nature make her an invaluable steward of the arcane, and her collection of enchanted diamonds only amplifies her power. Cool-headed and pragmatic, she rewards loyalty with potent artifacts, using her vast knowledge of magic to maintain order and ensure the vision of Xarathian is realized. Those who prove their worth may find themselves the recipient of the Diamond Hand’s favor—but only if they can meet her exacting standards.
