Settlement Creation Template for ChatGPT

1. What is the settlement’s name?
(Example: Vorran, Eldermere, Ravenshade)
2. Is the settlement a Village (population up to 500), Town (population 501–5,000), or City (population 5,001+)?
(Example: Village, Town, City)
3. What is the settlement’s defining trait?
(Examples: Fortified outer wall, Sprawling cemetery, Clean streets and well-maintained buildings, Impressive structure like a temple or castle, Lots of mud, filth, and litter)
4. What is the settlement’s claim to fame?
(Examples: Delicious food, Seasonal festival, Powerful guilds, Ancient ruins, Renowned spellcasters, Piety, Gambling, Education, Flowers)
5. What is the settlement’s current calamity?
(Examples: Monsters infest the settlement, War brews between rival guilds, A natural disaster looms, Scandal threatens powerful families, A prophecy of doom has residents on edge)
6. Who is/are the local leader(s)?
(Examples: Respected and fair council, Feared tyrant, Coward manipulated by others, Contested leadership with open fighting, Religious leader or council, Mysterious conspirators)
7. Who are the noteworthy people?
(Describe important figures like adventurers, merchants, scholars, criminals, or other key NPCs.)
8. What are the noteworthy places?
Include at least one tavern and four others. Examples: pawnshop, apothecary, grocer, bookstore, smithy, armorer, undertaker, jeweler, tailor, mapmaker, scribe.
9. What is the GP (gold piece) value of the most expensive item for sale?
(Village: 20 GP, Town: 2,000 GP, City: 200,000 GP)
10. What is the settlement’s historical importance?
(Examples: Founded during an ancient empire, Site of a great battle, Hometown of a famous hero, Contains ruins of a lost civilization.)
11. What magical elements are there?
(Examples: Magical academy, Enchanted forests, Arcane relics, Leyline convergence, Warded districts, Frequent magical phenomena.)
12. What is the community atmosphere?
(Examples: Warm and welcoming, Suspicious of outsiders, Energetic and ambitious, Wary and secretive, Peaceful and relaxed.)
13. What iconic landmarks are there?
(Examples: Towering statue, Grand bridge, Massive ziggurat, Ancient tree, Magical fountain, Imposing citadel.)
14. What are the geographical surroundings?
Describe the environment and its impact on the settlement. Examples: Riverbanks fostering trade, Fertile plains for agriculture, Mountain passes for defense, Forests for timber and hunting.
15. How many and what city districts are there?
