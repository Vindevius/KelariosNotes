**1. What is the settlement’s name?**  
Arlindar

---

**2. Is the settlement a Village (population up to 500), Town (population 501–5,000), or City (population 5,001+)?**  
City

---

**3. What is the settlement’s defining trait?**  
Grand canals and intricate stone bridges that span throughout the city, giving it a unique and picturesque layout akin to a "city on water."

---

**4. What is the settlement’s claim to fame?**  
Renowned artisans who craft exquisite glasswork and jewelry that fetch high prices across the realm.

---

**5. What is the settlement’s current calamity?**  
A recent break in the magical barriers protecting the city has allowed water elementals to flood the lower districts, causing panic and halting trade.

---

**6. Who is/are the local leader(s)?**  
Lady Aleryn Vennel, an iron-willed and respected leader, governs Arlindar alongside a council of skilled artisans and merchants.

---

**7. Who are the noteworthy people?**

- **Thalric Everforge:** A dwarven smith known for embedding magical properties into glass weapons.
- **Selira Dewbright:** A halfling bard famous for her enchanting performances in the city’s grand amphitheater.
- **Kovan Drelkar:** A reclusive mage studying the ancient leyline beneath the city.
- **Mayor Andrus Quain:** A well-meaning but slightly corrupt official overseeing the Riverfront District.

---

**8. What are the noteworthy places?**

- **The Glass Swan Tavern:** A cozy tavern with crystalline windows overlooking the largest canal.
- **Celestine Forge:** Thalric Everforge's workshop where glass weapons and tools are created.
- **The Azure Market:** A sprawling open-air market featuring exotic wares and Arlindar’s famous glass jewelry.
- **The Amphitheater of Echoes:** An open performance space renowned for its acoustics and nightly entertainment.
- **Mystara’s Archivum:** A towering library housing ancient scrolls and tomes, including blueprints for the city’s magical barriers.

---

**9. What is the GP (gold piece) value of the most expensive item for sale?**  
200,000 GP (Rare enchanted glass artifact known as "The Prism of Eternity").

---

**10. What is the settlement’s historical importance?**  
Founded on the convergence of two powerful leylines, Arlindar was once the seat of an ancient mageocracy. The city's canals were magically engineered centuries ago to sustain life and commerce.

---

**11. What magical elements are there?**

- A leyline convergence beneath the city strengthens magic.
- The enchanted canals glow faintly at night due to residual arcane energy.
- Mystara’s Archivum contains spell scrolls and relics from the mageocracy era.

---

**12. What is the community atmosphere?**  
Energetic and ambitious, the citizens of Arlindar pride themselves on their craft and ingenuity but harbor an undercurrent of fear due to the elemental incursions.

---

**13. What iconic landmarks are there?**

- **The Crystal Spire:** A towering structure in the city center, reflecting sunlight in dazzling displays.
- **The Great Canal Gate:** A massive, ornate gate that regulates the flow of water between districts.
- **The Ruins of Arvethis:** Crumbled remains of a mageocracy temple near the city outskirts, now a place of mystery and intrigue.

---

**14. What are the geographical surroundings?**  
Arlindar is nestled in a fertile river delta bordered by lush wetlands to the east and rolling hills to the west. The rivers provide abundant fish and serve as trade routes, while the hills yield marble and precious minerals. The wetlands, however, are often shrouded in mist, harboring both beauty and danger.
