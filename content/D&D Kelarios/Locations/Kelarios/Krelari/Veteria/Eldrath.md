1. **Settlement Name:** Eldrath
2. **Type of Settlement:** City
3. **Defining Trait:** **Vast Canal System** – Eldrath is known for its intricate network of canals that wind through the city, fed by the river flowing into the Valmere. These canals serve as both transportation routes and a means to manage the river’s water, providing the city with an impressive water infrastructure. Boats, barges, and small ferries are the primary mode of transportation in Eldrath, and the streets are lined with walkways elevated above the canals.
4. **Claim to Fame:** Eldrath is famous for its **rich history in trade and maritime innovations**. The city has long been a hub for merchants, particularly in rare spices, silk, and exotic goods from across Veteria. Additionally, Eldrath is renowned for its **advanced shipbuilding** industry, which has produced some of the finest vessels for trade and exploration. The city is also famous for hosting a seasonal **Regatta Festival**, where competitors race the city's finest boats along the river and canals.
5. **Current Calamity:** Eldrath is dealing with a **devastating riverbank erosion**. The banks of the river that feed the canals are crumbling, threatening the integrity of the entire canal system. Several bridges and docks have already been damaged, and the constant threat of flooding looms over the city. Many fear that if the erosion isn’t addressed soon, Eldrath could face a catastrophic collapse of its essential infrastructure, leading to a potential loss of trade and livelihood.
6. **Local Leaders:** The city is governed by a **Council of Merchant Lords**, headed by **Lady Ilyana Arinfall**, a shrewd and influential figure in Eldrath's trade circles. While she is highly respected, her leadership has been questioned due to her reliance on mercantile interests and her somewhat detached approach to the riverbank erosion crisis. The council, consisting of powerful merchants and wealthy shipbuilders, has been slow to respond to the calamity, which has led to growing unrest among the working class and shipbuilders.
7. **Noteworthy People:**
    - **Lady Ilyana Arinfall:** The council leader and head of Eldrath’s most influential merchant family. She is well-known for her vast wealth and influence but has been accused of prioritizing personal gain over the welfare of the city.
    - **Kaelen Vorthil:** A renowned shipwright who is spearheading an initiative to design a new type of boat that could survive the dangerous conditions in Eldrath’s canals. Kaelen is a passionate innovator but struggles to secure support from the city’s leaders.
    - **Orwen Farrow:** A former adventurer turned historian and scholar. Orwen is one of the few who believes that the river erosion may have magical causes tied to ancient events, and she has been researching lost artifacts or spells that might help alleviate the disaster.
    - **The Gray Watcher (Mira Falest):** A mysterious figure who has recently arrived in Eldrath, Mira seems to know far more about the erosion than anyone else, claiming to have knowledge of ancient river magics that could reverse the damage. She is viewed with suspicion but also a hint of hope.
8. **Noteworthy Places:**
    - **The Drowned Mermaid Tavern:** A bustling tavern located near the southern dock, famous for its lively atmosphere and the freshest seafood in Eldrath. It is a favorite spot for sailors and merchants to unwind after a long day.
    - **The Canalworks Workshop:** A vast workshop where the city's boats and ships are built and repaired. The city’s shipwrights gather here to collaborate on designs, and it is rumored that Kaelen Vorthil is working on a new boat design to save the city’s fleet.
    - **The Riverstone Apothecary:** A highly regarded apothecary that sells rare river herbs and tonics, many of which are believed to have magical properties that help counteract the pollution and sickness caused by the failing canals.
    - **The Eldrath Archives:** An ancient library and museum filled with records of the city’s history, including its maritime trade routes, shipbuilding techniques, and the legends of river spirits. The archives are also home to scholars like Orwen Farrow, who study Eldrath’s forgotten magical history.
    - **The Stilted Market:** An open-air market suspended on stilts above the canals, where vendors sell exotic goods from across Veteria. The market is famous for its rare spices, silks, and trinkets, and is a major draw for merchants and tourists.
9. **Most Expensive Item for Sale:** 150,000 GP – A **legendary river spirit charm**, an ancient artifact said to have the power to control the flow of water in the canals. It is believed to be able to reverse the damage caused by the erosion, though its authenticity is disputed.
10. **Historical Importance:** Eldrath was founded centuries ago during a great expansion of trade along the rivers that eventually fed into the Valmere River. The city became a vital maritime hub, with its shipyards producing vessels for both merchants and explorers. It is also home to ancient relics of river spirits and magical artifacts related to water manipulation. Eldrath is regarded as one of the foremost cities for trade, with its waterways creating a unique blend of commerce, culture, and magical history.
11. **Magical Elements:** Eldrath is built atop a **powerful water leyline** that runs beneath the city, which once enhanced the prosperity of the city’s waterways. However, scholars like Orwen Farrow suspect that the leyline is now unstable, contributing to the riverbank erosion. Some of the older districts in the city still have remnants of **water magic** that were used to control the river and canals, and rumors suggest that a powerful artifact, the **Heart of the River**, may still be hidden within the Eldrath Archives.
12. **Community Atmosphere:** The atmosphere in Eldrath is **tense and divided**. While the wealthy merchant lords are concerned with the economic downturn caused by the crisis, they are slow to take action. The working class, including sailors, shipbuilders, and dockworkers, is becoming increasingly frustrated with the lack of progress and the failure to address the erosion. There is a growing sense of **desperation** as the city’s future seems uncertain, though pockets of hope remain, particularly among those who believe in ancient magic or technological innovation.
13. **Iconic Landmarks:**
	- **The Eternal Dock:** A grand, stone dock that extends far into the river, built as a tribute to the city’s maritime heritage. It is said that no matter how much the river erodes, the Eternal Dock will always remain standing as a symbol of Eldrath’s resilience.
	- **The Waterspire Tower:** A tall, twisting tower made of white stone that overlooks the city from its highest point. It is said to be a remnant of an ancient river deity’s temple, and some believe it may hold the key to understanding the magic that once protected the river.
	- **The Bridge of Whispering Waters:** An ornate bridge that spans the river where it flows into the Valmere. It is believed to have been built with the aid of water magic, and locals claim that the sound of rushing water beneath the bridge can whisper forgotten secrets to those who listen closely.
14. **Geographical Surroundings:** Eldrath is located along the banks of a wide river that eventually joins the Valmere River. The land around the city is **fertile and rich in natural resources**, with dense forests to the west and expansive farmlands to the east. The river has historically been a vital source of both trade and water, but the ongoing erosion of the riverbanks is putting these resources at risk. The surrounding wilderness is also home to **mythical creatures** connected to the river, adding an element of mystery and potential danger to the city.
15. **City Districts:**
	- **The Canal District:** The industrial heart of Eldrath, where the shipyards, boatbuilders, and canal infrastructure are located. It is a noisy and busy area, vital for the economy.
	- **The Merchant District:** Where the city’s wealthiest traders and merchants live. It is filled with lavish estates, high-end shops, and lavish markets.
	- **The Scholar’s Quarter:** A quieter district known for its libraries, magical institutions, and academies where scholars study river magic and history.
	- **The Dockside District:** A bustling area on the river’s edge, full of taverns, inns, and fish markets. It is the lifeblood of the city’s trade and has the most direct access to the river.
	- **The Old Town:** The oldest district in the city, home to historical buildings, ancient temples, and ruins from Eldrath’s founding days. The area is a popular tourist attraction and has a few hidden magical secrets.
