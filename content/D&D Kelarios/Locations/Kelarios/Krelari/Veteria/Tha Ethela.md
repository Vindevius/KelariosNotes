1. **Settlement Name:** Tha Ethela
2. **Type of Settlement:** City
3. **Defining Trait:** The most defining feature of Tha Ethela is its **Grand Keep**, a towering structure overlooking the city, with a massive gladiatorial arena at its base, once a symbol of the empire's power and military prowess. The keep’s faded grandeur is a stark reminder of the city’s past glory.
4. **Claim to Fame:** Tha Ethela was once the heart of a mighty empire, and its gladiatorial games in the arena were famous throughout the known world. Now, despite its faded majesty, it still draws visitors who seek to experience the remains of its imperial splendor and the famed gladiator fights that continue in the arena.
5. **Current Calamity:** The city is struggling with **cultural decay and political fragmentation**. Despite the might of the former empire, internal strife, corruption, and a lack of clear leadership have caused a rapid decline. The streets once filled with military pride are now home to beggars and criminals. There are whispers of external threats from Kyria, taking advantage of the city’s weakness.
6. **Local Leaders:** Tha Ethela is now governed by a **Council of Nobles**, each with their own faction. The council’s authority is waning as the various factions bicker for control, and a number of nobles are vying for the position of **Emperor**. The position is effectively symbolic, as the true power lies in the streets and among the influential mercenary guilds.
7. **Noteworthy People:**
    - **Magister Aric Tahlvar:** A former noble who has taken refuge among the commoners, believed to be orchestrating a movement to bring the city back to its imperial roots.
    - **Fiora Ironhand:** A famous gladiator turned mercenary leader, she commands a powerful group of ex-soldiers and is a hero to many in the city.
    - **Kira Dorne:** A well-known merchant who deals in rare artifacts, many of which date back to the empire's height. Some say she’s looking for a way to use these artifacts to revive Tha Ethela’s past.
8. **Noteworthy Places:**
    - **The Arena of Blood:** The gladiatorial arena at the base of the keep, where famous battles are fought to this day. It’s both a tourist attraction and a political tool for the city’s leaders.
    - **The Iron Flagon Tavern:** A rowdy tavern frequented by mercenaries, gladiators, and the disillusioned. It’s a place where you can hear the gossip of the city’s underworld.
    - **The King’s Hall:** The faded, ceremonial hall where the last king, King Astrolites Harlor, ruled. It is still used for grand ceremonies but is mostly ignored by the population now.
    - **The Market of Fallen Stars:** A market selling rare and ancient items, many of them relics from the empire’s peak, and other goods from across the known world.
    - **The Memorial Garden:** A quiet and somber place where statues of fallen emperors and heroes of the empire stand, slowly being overtaken by weeds and decay.
9. **Most Expensive Item for Sale:** 500,000 GP – An ancient relic, a golden crown once worn by the last king, said to hold a powerful magic that can restore the glory of the empire if used correctly.
10. **Historical Importance:** Tha Ethela was the capital of the Veterian Empire, a once-mighty civilization that controlled vast lands. The city stands as a monument to the empire’s might and eventual fall, especially after the defeat of the **Vile Ones** twenty years ago, an event that shattered its dominance.
11. **Magical Elements:** Tha Ethela still contains remnants of powerful **arcane relics** from its imperial past. Some of the ancient artifacts held in the Memorial Garden and Emperor’s Hall are said to be imbued with powerful magic, though many have been lost or stolen. The city is also rumored to sit on a **leyline**, which powers the arena’s enchantments.
12. **Community Atmosphere:** The atmosphere is one of **melancholy and tension**. While the people still revere their history and former glory, they are disillusioned by the city’s decline. Outsiders are seen as a potential means of restoration, but there is also a strong sense of betrayal by the old empire. The streets are filled with those trying to survive, from mercenaries to thieves.
13. **Iconic Landmarks:**
	- **The Grand Keep:** The towering structure overlooking the city, home to the ruling council and the arena. Its once-impressive architecture now shows signs of wear and decay.
	- **The Arena of Blood:** A colossal gladiatorial arena, now used for both entertainment and political gain.
	- **The Obelisk of the Fallen:** A monument commemorating those who fell in the war against the Vile Ones.
	- **The Gates of Ethela:** The grand gates that were once the symbol of the empire’s power. They are now in disrepair and stand open, no longer guarding the city but instead serving as a symbol of lost authority.
14. **Geographical Surroundings:** The city sits at the southern edge of **Lake Lirienne**, where the river from **Arvelin** meets the lake. The city’s location was once strategic for trade, but now its waterways are often clogged with debris and have become dangerous due to shifting tides and storms. The fertile lands around the city have been left largely untended, though there is still some agriculture in the outskirts.
15. **City Districts:**
	- **The Imperial District:** Home to the Grand Keep, the council’s chambers, and some of the old noble houses.
	- **The Arena District:** A bustling area surrounding the gladiatorial arena, filled with taverns, inns, and businesses catering to mercenaries and tourists.
	- **The Merchant’s Quarter:** The hub for trade, with an open-air market, shops selling relics, and various wares from across the lands.
	- **The Fallen District:** Once a thriving residential area for the elite, now a slum filled with poverty, crime, and discontent. The streets are dark and dangerous.

Tha Ethela has truly fallen from its former glory, but it still retains its historical and political significance, even as it teeters on the edge of irrelevance. The struggle between maintaining its imperial identity and adapting to the changing world is at the heart of its decline.
