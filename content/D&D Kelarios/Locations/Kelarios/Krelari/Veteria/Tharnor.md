1. **Settlement Name:** Tharnor
2. **Type of Settlement:** City
3. **Defining Trait:** **Massive Dams and Watermills** – Tharnor is famous for its monumental dams and watermills, constructed where the Valmere River meets another river to the east. These structures control the flow of the rivers, generating power for the city and protecting it from flooding. The dams and watermills are integral to Tharnor’s thriving economy and infrastructure.
4. **Claim to Fame:** Tharnor is renowned for its **innovative hydraulic engineering**, particularly the system of dams and watermills that power its industries, including textile production, grain mills, and metalworking. The city is also famous for its **exported textiles**, particularly luxurious fabrics made from river silk, a rare material harvested from the banks of the rivers.
5. **Current Calamity:** Tharnor is facing a **severe water shortage**. The merging of two rivers has caused a shift in the water flow, and the once-reliable dams are beginning to fail. Additionally, the river silk trade has slowed down due to overharvesting and environmental degradation. These combined factors have left the city in a state of crisis, with food shortages and unrest brewing among the poorer classes.
6. **Local Leaders:** The city is governed by a **council of engineers**, led by **Jaren Galthor**, a brilliant but secretive chief engineer. While Jaren is respected for his technical expertise, his tendency to keep crucial decisions behind closed doors has caused tension among the citizens. The council also includes several wealthy textile barons who have a heavy influence on the economy and have clashed with Jaren's vision of sustainability.
7. **Noteworthy People:**
    - **Jaren Galthor:** The chief engineer and leader of the ruling council. He is determined to solve the city's water crisis but faces resistance from the wealthier factions who fear the cost of his proposed solutions.
    - **Lysa Tamsyn:** A prominent river silk merchant and former ally of the council. She has become more of a rogue player in the city’s politics, seeking to control the remaining silk trade by exploiting the city's water shortage for profit.
    - **Lennox Graves:** A former soldier turned scholar who now studies the historical and magical properties of the city’s water systems. His research has led him to uncover ancient methods of river control and rumored lost technologies that could save the city—if they can be found in time.
8. **Noteworthy Places:**
    - **The Silver Current Tavern:** A popular tavern located near the river docks, where merchants and traders meet to discuss deals and gossip about the city’s latest developments. The tavern’s owner is a former adventurer who has heard many tales of the city’s ancient water engineering secrets.
    - **The Waterwheel Workshop:** A large industrial workshop where the city’s famed watermills are crafted and repaired. It is a place of constant activity and a symbol of the city’s reliance on water-based technology.
    - **The River Silk Market:** The central hub for trading river silk and related textiles. The market is filled with colorful fabrics and exotic goods, but a sense of anxiety lingers as business slows due to the city’s growing water crisis.
    - **Tharnor’s Library of Engineering:** A vast repository of knowledge on ancient and modern hydraulic technologies. It is a place of learning for engineers and scholars alike, and some believe it holds the key to saving the city’s water system.
    - **The Broken Dam Ruins:** A failed dam located at the northern edge of the city, it was once part of the first attempt to control the merging rivers. The dam has now become a dangerous landmark where adventurers search for ancient relics or lost engineering secrets.
9. **Most Expensive Item for Sale:** 250,000 GP – An **ancient hydraulic relic**, a carved stone tablet believed to hold the secrets to the original engineering of the city’s river control systems. It is rumored that whoever possesses the tablet could restore the city’s water balance.
10. **Historical Importance:** Tharnor was founded centuries ago by a group of engineers who harnessed the power of the Valmere River and the smaller river to the east. The city’s engineering feats allowed it to prosper in a previously uninhabitable region, but it also led to the creation of powerful trade networks based on river silk. Tharnor is considered one of the early marvels of hydraulic engineering in Veteria.
11. **Magical Elements:** Tharnor’s water systems are believed to be **enchanted**, with ancient magic infused into the design of the original dams and watermills. Some scholars, like Lennox Graves, believe that there is an old **leyline** running beneath the city’s rivers, enhancing its hydraulic properties. There are also rumors of hidden **magical artifacts** that can manipulate water hidden within the Broken Dam Ruins.
12. **Community Atmosphere:** Tharnor’s community is **divided** and **anxious**. The wealthier classes are focused on maintaining their profits, particularly in textiles, while the poorer citizens are starting to grow frustrated with the slow pace of change. There is a general feeling of **tension** in the air, as many believe that the council is not doing enough to address the growing water shortage. The citizens are **skeptical of outsiders** but generally polite and practical.
13. **Iconic Landmarks:**
	- **The Grand Confluence Bridge:** A massive stone bridge that spans the point where the Valmere River meets the smaller river. It is an architectural marvel and symbol of the city’s mastery over the rivers.
	- **The Silver Fountain:** A grand fountain made from polished silver, built at the center of the city. It once symbolized the city’s prosperity, but now, it serves as a reminder of what is at stake if the water crisis is not addressed.
	- **The Great Dam:** A colossal, ancient dam built over the merging rivers. Though it no longer functions properly, it is a symbol of the city’s original glory and a constant reminder of the challenges ahead.
1. **Geographical Surroundings:** Tharnor lies at the **confluence of the Valmere River** and a smaller river that winds down from the northeastern mountains. The city is surrounded by fertile plains and forested hills that provide resources for the growing textile industry. However, the changing river flow is beginning to affect the surrounding agriculture, and the water crisis has begun to spread beyond the city into the surrounding farms.
2. **City Districts:**
	- **The River District:** The industrial heart of the city, filled with watermills, workshops, and warehouses. It is a noisy and busy area, essential for the city’s economy.
	- **The Silk District:** Where the wealthy merchants and traders live, it is full of high-end textile shops, fine restaurants, and opulent estates.
	- **The Engineer’s Quarter:** A district filled with workshops, schools, and the library of engineering. It is where the city’s finest minds gather to try and solve the water crisis.
	- **The Ruined District:** The northern area of the city that houses the Broken Dam Ruins and the ancient river systems. It is considered dangerous and has fallen into disrepair.
