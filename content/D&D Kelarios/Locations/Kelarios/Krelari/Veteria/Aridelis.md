1. **Settlement Name:** Aridelis
2. **Type of Settlement:** City
3. **Defining Trait:** **Riverside Canals** - Aridelis is built along the **Valmere River** and is crisscrossed with canals that flow into the river, allowing for easy transport of goods and people. These canals are lined with beautiful stone bridges and are a central feature of daily life.
4. **Claim to Fame:** Aridelis is known for its **mastery of river engineering**, particularly its impressive system of canals, waterwheels, and aqueducts that power much of the city’s industry. Its skilled engineers and craftsmen draw attention from all over Veteria. The city is also famous for its **harboring of ancient river artifacts**, including boats, statues, and writings that hint at lost civilizations.
5. **Current Calamity:** The city is suffering from **pollution** and **overcrowding**. The once-pristine canals have become murky with waste from the city's expanding population and industry. The merge of two rivers to the north is causing unpredictable flooding, putting parts of the city at risk. Additionally, some have turned to **smuggling artifacts** as a way to profit off the city's ancient ruins, which has attracted the attention of dangerous criminals.
6. **Local Leaders:** The city is led by **Vidal Vaelarn**, a young and ambitious mayor who is trying to modernize the city. However, he struggles with **political instability**, as the older, more traditional factions, particularly those who control the river trade and ancient relics, resist his reforms. The city's leadership is often divided between these factions.
7. **Noteworthy People:**
    - **Sennor Wildleaf:** A renowned river engineer and inventor, responsible for many of the city's modern water-based innovations. He is both admired and secretly feared for his ability to control the flow of the city's rivers.
    - **Lady Ilaya Vaelarn:** Vidal’s sister, a noblewoman who holds significant sway over the merchant class and has been rumored to use the city's ancient artifacts for political manipulation.
    - **Kaelen Mossfoot:** A scholar and historian who specializes in the ancient river civilizations. He has discovered several secrets hidden beneath the city, but his research has made him a target for powerful factions.
8. **Noteworthy Places:**
    - **The Silver Current Tavern:** A popular gathering place for river traders, scholars, and travelers. It serves the finest fish dishes in the city and is known for hosting discussions about politics and the city’s future.
    - **The River’s Edge Market:** A bustling market where traders from the river regions come to sell exotic goods, including rare fish, waterborne goods, and ancient relics.
    - **The Temple of the Flowing Waters:** An ancient temple dedicated to water deities, where city leaders often go for blessings before important decisions. It is rumored to be built over ruins from a lost civilization that once dominated the riverbanks.
    - **The Iron Aqueduct:** A massive aqueduct that carries water from the nearby river systems to the heart of the city. It is an engineering marvel, but its foundations are beginning to show signs of wear and pressure from the city’s growing population.
    - **The Shifting Tombs:** A hidden and dangerous ruin that lies beneath Aridelis. Ancient, forgotten tunnels and chambers are believed to be buried here, filled with relics and perhaps even secrets about the city’s origins.
9. **Most Expensive Item for Sale:** 150,000 GP – An **ancient river artifact**, a golden boat said to have been used by the founders of the river civilization that once lived along the Valmere. It is rumored to have magical properties, including the ability to control water.
10. **Historical Importance:** Aridelis is one of the few cities in Veteria that has continuously thrived since the fall of the empire. It was founded as a key trade hub at the confluence of two important rivers and has preserved many relics from ancient civilizations that predate the current empire. The city’s historical importance lies in its role as a crossroads of cultures and its harboring of ancient river ruins.
11. **Magical Elements:** Aridelis is a city steeped in **water magic**. The convergence of two rivers is said to cause magical phenomena that allow some water-based magic to be stronger here than in other regions. There are rumors of hidden **arcane relics** deep within the ancient ruins beneath the city, especially in the Shifting Tombs. The **river temples** scattered around the city are said to hold strong magical power, though it’s unclear whether they still function.
12. **Community Atmosphere:** The atmosphere of Aridelis is **dynamic** and **resourceful**. While the city is rapidly modernizing, it retains a deep respect for its past and the power of water. People from all walks of life can be found here, and the general mood is one of optimism mixed with a healthy dose of skepticism toward change. The growing pollution and overcrowding have led to some **resentment** toward the rich, but most people are united by their shared history and reliance on the river.
13. **Iconic Landmarks:**
	- **The River’s Gateway Bridge:** A massive stone bridge that spans the Valmere River, marking the entrance to the city from the south. It’s an iconic symbol of Aridelis’ mastery of water engineering.
	- **The Temple of the Flowing Waters:** A large, intricate temple complex that sits at the edge of the river and is visible from nearly every corner of the city.
	- **The Tower of Streams:** A tall, cylindrical tower that houses the city’s most advanced river-based engineering workshop. It’s an imposing structure built directly over the river, with flowing water beneath it.
	- **The Forgotten Plaza:** An ancient, neglected public square with statues and ruins from the lost river civilization. It is a place of mystery and wonder, drawing historians and treasure hunters alike.
1. **Geographical Surroundings:** Aridelis sits at the confluence of the **Valmere River** and another smaller river to the east. The rivers create a fertile and rich environment for agriculture, particularly fish and water-based crops, making the region prosperous. However, the merging of these two rivers to the north causes unpredictable flooding and shifting landscapes, which have been a source of concern for the citizens. The surrounding land is mostly **marshy wetlands**, with forests to the west that provide timber and other resources.
2. **City Districts:**
	- **The River District:** The heart of the city’s commerce, filled with canals, markets, and the docks where traders arrive from across the region.
	- **The Engineer’s Quarter:** A district known for its workshops, waterwheels, and mechanical innovations. This area is full of ambitious inventors and artisans who build the city’s water infrastructure.
	- **The Noble District:** Where the wealthier residents and politicians reside, filled with large estates and villas overlooking the river.
	- **The Ancient District:** The oldest part of the city, with ruins, ancient temples, and hidden catacombs from past river civilizations. It is both a place of history and a mystery to many.
