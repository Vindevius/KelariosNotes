1. **What is the settlement’s name?**
- **Caerwyn**
2. **Is the settlement a Village, Town, or City?**
- **City** (Population 85,000+)
3. **What is the settlement’s defining trait?**
- **A sprawling citadel** perched on a hill overlooking the rivers, symbolizing the seat of power for [[Stagalla]]. The citadel’s golden spires can be seen from miles away, shining brilliantly in the sun.
4. **What is the settlement’s claim to fame?**
- Renowned for its **political influence and cultural sophistication**, Caerwyn is the heart of Stagalla's governance, arts, and diplomacy. It hosts **The Grand Conclave**, a yearly gathering of leaders from across the kingdom.
5. **What is the settlement’s current calamity?**
- A **rising tension among noble families** threatens to erupt into open conflict, as rival factions vie for influence over the throne. Additionally, whispers of a **hidden rebellion** add to the city’s unease.
6. **Who is/are the local leader(s)?**
- **King Edryc Lathorne IV**, a wise yet aging monarch beloved by the common folk but mistrusted by the ambitious nobles. His failing health has emboldened potential successors to position themselves for the throne.
- **Lady Yselda Dauren**, the head of the Council of Ministers, is a pragmatic yet secretive politician, suspected of consolidating power for her own ends.
7. **Who are the noteworthy people?**
- **Lord Varrin Ardyn** – The charismatic leader of the Ardyn faction, known for his oratory and ambition to modernize Stagalla.
- **Maelis of the Gilded Quill** – A celebrated playwright and bard whose works often carry veiled critiques of the nobility.
- **Thorne Fleetblade** – A former mercenary turned adventurer, now a captain in the city guard, tasked with keeping peace amid political turmoil.
- **Sister Elenna Vyral** – A healer and high priestess of the Temple of Luminara, revered for her wisdom and calming influence during times of crisis.
8. **What are the noteworthy places?**
- **The Golden Gryphon Inn** – A luxurious tavern catering to travelers, nobles, and adventurers, featuring performances by renowned bards and exotic delicacies.
- **The Artisan’s Promenade** – A bustling district filled with master craftsmen and artists, known for its exquisite jewelry, tapestries, and enchanted trinkets.
- **The Grand Archives** – A vast library housing thousands of tomes on history, magic, and philosophy, attracting scholars from across the continent.
- **The Temple of Luminara** – A grand temple dedicated to the goddess of light and order, serving as a center of worship and healing.
- **The Market of Ten Rivers** – An enormous open-air market where traders from all over Stagalla and neighboring lands gather to sell exotic goods and rare magical items.
9. **What is the GP (gold piece) value of the most expensive item for sale?**
- **200,000 GP** for an ancient, enchanted amulet that enhances diplomatic charisma and provides magical protection, available only in the Artisan’s Promenade.
10. **What is the settlement’s historical importance?**
- Caerwyn was **founded during the unification of Stagalla**, chosen for its strategic position at the confluence of two rivers. It has been the site of numerous historic treaties and battles, and its citadel has never fallen to an invading force.
11. **What magical elements are there?**
- **Warded districts** protect against magical threats, particularly in the citadel and Artisan’s Promenade.
- The **Crystal Fountain** in the Temple of Luminara is a source of holy water said to possess miraculous healing properties.
- **Frequent magical phenomena**, such as glowing lights over the rivers during full moons, attributed to leyline activity beneath the city.
12. **What is the community atmosphere?**
- **Energetic and ambitious**, with a sharp divide between the vibrant lower districts and the more reserved and opulent noble quarters. The people are proud of their city but wary of the political scheming that defines its upper echelons.
13. **What iconic landmarks are there?**
- **The Sunspire Citadel** – The heart of the kingdom’s governance, a magnificent fortress with golden spires symbolizing the unity of Stagalla.
- **The Twin Rivers Bridge** – A massive stone bridge adorned with statues of past kings and queens, spanning the major river and connecting the city’s districts.
- **The Obsidian Obelisk** – A mysterious, ancient monument on the outskirts of the city, believed to predate the kingdom itself.
14. **What are the geographical surroundings?**
- Located in a **fertile river valley**, Caerwyn’s position at the confluence of two rivers makes it a natural hub for trade and agriculture. To the west, fertile plains stretch far, while dense forests to the north provide timber and hunting grounds. The smaller river serves as a strategic defense and a source of irrigation.
15. **How many and what city districts are there?**
- **Noble Quarter** – Home to grand estates and government buildings, including the Sunspire Citadel.
- **Artisan’s Promenade** – A cultural hub filled with workshops, galleries, and theaters.
- **Riverside District** – The bustling trade and market area, lined with warehouses and merchant stalls.
- **Temple Ward** – Dedicated to religious and magical pursuits, including the Temple of Luminara.
- **Commons** – The residential area for the working class, lively and crowded, with taverns and shops catering to everyday needs.
