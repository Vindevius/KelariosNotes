1. **What is the settlement’s name?**
    - **Cryndralis**
2. **Is the settlement a Village, Town, or City?**
    - **City** (Population ~12,000)
3. **What is the settlement’s defining trait?**
    - **Frozen Canals**, with many streets replaced by waterways that freeze over in winter, transforming into paths for ice skates and sleds.
4. **What is the settlement’s claim to fame?**
    - **Ice-Sailing**, a unique sport where residents navigate frozen rivers and canals on sled-like vessels with sails, attracting thrill-seekers and spectators.
5. **What is the settlement’s current calamity?**
    - **River Blight**, an unknown affliction turning the water of the freezing river and canals dark and toxic, killing fish and threatening trade and drinking supplies.
6. **Who is/are the local leader(s)?**
    - **High Chancellor Vyra Selindris**, a pragmatic and determined leader whose policies aim to balance trade and public welfare, but whose rule is increasingly challenged due to the calamity.
7. **Who are the noteworthy people?**
    - **Kaltis Vrynforge** – A dwarven master engineer who maintains the frozen canal systems.
    - **Ellira Frosstrain** – An elven herbalist and healer searching for a cure to the River Blight.
    - **Captain Drevik Tundralance** – Commander of the city guard, known for his stern demeanor and unshakable loyalty to Cryndralis.
    - **Tyvren “The Whisper” Sablecoat** – A smuggler and information broker who thrives in the shadows of the canals.
    - **Lyria Shardveil** – A bard renowned for her hauntingly beautiful songs about Cryndralis' icy winters.
8. **What are the noteworthy places?**
    - **The Frostbitten Flagon** – A lively tavern serving warming spirits and hearty meals to fend off the tundra's chill.
    - **Shardworks Armory** – Renowned for its intricate froststeel weapons, forged to withstand the tundra's elements.
    - **The Glasswater Market** – A sprawling marketplace along the frozen canals, offering exotic goods brought by traders.
    - **The Crystal Atrium** – A magical greenhouse where enchanted plants grow despite the harsh climate.
    - **The Iceflow Archives** – A grand library partially carved from glacial ice, containing texts on northern lore and magic.
9. **What is the GP (gold piece) value of the most expensive item for sale?**
    - **200,000 GP** for an **Iceveil Amulet**, a rare artifact that protects the wearer from cold and enhances frost magic.
10. **What is the settlement’s historical importance?**
- Cryndralis was a **bastion of resistance** during an ancient northern war, using its frozen canals and fortified ice walls to repel invaders.
11. **What magical elements are there?**
- **Enchanted Ice Canals**, which stay frozen year-round due to ancient spells.
- **The Frostspire**, a towering obelisk of ice at the city's center, radiating protective magic.
- **Glacial Spirits**, ethereal beings said to inhabit the ice, occasionally aiding or hindering the city’s residents.
12. **What is the community atmosphere?**
- **Energetic and Ambitious**, with a populace striving to overcome adversity, innovate, and celebrate their icy heritage.
13. **What iconic landmarks are there?**
- **The Frostspire**, a towering magical obelisk.
- **The Frozen Gates**, massive ice-carved barriers guarding the city’s northern entrance.
- **The Silver Glade**, an enchanted grove of ever-frosted trees at the city's edge.
- **The Ice-Sail Arena**, where the city's famed ice-sailing tournaments take place.
14. **What are the geographical surroundings?**
- Cryndralis lies on the **banks of a freezing river**, bordered by **frozen tundra** to the south and **snow-draped coniferous forests** to the northwest. The river once supported trade and fishing but is now afflicted by the River Blight, forcing the city to rely more on land-based routes and ice-sailing caravans.
15. **How many and what city districts are there?**
- **The Frostholm District** – Residential area with homes built into natural ice and rock formations.
- **The Canal Quarter** – Center of trade and commerce along the frozen waterways.
- **The Frostspire Ward** – Governmental and administrative hub surrounding the Frostspire.
- **The Forge District** – Industrial zone where froststeel weapons and tools are crafted.
- **The Greenveil Enclave** – Houses the Crystal Atrium and serves as a sanctuary for herbalists and alchemists.
- **The Iceflow Outskirts** – Perimeter district for hunters, trappers, and those living close to the tundra's edge.
