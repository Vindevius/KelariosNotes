1. **What is the settlement’s name?**
    - **Glacoria**
2. **Is the settlement a Village, Town, or City?**
    - **Town** (Population ~4,300)
3. **What is the settlement’s defining trait?**
    - **Glacier-Hewn Architecture**, with buildings carved directly into the ice and stone of the tundra, blending seamlessly into the environment.
4. **What is the settlement’s claim to fame?**
    - **Frostglass Craftsmanship**, a unique glass-like material forged from enchanted glacial ice, renowned for its beauty and resilience.
5. **What is the settlement’s current calamity?**
    - **Encroaching Ice Wraiths**, malevolent spirits from the frozen wastes, are attacking travelers and settlements, putting Glacoria on edge.
6. **Who is/are the local leader(s)?**
    - **Warden Kaelitha Rimeheart**, a stoic ranger who commands respect through her bravery and deep knowledge of the tundra. She leads both the town guard and an elite scouting force.
7. **Who are the noteworthy people?**
    - **Eryndel Glaceforge** – A master Frostglass artisan whose works are sought after across the region.
    - **Vikar Stoneseer** – A hermit druid living on the outskirts, believed to have insight into the Ice Wraiths’ origins.
    - **Captain Sythrin Wyrdveil** – The commander of the Frostwatch, a group tasked with protecting Glacoria from the growing threats.
    - **Asha Coldbloom** – A merchant specializing in rare tundra herbs and magical reagents.
    - **Tarvin Icevein** – A reclusive scholar researching ancient ruins buried beneath the ice.
8. **What are the noteworthy places?**
    - **The Frosted Stag Tavern** – Known for its warm hearth and spiced tundra ale, it serves as a refuge from the bitter cold.
    - **Glaceforge Hall** – Eryndel’s workshop, where the town’s famous Frostglass is crafted and sold.
    - **The Chillwind Market** – A bustling open-air market selling furs, herbs, and enchanted items from the tundra.
    - **The Icehearth Sanctuary** – A small but stunning temple to the gods of winter and survival.
    - **The Glacier Archives** – A subterranean library containing records and artifacts from long-forgotten civilizations.
9. **What is the GP (gold piece) value of the most expensive item for sale?**
    - **2,000 GP** for a **Frostglass Shield**, resistant to fire and imbued with protective enchantments.
10. **What is the settlement’s historical importance?**
- Glacoria was founded on the site of an **ancient civilization** that thrived in the tundra. Many of its ruins and artifacts lie buried beneath the ice, drawing scholars and adventurers.
11. **What magical elements are there?**
- **Glacial Leylines**, faint magical currents running beneath the ice, which power the crafting of Frostglass.
- The **Everfrost Beacon**, a towering crystalline structure that radiates warmth and wards off lesser magical threats.
- **Frostflower Blooms**, rare tundra plants with potent magical properties, grow in the surrounding area.
12. **What is the community atmosphere?**
- **Wary and resilient**, as residents are cautious of outsiders but fiercely loyal to one another, united by the harsh environment.
13. **What iconic landmarks are there?**
- **The Everfrost Beacon**, a massive crystalline obelisk that emits a soft blue glow, visible for miles.
- **The Iceveil Bridge**, an arched structure of Frostglass spanning the River Eldrin.
- **The Glacier Gate**, a fortified entrance carved into a natural ice wall.
- **The Shardspire**, a jagged, naturally occurring spire of ice that looms over the town.
14. **What are the geographical surroundings?**
- Glacoria sits on the **River Eldrin’s frozen banks**, surrounded by **snow-covered plains** and **glacial formations**. Sparse tundra vegetation supports minimal agriculture, while nearby caves and ice shelves offer shelter and resources. The environment is harsh but navigable for those accustomed to the cold.
15. **How many and what city districts are there?**
- **The Frostglass District** – The industrial heart, housing workshops and smithies.
- **The Hearth District** – A residential area with communal firepits and insulated homes.
- **The Tundra Ward** – The outskirts, where hunters, druids, and scouts live and operate.
- **The Glacial Gate Quarter** – A defensive zone near the town’s fortified entrance.
- **The Iceveil Market** – A central trading hub, vibrant with life despite the cold.
