### 1. **What is the settlement’s name?**
- **Drakemere**
---

### 2. **Is the settlement a Village, Town, or City?**
- **City** (Population ~45,000)
---

### 3. **What is the settlement’s defining trait?**
- **The Harbor of Wyrms**, a massive port shaped like a dragon’s maw, with enormous stone arches carved into dragon heads guarding the entrance to the harbor.
---

### 4. **What is the settlement’s claim to fame?**
- Known for its **shipbuilding and navigation**, Drakemere produces the finest vessels in Stagalla. Its shipwrights are unmatched, and their enchanted ships are sought after by traders, explorers, and military fleets.
---

### 5. **What is the settlement’s current calamity?**
- A **series of mysterious disappearances** have plagued the docks, with rumors of sea monsters or an ancient curse connected to the harbor.
---

### 6. **Who is/are the local leader(s)?**
- **Lord-Merchant Aedric Solryn**, the shrewd and pragmatic head of the Drakemere Merchant’s Guild, who rules the city like a business.
- **Captain Vyressa Stormborn**, the commander of the city’s naval defense and a respected hero of the Icelorn Strait. She is outspoken about the rising threat to the harbor.
---

### 7. **Who are the noteworthy people?**
- **Master Corrath Velstrin** – The chief shipwright, a grizzled artisan who claims to have crafted a ship that once sailed into a storm and returned unscathed.
- **Ilaem the Salvager** – A mysterious diver who recovers treasures from shipwrecks in the Icelorn Strait and sells them in the black market.
- **Arlen Duskwake** – A scholar and historian studying ancient ruins found along the coast, believed to be linked to the harbor’s curse.
- **Mara Hollowstream** – The owner of the **Siren’s Call Tavern**, known for her sharp wit and extensive network of information.
---

### 8. **What are the noteworthy places?**
- **The Siren’s Call Tavern** – A lively and raucous meeting place for sailors, traders, and adventurers, decorated with salvaged ship parts and glowing sea lanterns.
- **The Kraken’s Forge** – A renowned smithy specializing in crafting nautical tools, weapons, and ship fittings.
- **The Navigators’ Guildhall** – A grand hall where maps and charts are made and sold, and expert navigators offer their services.
- **The Pearl Market** – A vibrant market on the docks where exotic goods, magical relics, and rare pearls from the strait are traded.
- **The Mariner’s Chapel** – A serene place of worship dedicated to Avesca, goddess of the sea, where sailors seek blessings before voyages.
---

### 9. **What is the GP (gold piece) value of the most expensive item for sale?**
- **200,000 GP** for the **Wyrmwind Compass**, a magical artifact said to guarantee safe passage through even the fiercest storms.
---

### 10. **What is the settlement’s historical importance?**
- Drakemere was founded during the **Age of Dragons**, with legends claiming it was once the lair of a sea dragon. The city’s name and harbor carvings pay homage to this mythic past.
---

### 11. **What magical elements are there?**
- **Arcane relics** frequently wash ashore or are dredged from shipwrecks in the strait.
- **Leyline convergence** in the harbor enhances enchantments, making Drakemere the premier location for crafting magical ships.
- **Frequent magical phenomena**, such as glowing tides or mysterious singing heard on moonlit nights, believed to be tied to the sea dragon legend.
---

### 12. **What is the community atmosphere?**
- **Wary and secretive**, especially in light of the recent disappearances. While the people are proud and resourceful, they are cautious of outsiders and superstitions.
---

### 13. **What iconic landmarks are there?**
- **The Dragon’s Maw Harbor** – The iconic dragon-head arches at the harbor’s entrance are visible from miles away.
- **The Lantern Tower** – A massive lighthouse powered by an ever-burning magical flame, guiding ships safely into port.
- **The Sea Drake Statue** – A colossal bronze statue of a sea dragon in the central square, said to protect the city from harm.
- **The Coral Spire** – A mysterious, ancient tower on a nearby island, partially submerged and surrounded by glowing coral.
---

### 14. **What are the geographical surroundings?**
- Situated on the **Icelorn Strait**, Drakemere’s coast features jagged cliffs, sandy beaches, and natural coves perfect for shipbuilding. The icy waters are rich with fish and pearls, while the nearby islands harbor hidden dangers and ruins.
---

### 15. **How many and what city districts are there?**
- **Harbor District** – The bustling hub of trade, docks, and shipyards.
- **Artisan’s Quarter** – Home to shipwrights, smiths, and craftsmen specializing in nautical goods.
- **Market Square** – A vibrant area for trade and commerce, including the Pearl Market.
- **Old Town** – The original settlement, featuring narrow streets, ancient stone buildings, and the Sea Drake Statue.
- **Temple Ward** – Centered around the Mariner’s Chapel, this district is a peaceful refuge for sailors and the faithful.
