### 1. **What is the settlement’s name?**

- **Frosthaven**

---

### 2. **Is the settlement a Village, Town, or City?**

- **City** (Population ~35,000)

---

### 3. **What is the settlement’s defining trait?**

- **Frost-Forged Bridges**, intricate stone-and-iron bridges connecting the city’s districts over the wide and slow-moving river, each decorated with ice-resistant runes that glow faintly in the cold.

---

### 4. **What is the settlement’s claim to fame?**

- Renowned for its **frostglass**, a translucent, enchanted material crafted from riverbed minerals that is both durable and beautiful. It’s widely used in architecture, art, and magical crafting.

---

### 5. **What is the settlement’s current calamity?**

- **A mysterious blight** affecting the river’s ecosystem, killing fish and plants, and spreading sickness to those who rely on the water.

---

### 6. **Who is/are the local leader(s)?**

- **Council of Elders**, a group of seven experienced leaders chosen for their wisdom and service. The head councilor is **Erynn Kaelthor**, a no-nonsense elder with a strong connection to the local druids.

---

### 7. **Who are the noteworthy people?**

- **Master Arkin Frostwright** – The city’s most famous frostglass artisan, whose works are in high demand across the region.
- **Lyrica Wintermourn** – A wandering bard who arrived recently and claims to have insight into the river’s curse.
- **Calyth Arvidor** – The captain of the Frostguard, the city’s militia, known for her skill with a frost-enchanted halberd.
- **Brother Saren Mornshade** – A priest of Avesca who oversees the **Shrine of the River’s Heart**, dedicated to the river spirit that protects the region.
- **Voren Iceclaw** – A poacher-turned-hunter who now tracks dangerous creatures threatening Frosthaven.

---

### 8. **What are the noteworthy places?**

- **The Frozen Lantern Tavern** – A warm, bustling inn where enchanted frostglass lanterns illuminate the tables, and travelers share stories by roaring hearths.
- **The Frostglass Foundry** – The workshop where the famous frostglass is crafted, led by Master Arkin.
- **The River’s Heart Shrine** – A serene temple on the riverbank where prayers are offered to the spirit of the river.
- **Winter’s Edge Armory** – A shop specializing in cold-resistant armor and frost-enchanted weapons.
- **The Cryomancer’s Hall** – A magical academy focusing on frost magic, weather manipulation, and alchemy related to the cold.

---

### 9. **What is the GP (gold piece) value of the most expensive item for sale?**

- **200,000 GP** for the **Shard of Everice**, a legendary frostglass relic that provides immunity to cold damage and radiates a protective aura.

---

### 10. **What is the settlement’s historical importance?**

- Frosthaven was founded by explorers fleeing a terrible winter storm centuries ago. The river and its spirit saved their lives, leading to the city’s reverence for the waterway and its bounty.

---

### 11. **What magical elements are there?**

- **Frostglass crafting** imbues magical properties into tools, weapons, and art.
- The **river spirit**, an ancient elemental force, occasionally manifests in times of great need.
- **Cryomantic studies** at the Cryomancer’s Hall create frequent magical phenomena, like controlled snowfalls or icy sculptures that move like living beings.

---

### 12. **What is the community atmosphere?**

- **Warm and welcoming**, with a strong sense of unity and resilience. Despite the current blight, the people work together to protect and sustain their home.

---

### 13. **What iconic landmarks are there?**

- **The Winterfang Bridge** – The largest and most ornate of the Frost-Forged Bridges, adorned with frostglass panels that shimmer in the sunlight.
- **The River’s Embrace Plaza** – A central square featuring a massive frostglass fountain shaped like flowing water.
- **The Cryomancer’s Spire** – A towering, frost-encrusted structure that houses the magical academy.
- **The Icewarden Keep** – The city’s fortress, constructed from frostglass and stone, protecting the northern approaches.

---

### 14. **What are the geographical surroundings?**

- Frosthaven lies beside the **River Myrris**, which provides trade, food, and water. Dense **coniferous forests** surround the city, supplying timber and game. The **tundra’s edge** to the north brings cold winds and hardy travelers. Fertile riverbanks allow limited but vital agriculture.

---

### 15. **How many and what city districts are there?**

- **The River Ward** – Home to docks, markets, and the Frostglass Foundry.
- **The Artisan’s Quarter** – Where artisans, smiths, and merchants craft and sell their wares.
- **The Scholar’s Hollow** – Centered around the Cryomancer’s Hall and other places of learning.
- **The Sentinel District** – A fortified area housing the Icewarden Keep and the Frostguard barracks.
- **The Temple Ward** – Where the River’s Heart Shrine and smaller places of worship reside.
