1. **What is the settlement’s name?**
    - **Glacivane**
2. **Is the settlement a Village, Town, or City?**
    - **City** (Population ~8,500)
3. **What is the settlement’s defining trait?**
    - **Heated Walkways** – The city uses geothermal energy to keep key streets and bridges free of ice, enabling year-round movement despite the harsh climate.
4. **What is the settlement’s claim to fame?**
    - **Thermal Springs** – Natural hot springs along the near-frozen river provide warmth and health benefits, attracting visitors and settlers.
5. **What is the settlement’s current calamity?**
    - **Rift Raiders** – A band of marauders from the Bitterglass Peaks has been launching increasingly bold attacks on the city’s trade routes, cutting off vital supplies.
6. **Who is/are the local leader(s)?**
    - **Marshal Kelvar Harrowfell** – A battle-hardened veteran with a keen mind for strategy, striving to defend Glacivane against external threats.
    - **Councilor Alira Snowshade** – A cunning and ambitious politician who seeks to exploit the current crisis for personal gain.
7. **Who are the noteworthy people?**
    - **Drystan Embercleft** – A gnome artificer responsible for maintaining the geothermal heating systems.
    - **Faylen Frostroot** – A half-elf herbalist who creates potent remedies using rare plants from the Bitterglass Peaks.
    - **Krennar "Blightbane" Orindor** – A dwarven mercenary captain leading a group of hunters targeting Rift Raiders.
    - **Vierra Ashshroud** – A mysterious mage researching ancient arcane phenomena tied to the Bitterglass Peaks.
    - **Liora Valecryst** – A popular bard and storyteller who weaves tales of Glacivane’s history and heroism.
8. **What are the noteworthy places?**
    - **The Steaming Tankard** – A cozy tavern where travelers warm themselves with spiced cider and stew.
    - **Emberstone Forge** – A smithy specializing in tools and weapons tempered with geothermal heat.
    - **Thermalaine Baths** – Public hot springs with healing properties, a hub for relaxation and social gatherings.
    - **Frostveil Emporium** – A shop dealing in rare goods brought over the Bitterglass Peaks.
    - **Auric Archives** – A library and research center focusing on the ancient lore of the region.
9. **What is the GP (gold piece) value of the most expensive item for sale?**
    - **200,000 GP** for a **Ring of Eternal Warmth**, which shields the wearer from cold and grants resistance to frost magic.
10. **What is the settlement’s historical importance?**
- Glacivane was built atop ancient **geothermal ruins** from a long-lost civilization, harnessing their technology to survive the tundra’s extreme cold.
11. **What magical elements are there?**
- **Runic Hot Springs**, enchanted to amplify their healing properties.
- **Leyline Divergence**, a network of magical energy beneath the city that powers its geothermal systems.
- **Auroral Nights**, a phenomenon where the sky shimmers with magical light, inspiring awe and superstition.
12. **What is the community atmosphere?**
- **Warm and Welcoming**, with residents taking pride in their city’s resilience and offering hospitality to travelers braving the Bitterglass Peaks.
13. **What iconic landmarks are there?**
- **The Frostforge Bridge**, a massive stone-and-ice structure spanning the near-frozen river.
- **The Thermal Cascade**, a series of natural hot springs descending from the mountains into the city.
- **The Sentinel Spire**, a watchtower at the edge of the Bitterglass Peaks, manned by vigilant guards.
- **The Crystal Chapel**, a temple built entirely of magically fused ice and stone.
14. **What are the geographical surroundings?**
- The city lies between the **Bitterglass Peaks** and the **Frostvein River**, whose waters remain liquid due to geothermal activity. The surrounding tundra is barren, with sparse forests offering limited resources. The mountain passes are treacherous, but they serve as vital trade routes.
15. **How many and what city districts are there?**
- **The Hearthward** – Residential district with heated streets and communal fires.
- **The Forgeworks** – Industrial hub utilizing geothermal energy.
- **The Steamward** – District around the Thermalaine Baths, catering to travelers and merchants.
- **The Frostspire Keep** – Administrative and military center, housing the city’s leaders and defenders.
- **The Trade Quarter** – Markets and shops dealing in goods from across the region.
