1. **What is the settlement’s name?**
    - **Rivermont**
2. **Is the settlement a Village, Town, or City?**
    - **City** (population 8,000)
3. **What is the settlement’s defining trait?**
    - Rivermont is defined by its massive **confluence bridge**, which spans the merging rivers, connecting the city to both its northern and southern districts.
4. **What is the settlement’s claim to fame?**
    - Rivermont is renowned for its **silk industry**, producing some of the finest silk fabric in the region, which is highly sought after by merchants, nobles, and royalty.
5. **What is the settlement’s current calamity?**
    - Rivermont faces growing **tensions between Stagallan and Veterian refugees**, as well as political instability. The citizens are divided over loyalty to their new Stagallan rulers and the desire to reclaim their Veterian heritage. Unrest is simmering beneath the surface, with occasional riots breaking out.
6. **Who is/are the local leader(s)?**
    - The city is governed by **Governor Lysara Wrenhold**, a stern but charismatic leader who was appointed by Stagalla. However, the city’s previous leaders, exiled Veterian nobles, maintain a covert underground network that opposes the Stagallan presence, creating a divided leadership with secret alliances and rival factions.
7. **Who are the noteworthy people?**
    - **Senric Orosk** – A former Veterian general now turned mercenary, Senric is a force to be reckoned with in the city's lower districts, commanding a group of loyal followers.
    - **Aelia Valear** – A local silk merchant who has managed to navigate the political upheaval and become one of the richest individuals in the city. She is rumored to have connections to both Stagallan officials and former Veterian nobles.
    - **Chandra Wyrsbane** – A charismatic street preacher who speaks of an impending revolution and calls for the unification of Rivermont under the Veterian flag.
8. **What are the noteworthy places?**
    - **The Confluence Tavern** – A bustling watering hole located where the rivers meet, serving both local brews and exotic imports. It’s the prime spot for adventurers and traders.
    - **The Silk House** – A large and elegant fabric shop, selling the city’s finest silks. It also doubles as a meeting place for the city's elite and politicians.
    - **Lysara’s Keep** – A large stone keep overlooking the rivers, serving as the seat of the city’s governance and the residence of Governor Lysara Wrenhold.
    - **Old Rivermont Market** – The original market district of the city, now a maze of winding alleys and stalls selling everything from exotic spices to underground weapons.
    - **The River’s Edge Chapel** – A beautiful temple dedicated to the river gods, located along the riverbanks. It has become a neutral ground for religious leaders and political figures seeking to broker peace.
9. **What is the GP (gold piece) value of the most expensive item for sale?**
    - **100,000 GP** for an exquisite bolt of royal silk from the Silk House, famed for its shimmering quality and durability.
10. **What is the settlement’s historical importance?**
- Rivermont was **once a key port city in Veteria**, strategically placed at the meeting of two major rivers. During the war twenty years ago, it was one of the last cities to fall, witnessing a great battle on its riverbanks. Its fall marked the decline of Veterian power in the region, and its eventual annexation by Stagalla has left scars both physically and politically.
11. **What magical elements are there?**
- Rivermont is known for **river magic**, as the merging of the two rivers is thought to enhance magical powers related to water and flow. Some local spellcasters can manipulate the flow of the rivers or communicate with water spirits. A hidden magical order known as the **Tidesworn** operates in secret, guarding powerful water-related artifacts.
12. **What is the community atmosphere?**
- The atmosphere in Rivermont is **tense and divided**, with the war and subsequent political shift still fresh in the minds of its residents. While there are pockets of vibrant trade and friendly camaraderie, many people are on edge due to the political unrest and the looming threat of conflict.
13. **What iconic landmarks are there?**
	- **The Confluence Bridge** – The city’s defining feature, a grand bridge that connects both banks where the two rivers converge. It is a symbol of the city’s past and present.
	- **The Silver Fountain** – An ancient, ornate fountain made of stone and silver, located in the city center. It is said to be the source of the city’s prosperity.
	- **Lysara’s Keep** – The imposing seat of Stagallan power, perched above the city and the confluence of the rivers, offering a commanding view of Rivermont.
1. **What are the geographical surroundings?**
- Rivermont is surrounded by **fertile river valleys**, providing ample agricultural resources, and dense forests further to the north, ideal for logging. The merging of the two rivers makes it an important trade hub, but the constant threat of flooding keeps the city’s inhabitants on edge during the rainy season.
2. **How many and what city districts are there?**
	- **The Northern District** – Primarily residential, home to the nobility and the more affluent residents, including merchants, guilds, and city officials.
	- **The Market District** – Where trade and commerce flourish, with busy streets lined with shops, taverns, and goods from all over the world.
	- **The Riverside District** – Known for its bustling docks and warehouses, where the river meets the city. This district also serves as a hub for smuggling and underground trade.
	- **The Old District** – The oldest part of the city, with narrow alleyways, historic buildings, and ancient ruins from the Veterian era. It is also home to the secretive Tidesworn order.
