1. **What is the settlement’s name?**
    - **Frostreach**
2. **Is the settlement a Village, Town, or City?**
    - **City** (Population ~6,000)
3. **What is the settlement’s defining trait?**
    - **Ice-Bound Harbor** – Frostreach’s docks are carved directly into the glacier at the mouth of the Frostvein River, with reinforced piers that can withstand the encroaching ice.
4. **What is the settlement’s claim to fame?**
    - **Frozen Trade Hub** – The city serves as a crucial port for trade with northern seafarers and isolated settlements in Stagalla.
5. **What is the settlement’s current calamity?**
    - **Encroaching Ice** – The harbor and surrounding areas are freezing over faster than usual, threatening to cut off trade and supplies. Some believe it is tied to a curse or magical imbalance.
6. **Who is/are the local leader(s)?**
    - **Harbormaster Korran Velswick** – A pragmatic dwarf with a no-nonsense approach to running the city and managing its trade.
    - **Elder Maelis Greythorn** – An elven seer who warns that the calamity may have supernatural origins, causing tension with Korran.
7. **Who are the noteworthy people?**
    - **Captain Seraya Frostcloak** – A renowned ship captain whose fleet braves the icy northern waters.
    - **Tilmar "Tidewalker" Elaren** – A half-elf scholar researching the accelerating ice growth.
    - **Brurik Coldhammer** – A blacksmith specializing in tools and weapons tempered for the extreme cold.
    - **Josslyn Wintersong** – A charismatic bard and spy gathering information on the city’s troubles.
    - **Grynn Thornhearth** – A human rogue and leader of the **Frostfangs**, a smuggling group exploiting the current chaos.
8. **What are the noteworthy places?**
    - **The Frosted Tankard** – A lively tavern known for its strong mead and warm hearth.
    - **Coldspire Forge** – A smithy that uses enchanted flames to work steel in subzero conditions.
    - **Glacier Market** – A bustling trade square built on reinforced ice, featuring northern wares and exotic goods.
    - **The Tempest Chapel** – A shrine dedicated to sea and storm deities, often visited by sailors and traders.
    - **The Shardhaven Archive** – A small library housing tomes on the city’s history and arctic lore.
9. **What is the GP (gold piece) value of the most expensive item for sale?**
    - **200,000 GP** for the **Amulet of Winter’s Embrace**, which grants immunity to cold damage and enhances frost magic.
10. **What is the settlement’s historical importance?**
- Frostreach was founded as a **strategic port** during a past empire’s northern expansion, serving as a staging ground for explorations into the uncharted northern sea.
11. **What magical elements are there?**
- **Glacial Wards** – Ancient enchantments protect the harbor from completely freezing but are weakening.
- **Leyline Disturbance** – The nearby leyline convergence may be causing the unnatural ice growth.
- **Polar Auroras** – The night sky is often filled with vivid magical lights that some claim hold divinatory power.
12. **What is the community atmosphere?**
- **Wary and Secretive**, with residents growing distrustful of outsiders as the city’s challenges mount.
13. **What iconic landmarks are there?**
- **The Reachspire** – A massive tower of ice and stone overlooking the harbor, serving as a watchtower and signal beacon.
- **The Ice Floe Bridge** – A natural ice formation connecting the city to the river’s northern bank, reinforced with magic.
- **The Silent Beacon** – A towering statue of a cloaked figure said to guard the city against supernatural threats.
14. **What are the geographical surroundings?**
- Frostreach lies on the **frozen delta** where the Frostvein River meets the northern sea. The coastline is a mix of ice shelves and rocky outcroppings, while inland tundra provides limited resources. The icy waters are home to dangerous sea creatures, but also prized fish.
15. **How many and what city districts are there?**
- **The Harborfront** – Dockyards and trade houses managing goods and shipping.
- **The Glacier Ward** – Residential district with small, insulated homes.
- **The Reachspire Quarter** – Administrative and military district.
- **The Market Ward** – Central hub for trade and commerce.
- **The Northreach** – Outskirts housing laborers, hunters, and poorer residents.
