### 1. **What is the settlement’s name?**

- **Eldermoor**

---

### 2. **Is the settlement a Village, Town, or City?**

- **City** (Population ~22,000)

---

### 3. **What is the settlement’s defining trait?**

- **Terraced Waterfront**, with multi-tiered docks, gardens, and bustling markets overlooking the river, designed to prevent flooding and optimize trade.

---

### 4. **What is the settlement’s claim to fame?**

- Known for its **Riverwine**, a unique blend fermented using rare fruits grown in the fertile riverbanks, exported far and wide.

---

### 5. **What is the settlement’s current calamity?**

- **Rival merchant guilds** are locked in a bitter power struggle, leading to rising tensions, street clashes, and disruption of trade.

---

### 6. **Who is/are the local leader(s)?**

- **Merchant Council**, a group of five influential traders. Currently dominated by two opposing factions: **House Orlanth**, led by the ambitious and ruthless merchant **Iris Orlanth**, and **The Rivermill Consortium**, headed by the pragmatic but shrewd **Marro Grethain**.

---

### 7. **Who are the noteworthy people?**

- **Captain Varis Wyrmbane** – Leader of the **Riverguard**, tasked with maintaining order amidst the guild strife.
- **Liora Venfell** – An enigmatic herbalist whose remedies are highly sought after, rumored to have connections with druids.
- **Garik Blackmoor** – A former pirate-turned-dockmaster who enforces strict discipline on the bustling waterfront.
- **Ethelin Duskshade** – A playwright whose politically charged dramas often mirror the city’s tensions.
- **Thane Clovrick** – An independent blacksmith whose quality work is prized by both guilds, making him a figure of contention.

---

### 8. **What are the noteworthy places?**

- **The Gilded Oar Tavern** – A favorite of traders and sailors, known for its Riverwine tastings and heated debates.
- **The Azure Forge** – Thane Clovrick’s smithy, producing some of the best weapons and tools in the region.
- **The Rivermarket** – A sprawling market with traders from far-off lands selling exotic wares.
- **The Eldergrove Apothecary** – Liora Venfell’s shop, offering rare potions and cures, often surrounded by a faint, floral aroma.
- **The Driftwood Theater** – A popular venue for plays and performances, located near the riverfront.

---

### 9. **What is the GP (gold piece) value of the most expensive item for sale?**

- **200,000 GP** for a **Shard of Tempest Glass**, a legendary relic said to protect its wielder from water and wind-based magic.

---

### 10. **What is the settlement’s historical importance?**

- Eldermoor was established as a trading hub during the height of an ancient empire, serving as a vital link between inland territories and coastal regions.

---

### 11. **What magical elements are there?**

- **Leyline-fed irrigation systems**, ensuring the fertile riverbanks produce abundant crops.
- The **Riftstone Fountain**, located in the city center, emits magical water believed to bless traders with fortune and safe travels.
- Enchanted **river lanterns** that guide boats at night, glowing brighter in response to nearby hazards.

---

### 12. **What is the community atmosphere?**

- **Energetic and ambitious**, with a constant buzz of trade and activity, though the guild conflict has made some residents cautious and distrustful.

---

### 13. **What iconic landmarks are there?**

- **The Terraced Docks** – A marvel of engineering, providing stunning views of the river and facilitating trade.
- **The Riftstone Fountain** – A towering structure of crystalline stone, pulsing faintly with blue light.
- **The Guildhall Spire** – A towering building where the Merchant Council meets, symbolizing the city’s trade-centric identity.
- **The Old Mill** – A defunct mill turned into a gathering place for artists and performers.

---

### 14. **What are the geographical surroundings?**

- Eldermoor lies on the banks of the **River Eldrin**, surrounded by **fertile plains** ideal for farming and vineyards. Dense **woodlands** to the south provide timber, while rocky **bluffs** to the east offer natural defenses and quarry resources.

---

### 15. **How many and what city districts are there?**

- **The Rivermarket District** – The heart of commerce, with bustling shops and warehouses.
- **The Artisan’s Quarter** – Home to smiths, masons, and other craftsmen.
- **The Winefields Ward** – Located on the outskirts, housing the city’s vineyards and granaries.
- **The Docklands** – The lively waterfront area dominated by trade and shipping.
- **The Guildspire District** – The political center, housing the Guildhall Spire and affluent merchant estates.
