### The Current Council of the Star

#### 1. **Nexus Sparrow** - _Warforged Bard and Leader of the Council_

- **Description:** Nexus Sparrow is a unique warforged, crafted with intricate designs that symbolize stars, constellations, and the cosmos itself. Nexus’s voice is deep and resonant, with a metallic, haunting quality that resonates with the people of Kelar. As a bard, Nexus weaves tales of Kelar's history and inspires unity through song and oratory.
- **Personality and Role:** Nexus is wise, calm, and profoundly compassionate, seeing their leadership as a way to protect the people of Kelar from internal strife and outside threats. They have a knack for diplomacy and strategy, always looking for peaceful solutions, though they won’t shy from firm decisions when needed.
- **Mark on Kelar:** Nexus has been instrumental in fostering alliances between Kelar and surrounding territories. Under Nexus's leadership, the council has developed into a cooperative body that values each member's strengths.

#### 2. **Edith "Eddy" Drakemantle** - _Dragonborn Paladin_

- **Description:** Eddy is a bronze-scaled dragonborn with a fierce and determined demeanor. She wields a massive warhammer, and her armor is engraved with the emblem of Kelar’s protective wards.
- **Personality and Role:** Eddy has a strong sense of justice and is unwavering in her dedication to protecting Kelar’s people. Known for her temper, Eddy isn’t one to mince words, but her fierce loyalty and courage make her an invaluable council member.
- **Mark on Kelar:** Eddy spearheaded the establishment of Kelar’s city guard and personally trains new recruits, ensuring the safety and discipline of Kelar’s defenders.

#### 3. **Zyra Windstep** - _Halfling Monk_

- **Description:** Zyra is a quick and nimble halfling, her every move precise and deliberate. She dresses in simple, lightweight garb that allows for fluid movement, and her piercing green eyes hold a steady, contemplative gaze.
- **Personality and Role:** Zyra is quiet, thoughtful, and deeply connected to her philosophy of balance. She values patience and is often a calming influence in council discussions. Zyra advises on matters requiring a careful hand and is known for her insight into moral and ethical issues.
- **Mark on Kelar:** Zyra has founded a small monastery within Kelar, where she teaches the path of harmony and self-discipline, bringing peace to troubled citizens and guiding others in their personal quests.

#### 4. **Myrtle Gamwich** - _Halfling Warlock_

- **Description:** Myrtle is small, with graying curls and a mischievous smile. She has a magical presence and an otherworldly twinkle in her eyes, marked by her pact with an unknown but benevolent entity.
- **Personality and Role:** Myrtle is eccentric, unpredictable, and full of curiosity. She often advises on arcane matters, and her unorthodox views sometimes surprise the other council members. Despite her oddities, she’s a trusted ally.
- **Mark on Kelar:** Myrtle has a small following of young spellcasters who seek her teachings, creating a subtle but profound legacy of magical knowledge and experimentation within the city.

#### 5. **Akhabelle Lightbane** - _Dwarf Rogue_

- **Description:** A short, sturdy dwarf with sharp eyes and a perpetual smirk, Akhabelle is adorned in dark leather, her demeanor mysterious and enigmatic.
- **Personality and Role:** Akhabelle is fiercely independent, preferring to work behind the scenes. She’s an expert in espionage and intelligence gathering, giving the council valuable insight into the city’s hidden threats.
- **Mark on Kelar:** Akhabelle’s network of informants has created an underground intelligence system that keeps Kelar’s council informed of potential dangers and secret plots.

#### 6. **Nabeora Panala** - _Elf Ranger_

- **Description:** With a serene aura and delicate elven features, Nabeora is connected to the wilderness and is often seen with a small animal companion by her side.
- **Personality and Role:** Nabeora is thoughtful and reserved, serving as the council’s connection to the natural world. Her advice is especially valuable in matters regarding nature, wildlife, and sustainability.
- **Mark on Kelar:** Nabeora has cultivated green spaces within Kelar, balancing urban development with nature conservation, making the city a harmonious blend of nature and civilization.

#### 7. **Iloridan Pervyre** - _Half-Elf Bard_

- **Description:** Iloridan is a charismatic half-elf with long, silver hair and a voice that can sway even the hardest hearts. He’s known for his charming and slightly flamboyant nature.
- **Personality and Role:** Iloridan is a diplomat and negotiator, often resolving conflicts within and beyond Kelar. His quick wit and humor lighten council proceedings.
- **Mark on Kelar:** Iloridan has built a school for oratory and the arts, inspiring a new generation of storytellers, diplomats, and performers.

#### 8. **Edladon Qinneiros** - _Half-Elf Rogue_

- **Description:** Edladon is wiry and quiet, with sharp, calculating eyes. He wears unassuming clothing that allows him to blend into crowds easily.
- **Personality and Role:** Edladon is cunning and observant, specializing in security and intelligence. He has a background in covert operations and handles delicate matters that require discretion.
- **Mark on Kelar:** Edladon has implemented a series of protective enchantments throughout the city, quietly boosting Kelar’s defenses without drawing attention.

#### 9. **Arvyn Frosthollow** - _Half-Orc Druid_

- **Description:** A broad-shouldered half-orc with a deep connection to the earth, Arvyn is calm and wise, wearing robes that mirror the textures of moss and bark.
- **Personality and Role:** Arvyn is grounded and compassionate, with a deep respect for life. He advises on issues related to resources and environmental impact.
- **Mark on Kelar:** Arvyn’s influence has helped establish sustainable farming and preservation methods, ensuring that Kelar remains resource-abundant without harming the land.

#### 10. **Arah Mornlight** - _Half-Elf Ranger_

- **Description:** Arah is a fierce, determined ranger with a close bond to animals, particularly her owl companion. She’s known for her skill with a bow and keen instincts.
- **Personality and Role:** Arah is practical and focused, often taking on missions that require scouting and protection of Kelar’s borders.
- **Mark on Kelar:** Arah has organized a network of rangers who patrol the outskirts of Kelar, ensuring the city’s safety and forming a quick-response force for any external threats.

### Fallen Members of the Council and Their Legacy

#### **Bultrak Ingoth** - _Half-Orc Paladin_

- **Legacy:** Bultrak was known for his unwavering dedication and bravery. He gave his life defending Kelar during the Siege of Kelar. His sacrifice became a legend, inspiring statues and tales that encourage citizens to value courage and selflessness.

#### **Bernard Hairyfoot** - _Halfling Barbarian_

- **Legacy:** Bernard was a fearless fighter, small in size but with an indomitable spirit. He defended Kelar’s poorer districts, and his memory is preserved in annual festivals held in his honor, celebrating the strength of the “small but mighty.”

#### **Thidbral Glaurn** - _Human Barbarian_

- **Legacy:** Thidbral was a mountain of a man, as fierce as the storms he fought in. He died holding back a collapsing building during the Siege of Kelar, saving hundreds. The people honor him through Thidbral’s Wall, a reinforced barrier at Kelar’s edge where the building collapsed.

---

### **Kix Zodrak** - _Former Council Member and Half-Orc Druid_

- **Whereabouts:** Kix Zodrak left Kelar in pursuit of ancient knowledge hidden in the dense forests and mountains. She believed Kelar was on the verge of an environmental catastrophe and sought answers from ancient druidic texts.
- **Mark on Kelar:** Kix left behind a collection of her extensive research on nature, ecology, and magical wildlife, which has since been compiled into the _Druidic Codex_. This codex is now used by Kelar’s rangers, druids, and botanists, helping them protect the city’s environment and better understand the surrounding wilderness. Though she is gone, her work continues to guide Kelar's approach to preserving the natural world. Some believe she may one day return with knowledge that could change the city’s fate.

---

This council stands as a tapestry of diverse talents, each member’s contributions shaping the identity and resilience of Kelar. Their legacies, both current and past, ensure that Kelar thrives as a beacon of harmony, strength, and wisdom.
