### City Template: **Iresport**

---

#### **1. Settlement Name:**

**Iresport**

---

#### **2. Settlement Type:**

**City** (Population: 7,500)

---

#### **3. Defining Trait:**

A massive, **fortified harbor** with an intricate system of water locks and docks designed for river barges and trade vessels.

---

#### **4. Claim to Fame:**

Iresport is renowned for its **maritime engineering** and **shipbuilding expertise**, supplying vessels for trade, fishing, and even military purposes.

---

#### **5. Current Calamity:**

A **looming workers’ strike** threatens to halt the production of new ships and repairs. The workers demand better wages and protections after a series of unexplained accidents at the shipyards.

---

#### **6. Local Leaders:**

A **Merchant Guild Council**, led by the ambitious and calculating **Evelyn Trossmoor**, oversees the city. Evelyn is respected for her economic prowess but criticized for her indifference to workers' concerns.

---

#### **7. Noteworthy People:**

- **Gareth Ironwake:** Master shipwright, an outspoken advocate for workers’ rights.
- **Amira Sennova:** Renowned hydromancer who maintains the city’s intricate water lock system.
- **Thalric "Blackhand" Darrow:** A reclusive smuggler rumored to operate out of the harbor’s underbelly.
- **Celia Broadleaf:** Tavern owner and retired adventurer with tales of treasure hidden in the Iresdi Mountains.
- **Brother Naelor:** A priest of the Riverkeeper, offering blessings for safe voyages.

---

#### **8. Noteworthy Places:**

- **The Gilded Anchor Tavern:** A bustling riverside tavern where sailors and merchants gather.
- **Iresport Shipyards:** The city’s lifeblood, producing and repairing vessels of all sizes.
- **The Waterclock Tower:** A timekeeping marvel powered by river currents, serving as a meeting point for locals.
- **Trossmoor Trade Hall:** Headquarters of the Merchant Guild Council, overseeing commerce.
- **Sennova’s Locks:** A marvel of hydromancy, enabling efficient trade and ship movement through the river’s varying depths.

---

#### **9. GP Value of Most Expensive Item for Sale:**

**200,000 GP** (Fine enchanted ships, capable of self-repair or faster travel using elemental magic.)

---

#### **10. Historical Importance:**

Founded as a strategic outpost during the **Great River Wars**, Iresport grew into a shipbuilding hub due to its location and access to the Clearbrook River.

---

#### **11. Magical Elements:**

- **Hydromancy Guild:** Supporting the locks and aiding with naval enchantments.
- **Riverbound Relics:** Ancient, water-infused artifacts found along the riverbed.
- **Frequent Magical Phenomena:** Strange lights and fogs occasionally rise from the river, attributed to leyline disturbances.

---

#### **12. Community Atmosphere:**

**Energetic and Ambitious** – The city is abuzz with activity, as its residents pride themselves on their contributions to Kyria’s trade and naval power.

---

#### **13. Iconic Landmarks:**

- **The Waterclock Tower:** A monumental timekeeping structure and symbol of innovation.
- **The Great Drydock:** The largest of its kind, capable of holding massive vessels.
- **The River Gate:** A fortified entrance to the harbor, protecting against riverborne threats.
- **The Riverkeeper Shrine:** A serene temple devoted to the river deity, adorned with flowing water features.

---

#### **14. Geographical Surroundings:**

- **Clearbrook River:** Wide and deep, facilitating large-scale trade.
- **Fertile Floodplains:** Surround the city, supporting agriculture and ensuring a steady food supply.
- **Distant Iresdi Mountains:** A source of timber and minerals, transported downstream to the city.

---

#### **15. City Districts:**

1. **Harborfront** – Docks, warehouses, and the shipyards.
2. **Artisan’s Wharf** – Workshops and craft halls for shipbuilders and related trades.
3. **Merchant’s Quarter** – Marketplaces and trade halls.
4. **Rivergate Ward** – A residential area for dockworkers and artisans.
5. **Highwater Heights** – Affluent district for merchants and guild leaders.
6. **Temple Row** – Religious and spiritual center, home to the Riverkeeper Shrine.

---

**Iresport** is a thriving, industrious city that balances tradition, innovation, and its ever-present challenges on the banks of the Clearbrook River.
