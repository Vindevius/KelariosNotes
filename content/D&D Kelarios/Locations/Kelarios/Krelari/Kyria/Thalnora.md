1. **Settlement Name:** Thalnora
2. **Type of Settlement:** City
3. **Defining Trait:** The city is built along the rocky cliffs overlooking the **Icelorn Strait**, with a combination of sturdy stone buildings and shipyards, utilizing the natural resources of the surrounding coast and cliffs.
4. **Claim to Fame:** Thalnora is known for its strategic importance as a naval hub and for its shipbuilding expertise. It also hosts an annual sea festival that celebrates the city’s history as a former Veterian outpost.
5. **Current Calamity:** The city faces mounting tensions with the nearby sea giants, as the shifting tides and increasing maritime traffic have disrupted the delicate balance in the strait. There are also whispers of sabotage among the shipbuilding guilds, which could hinder the city’s future trade routes.
6. **Local Leaders:** The city is led by **Lady Anya Frostweld**, a skilled diplomat and military leader who helped secure Thalnora’s transition to Kyria. She is widely respected for her governance and is considered a formidable leader in times of crisis.
7. **Noteworthy People:**
    - **Captain Torik Icevein:** A famous sea captain known for his explorations and expeditions into the northern seas.
    - **Nedra Silvershade:** A merchant who deals in rare artifacts and magical trinkets, some of which are from the strait's ancient past.
    - **Professor Elara Fennmore:** A historian who investigates the city’s Veterian past and the underwater ruins in the strait.
8. **Noteworthy Places:**
    - **The Icebound Tavern:** A cozy, popular tavern known for its warm fires and unique ice-cold drinks served in carved ice mugs.
    - **Frostweld Forge:** A renowned smithy known for crafting exceptional weapons and ship components.
    - **The Glacial Library:** A large repository of knowledge about the northern seas and Veterian history, frequented by scholars.
    - **Silver Needle Apothecary:** Specializes in remedies for sea-related illnesses, frostbite, and common colds.
    - **Bitterwind Market:** A lively market where traders from distant lands sell goods such as textiles, ship parts, and rare artifacts.
9. **Most Expensive Item for Sale:** 150,000 GP – A legendary trident once wielded by a sea king, said to control the tides.
10. **Historical Importance:** Thalnora was once a critical outpost for Veteria, strategically positioned to control access to the northern seas. It later became an important part of Kyria's naval strategy and exploration efforts.
11. **Magical Elements:** The city has ancient ties to water-based magic, with ley lines that flow beneath the Icelorn Strait. There are rumors of underwater ruins and relics imbued with powerful water magic, influencing the city’s maritime practices.
12. **Community Atmosphere:** The community is proud and independent, fiercely loyal to Kyria after having broken away from Veteria. The people are maritime-focused, and while they welcome newcomers, there is a general distrust of those with ties to the old Veterian empire.
13. **Iconic Landmarks:**

- **The Thalnora Keep:** A towering fortress that guards the entrance to the city, a symbol of its military heritage.
- **The Frozen Spire:** A massive ice pillar just off the coast, rumored to be the resting place of an ancient sea deity.
- **The Shattered Arch:** Remnants of an ancient Veterian monument located on the cliffs, offering stunning views of the strait.

14. **Geographical Surroundings:** The city is located along the **Icelorn Strait**, a narrow channel that connects the northern icy seas with the Bay of Trelas. The region is rich in marine life, vital for trade and food, but also home to dangerous creatures that roam the depths of the strait.
15. **City Districts:**

- **The Docklands:** The main trade and transportation hub of the city, home to bustling docks and shipyards.
- **Clifftop Citadel:** The elite district, where the nobility and military leaders reside, overlooking the Icelorn Strait.
- **The Merchant’s Quarter:** The heart of commerce, where markets and trade negotiations take place.
- **The Warrens:** A less glamorous district, home to the working class who maintain the city’s infrastructure, including the shipbuilding industry.
