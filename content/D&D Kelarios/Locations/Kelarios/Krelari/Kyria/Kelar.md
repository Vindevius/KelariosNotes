Kelar is the capital of Kyria, the first country in the world of Kelarios, on the western coast of the continent of Krelari. It has a long history and is the oldest city in the world. The government of Kyria (run by a council of 10 members) is centered at the Council of the Stars in the center of the city next to the Cloudkey Grove.
Beneath the Cloudkey Grove is the only living androsphinx Eradas. He guards the way to the Forge of Verithinus or Void Forge, the only known place with the capacity to forge a soul spike able to kill a god. Soul spikes shred a gods soul into three unrepairable parts, effectively killing the god.
The year is currently 4960. 23 years ago, the War of the Vile Ones raged. During the last days of the war, a demiplane of Morthadius called the Mist broke open. It filled the west coast of the continent of Krelari, including most of Kyria and all of the capital of Kelar, with a dense mist filled with shadows known as Umbraki. These Umbraki were dangerous foes. The mist would clear for most of the day but return at night.
The last battle in the War of the Vile Ones occurred in Kelar. The Vile Ones were three major dragon gods: Domexius, god of dominion, a red greatwyrm; Marcaith, god of affliction, a black greatwyrm; and Morthadius, god of death, a red shadow greatwyrm. Marcaith and Morthadius were defeated elsewhere prior to the final battle and are unimportant to Kelar's history. Domexius, however, laid siege to Kelar with his army from Veteria, a country north of Kelar. This army was led by Domexius's champion King Astrolites Harlor, a red Dragonborn and his lieutenant Heth, a leonin. Domexius and his lieutenant, an ancient red dragon named Vadro the Voiceless, were defeated through the efforts of the party that would come to be known as the Slayers of the Viles Ones and an ancient copper Dragon named Charadraak Quipclaw.
The Slayers of the Vile Ones consisted of the following individuals:
1. Diana Moonstone, a half-elf rogue/warlock and champion of Exsoria the goddess of exile.
2. Queen Leysa Doyle, a high elf druid (circle of the swamp) and champion of Aeontas the god of eternity. Leysa is the queen of Staderia, a country to the east.
3. Orion Ravensilence, a human light cleric of Aeontas, and the grandfather of Diana (on her mother's side)
4. General Comrad Stormbreaker, a blue dragonborn with wings, champion fighter and general of Kyria's 10th army.
5. Derek Nightlock, a half-elf assassin rogue/warlock of Exsoria
6. Aladus, a changeling bard
7. Lydia, Aladus's simulacrum. After the battle, she was gifted true life by the great god Verithinus. 
Leysa defeated Heth, the leonin. General Stormbreaker defeated King Astrolites Harlor in the battle as Domexius watched, clearing the mist around the Cloudkey Grove. Charadraak defeated Vadro the Voiceless in the skies above Kelar. The Slayers of the Vile Ones managed after Charadraak was eaten by Domexius to slay Domexius in the Cloudkey Grove outside the Council of the Stars. Diana dealt the final blow, severing Domexius's head. Diana took in some of the power of Domexius, beginning her ascent into godhood. Asmodeus, lord of Baator and of order, appeared through a portal and took Domexius's power, taking his place in the pantheon of the 21 major deities.
Orion Ravensilence died in the battle, after fighting valiantly with a holy light from Aeontas. Charadraak, who was eaten by Domexius, emerged alive from Domexius's corpse. Each of the surviving Slayers of the Vile Ones was given an epic boon of Verithinus, the god above all the other gods. Diana, Queen Leysa, Derek, Aladus, Lydia, and Charadraak all left the city after the war was over, fading into history. General Comrad Stormbreaker disappeared two years after the war searching for questions to lingering questions from his adventures with the Slayers of the Vile Ones. He was last seen entering the Thilcaith Plateau beyond the Iresdi mountains east of Kyria.
As the Slayers of the Vile Ones fought Domexius, Veterian troops laid siege to the city from the land and sea. A great ship called the Santamaria with its captain, Ardella Sephiran, sunk in the eastern bay. Ardella would be later resurrected and become known as Eris, an avatar of Diana with her new ship the Shadows' Revenge. Veteria was ultimately defeated by Kyria's armies. Kyria expanded after the war, reducing Veteria to a small country in comparison to its once larger empire. The mist around Kelar and the west coast of Krelari disappeared after the war, thanks to Charadraak who cast Wish to reverse its effects.
