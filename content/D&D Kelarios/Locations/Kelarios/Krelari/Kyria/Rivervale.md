1. **Settlement Name**: **Rivervale**
2. **Settlement Type**: **City** (population 7,800)
3. **Defining Trait**: **Impressive Grand Bridge** that spans the junction where the **Arius River** and **Dravon River** meet. The bridge is an architectural marvel, linking the two riverbanks and supporting both trade and local traffic.
4. **Claim to Fame**: **Trade Hub for River Routes**—Rivervale is renowned for its strategic position at the confluence of two major rivers, making it a bustling trade center for goods traveling by boat. It’s also famous for its **riverfront market**, which hosts exotic goods from all over the region.
5. **Current Calamity**: **Flooding and Erosion**—Rivervale's location at the river junction makes it vulnerable to seasonal floods, especially as recent rains have swollen the rivers to dangerous levels. The flooding is eroding the banks and threatening buildings along the waterfront. There is growing concern about the structural integrity of the Grand Bridge and surrounding areas.
6. **Local Leadership**: The city is governed by **Mayor Celina Marrowstone**, a respected former merchant with a sharp mind for commerce and city planning. She works in tandem with the **River Council**, a group of influential trade guilds and river barons who influence the local economy. While Celina is well-liked, the council's influence is contested by rival factions of wealthy traders, and tensions are high due to the recent flooding crisis.
7. **Noteworthy People**:
    - **Bartholomus “Bart” Wren**: A notorious river pirate turned reluctant hero who now works as an informant for the mayor. He is familiar with the hidden routes and backwater channels of the rivers.
    - **Tivella Erindale**: An ambitious young scholar who is conducting research on ancient water magic. She’s become a rising star in Rivervale’s arcane circles, and her discoveries could change the course of river magic.
    - **Ragar the Riverborn**: A half-giant fisherman turned local hero who single-handedly saved several people from a disastrous flood a few years ago. He is known for his immense strength and gentle nature.
    - **Darius Graywind**: A local nobleman who represents one of the most powerful merchant families in Rivervale. His political maneuvering often shapes the course of the River Council’s decisions.
8. **Noteworthy Places**:
    - **The Crooked Oar Tavern**: A riverside tavern known for its laid-back atmosphere and cheap, strong drinks. It serves as the central hub for locals to gossip, and many merchants gather here to discuss business.
    - **Dravon’s Emporium**: A well-known shop that sells rare river goods like furs, spices, and aquatic flora. The owner, Dravon, is a seasoned trader who has an eye for exotic items.
    - **Celina's Keep**: The mayor's residence and offices, a well-fortified mansion that also serves as a meeting place for the River Council. It is located near the Grand Bridge, offering views of the river junction.
    - **Riverside Apothecary**: A magical shop that specializes in potions for water travel and healing, run by the mysterious herbalist **Mira Lornvale**, who is rumored to have connections to the river spirits.
    - **The Flooded Vaults**: An ancient crypt located under Rivervale’s old quarter, said to hold the tombs of early river kings. The vaults are rumored to have hidden treasures and secret passages that some believe lead to forgotten waterways.
9. **Most Expensive Item for Sale**: **200,000 GP** for a **Golden Nautical Globe**, a rare artifact that maps all known rivers, including those thought to be mythical or lost. It is said to have been enchanted by an ancient river sorcerer and can be used to find hidden or dangerous river routes.
10. **Historical Importance**: Rivervale was founded as a settlement by explorers and river traders in an ancient empire's attempt to control the flow of goods through the region. Over the centuries, it grew into a bustling trade city and was the site of numerous battles between rival empires seeking control of the waterways. Rivervale's unique position at the confluence of two rivers allowed it to thrive and become a hub of commerce.
11. **Magical Elements**:
	- **Riverbound Magic**: Local mages specialize in water and river-based magic, often using enchantments to protect boats from storms, enhance crops on the riverbanks, or control the flow of water.
	- **The River Spirits**: The river itself is believed to be home to ancient spirits who guide the waters and protect Rivervale from true disaster. Shamans and river mystics regularly commune with these spirits for guidance.
	- **Hidden Arcane Relic**: There is a legend of a powerful **Water Rune** hidden deep beneath the city in the catacombs. This relic is said to hold the power to control or calm the rivers, making it of great importance to the city’s future.
1. **Community Atmosphere**: **Energetic and Ambitious**—Rivervale is a city of bustling commerce, where people are eager to make their fortune on the river. It’s a place where trade and innovation are valued, and there’s a constant sense of movement and opportunity. However, the rising floodwaters and the recent political tensions have cast a shadow over the city's normally lively and optimistic atmosphere.
2. **Iconic Landmarks**:
	- **The Grand Bridge**: Spanning the junction of the Arius and Dravon Rivers, the bridge is not only a vital transportation hub but also an iconic piece of Rivervale’s architecture. It is adorned with statues of mythical river gods and goddesses.
	- **The River Fountain**: Located in the central plaza, this massive fountain is said to be blessed by the river spirits and is a symbol of the city’s prosperity. Water from the river flows constantly through it, and the water is said to have healing properties.
	- **The Riverspire Tower**: A tall, slender tower built into the riverbank, once used as a lighthouse but now serving as a watchtower and magic research facility. It is the tallest structure in the city.
	- **The Floodwall**: A massive stone wall built to protect the city from flooding. It stretches along the riverbanks and includes several gates and sluices that can control the flow of water.
1. **Geographical Surroundings**: Rivervale lies at the confluence of the **Arius River** and the **Dravon River**, with fertile plains on its eastern side and dense forests to the west. The rivers are vital to the city's trade, as they connect it to distant ports and other cities. The fertile land around the rivers supports agriculture, particularly crops such as rice and grains. The surrounding forests are a source of timber and wildlife.
2. **City Districts**:
	- **Old Quarter**: The original settlement area, with narrow streets, old buildings, and a mix of residential homes, markets, and trade shops.
	- **The Merchant District**: Home to the grand trade halls, warehouses, and offices of wealthy merchants, this district is always bustling with activity.
	- **The Riverfront**: A lively district filled with docks, shipyards, and taverns. It is here where most of Rivervale’s river traffic comes and goes.
	- **The Heights**: The upper-class district where the wealthy, nobles, and important political figures live. It’s located on the higher ground near Celina’s Keep, with stunning views of the rivers.
	- **The Docks and Industrial Area**: Located near the edge of the river, this district is filled with factories, mills, and other industries that rely on the river for their operations. It is often loud and smells of saltwater and industry.
