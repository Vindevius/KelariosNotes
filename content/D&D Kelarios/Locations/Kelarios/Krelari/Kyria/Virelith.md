1. **Settlement Name:** Virelith
2. **Settlement Type:** Town (population 3,200)
3. **Defining Trait:** Virelith is known for its unique architecture, with buildings constructed on stilts and platforms to withstand the frequent tides and occasional flooding. This elevated design gives the town an ethereal, almost floating appearance as it rises out of the bay.
4. **Claim to Fame:** Virelith is renowned for its **Salted Moon Festival**, held annually during the first full moon of the year. The festival celebrates the town’s deep connection to the tides and moon cycles with a week of feasting, music, boat races, and intricate lantern displays that light up the bay at night.
5. **Current Calamity:** The town is suffering from a **mysterious blight** that is slowly killing off the fish stocks in the bay. The main source of income for the people of Virelith is fishing, and the blight is threatening to collapse the local economy. Some suspect dark magic or an ancient curse is behind the strange phenomenon.
6. **Local Leaders:**
    - **Lord Malthus Kreel:** A pragmatic and somewhat reserved lord, Lord Kreel is the appointed ruler of Virelith. He’s been actively seeking a solution to the blight, though his methods have led to suspicion among the townsfolk. There are rumors he may be involved with some unsavory figures, adding to the growing unrest.
    - **The Tidesingers:** A respected council of priests and shamans who worship the moon goddess, Yvara, and who perform important rituals to ensure the health of the bay. They are influential, though their power is being questioned as the blight persists.
7. **Noteworthy People:**
    - **Adira Soren:** A well-known scholar of the arcane, Adira is conducting research into the blight. She has been investigating the connection between the moon cycles and the town’s water sources, suspecting a link to long-forgotten rituals.
    - **Kaleus Lormar:** A former fisherman turned pirate, Kaleus is a brash and reckless individual who operates out of the bay and is rumored to have discovered something in the deeper waters of the bay that could be linked to the blight. He may know more than he’s letting on.
    - **Serefina Valmorin:** A mysterious herbalist and healer who has lived in the town for decades. She is often sought after for her remedies, but she’s also known to possess knowledge of ancient curses and magic. Some suspect her connection to the old ways might be the key to solving the blight.
8. **Noteworthy Places:**
    - **The Tidal Lantern Inn:** A popular tavern known for its warm atmosphere and the spectacular view of the lanterns floating on the bay during the Salted Moon Festival. It serves local delicacies and strong ales, though the innkeeper, Brenna, is rumored to know much more about the dark magic stirring in the bay.
    - **The Salted Wares Market:** A bustling market located by the docks where fishermen and traders sell salted fish, dried seaweed, and other coastal goods. It also hosts black market dealings on occasion, often involving forbidden artifacts or dubious trade.
    - **The Moonlit Altar:** A small temple located on the north side of the bay, built into the rocky hillside. It is dedicated to Yvara, the moon goddess, and it is the site of rituals meant to ensure the safety and prosperity of the town’s fishermen.
    - **Lormar’s Forge:** A smithy run by the former pirate, Kaleus Lormar. While it serves as a normal blacksmith, it’s also a front for his illicit activities, dealing with rare and dangerous artifacts. If one knows the right questions to ask, they can find more than just weapons and tools here.
    - **The Deepwater Cavern:** A secret, hidden cave beneath the town, said to lead into the ancient tunnels beneath the bay. It is said that whoever controls this cave controls the secrets of the town’s foundations, and some believe it holds the key to stopping the blight.
9. **Most Expensive Item for Sale:** A rare **Moonstone Amulet** believed to be capable of controlling the tides, priced at 45,000 GP. This artifact is rumored to have been lost for centuries, but a wealthy collector is offering a substantial price for anyone who can bring it to them.
10. **Historical Importance:** Virelith was once the site of a grand naval battle during the early days of the kingdom’s founding. It was also the location of the **Great Moonfall**, a celestial event where a comet passed close to the town, altering the town’s culture and rituals forever. Legends speak of the comet bringing with it both a curse and a blessing, but its meaning has been lost to time.
11. **Magical Elements:** Virelith is influenced by the **moon’s magic**. The tides themselves seem to hold a strange magical presence, and there are rumors that the town’s location is on top of an ancient leylines nexus that once fueled powerful rituals. The Tidesingers use moon-based magic to bless the fishers, and some locals whisper that the blight is a result of forgotten magic awakening beneath the bay.
12. **Community Atmosphere:** The atmosphere in Virelith is one of quiet anxiety. The mysterious blight has created a sense of urgency, with the community desperately trying to uncover the cause. While friendly, the people are increasingly wary of outsiders, especially anyone showing interest in the deeper mysteries of the bay.
13. **Iconic Landmarks:**
	- **The Crescent Spire:** An ancient tower that stands on one of the hills across the bay. It is said to be the remnants of an old watchtower from the time of the Great Moonfall, and its silhouette against the moonlit sky is a symbol of the town’s connection to the celestial.
	- **The Moon’s Eye Fountain:** A grand fountain located in the town’s central square. The fountain is shaped like a giant eye, with water cascading down in rippling patterns that resemble the phases of the moon. It is said that staring into the fountain during the full moon can reveal glimpses of the future.
	- **The Stilted Walkways:** A series of elevated wooden walkways that connect the various parts of the town, allowing people to move safely across the waterlogged areas. These walkways have become a symbol of resilience in the face of the challenges the town faces.
1. **Geographical Surroundings:** Virelith is nestled at the edge of a small bay that connects to the Bay of Trelas. The surrounding hills provide natural protection from the harsher storms of the open sea, but the bay itself is prone to rising tides. The hills across the bay are rich with dense forests, providing timber and resources for the town. The nearby waters are known for their bountiful fish, though the ongoing blight is putting that resource at risk.
2. **City Districts:**
	- **The Dock District:** The primary area for fishing, trade, and boatbuilding. This district has a rugged, utilitarian feel, with docks, warehouses, and fish markets dotting the landscape.
	- **The Moon District:** The heart of the town, filled with temples, markets, and cultural sites. It is named after the moon goddess Yvara and hosts many of the important rituals and festivals.
	- **The Hill District:** Situated on the northern hills, this district is home to the wealthy and influential, including Lord Kreel’s estate. It offers sweeping views of the bay and is where much of the town’s decision-making happens.
	- **The Forge District:** Home to the town’s blacksmiths, artisans, and the Lormar Forge, this district is filled with the sounds of hammering metal and the smell of freshly worked steel. It also harbors some of the town's more dubious figures.
