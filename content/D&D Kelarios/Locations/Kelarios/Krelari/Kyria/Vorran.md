A city in southern Kyria. [[Akona Dath]] is from here.

The bustling city of **Vorran** sits gracefully on the southern banks of the grand **Arius River**, its spires and stone buildings reaching toward the sky, their reflections dancing upon the water’s surface. This ancient city, the heart of southern **Kyria**, the world’s oldest and first nation, is a hub of commerce, culture, and knowledge. It has stood for centuries, its foundations deep in the history of the land, acting as a living testament to the country’s enduring legacy.

**Vorran** is a city of contrasts—old and new, tradition and innovation. The streets are a patchwork of old cobblestone pathways, worn by the tread of generations, and newer, bustling avenues lined with intricate marble buildings. Many structures have stood for centuries, their walls weathered by time yet maintained with careful reverence for their heritage. Massive archways and bridges connect the city to the rest of Kyria, including a grand bridge that spans the **Arius River** itself, a lifeline for trade and transport between the southern provinces and the rest of the continent.

At the heart of the city stands the **Citadel of Arius**, a massive fortress-palace where the ruling council of **Vorran** conducts their affairs. It is both a symbol of power and a beacon of knowledge, housing ancient scrolls and artifacts that tell the story of the world’s first civilization. The Citadel is built from towering grey stone, its walls adorned with intricate carvings that speak of the city’s glorious past. Surrounding the Citadel are great open courtyards and public squares, where people from all walks of life gather to trade goods, share news, and attend festivals or events in the city’s many theatres and cultural halls.

The **Arius River** itself is vital to the city’s life, providing water, transportation, and fertile land for agriculture. Boats, barges, and ferries traverse the river, bringing goods from the northern and eastern reaches of Kyria, as well as distant lands, to be sold in the markets of **Vorran**. The bustling **Riverfront District** is a hub of commerce, with busy marketplaces filled with traders, artisans, and travelers. The district is alive with the sounds of haggling, the clink of metal, and the calls of merchants hawking everything from exotic spices to rare gems.

The **Academy of Knowledge**, one of the most esteemed institutions in Kyria, is located near the heart of the city, alongside the **Library of Ages**—a towering structure that houses ancient texts, scrolls, and manuscripts collected over the millennia. Scholars from across the world come to **Vorran** to study under the guidance of the city’s most learned minds, and it is here that many of Kyria’s great leaders and thinkers are trained.

Vorran's **Warded District** is its most secure and mysterious quarter. Enclosed by high, magical barriers to keep the city’s arcane secrets safe, this area houses some of the most powerful mages in the world, as well as powerful relics and magical items. It is rumored that the city’s connection to ancient, magical knowledge is strongest here, hidden deep within the district’s mystical walls.

The **southern gates** of **Vorran** open into the fertile agricultural region known as **Selvona Plains**, where the land is rich and crops are plentiful. It is a peaceful area, where families farm the riverbanks, cultivating food and goods for the city’s ever-growing population. The **Riverway**, a winding road that leads from the gates to the plains, is often filled with wagons hauling the latest harvests, a constant stream of activity that never stops.

**Vorran’s** people are proud of their city’s ancient history but are also ever-driven toward progress. The blend of old-world charm with new advancements gives the city a unique vibrancy, with towering stone buildings side-by-side with newer, more innovative structures of glass and iron. The city hums with the energy of the past and the future, and those who live there are as deeply tied to the ideals of **Kyria’s** foundational culture as they are to the present moment.

**Vorran** is, without a doubt, the jewel of southern **Kyria**, a city that represents the grandeur and the wisdom of the world’s oldest civilization, where knowledge, power, and tradition come together in harmony. The river, the heart of the land, flows through it as a symbol of both the life force of the city and the deep current of history that runs beneath its vibrant surface.
