1. **Settlement Name:** Ilyria
2. **Settlement Type:** City (population 9,000)
3. **Defining Trait:** Ilyria is known for its stunning, multi-tiered canals that snake throughout the city, with boat transportation being the primary mode of travel. The canals are lined with luxurious villas and vibrant marketplaces, and boats of all sizes constantly drift along the waters.
4. **Claim to Fame:** Ilyria is famous for its annual **Festival of the Tides**, a celebration of the sea, where participants race elaborate, brightly decorated boats through the canals, culminating in a grand feast with seafood delicacies and aquatic-themed performances.
5. **Current Calamity:** The city is facing an escalating conflict between the merchant guilds over control of the lucrative fishing and shipbuilding industries. This rivalry has turned violent, with skirmishes in the streets and sabotaged boats threatening the city’s economy and the safety of its citizens.
6. **Local Leaders:**
    - **Mayor Selene Aurelian:** A strong-willed and charismatic leader who seeks to bring stability to the city amidst the guild conflicts. However, her authority is contested by the influential merchant families.
    - **The Syndicate Council:** A council of the wealthiest merchant families that effectively controls much of the city's economy. They are highly influential but also divided due to the ongoing power struggle.
7. **Noteworthy People:**
    - **Kieran Voss:** A daring pirate-turned-merchant who operates in the Bay of Trelas. He has a large following and is rumored to secretly fund one of the guilds, though his loyalties are unclear.
    - **Rina Verlan:** A famed architect and designer responsible for the grand bridges and impressive stonework around the city. Her designs have earned her the admiration of both locals and visitors alike.
    - **Captain Astrid Lunari:** A veteran naval officer who serves as the leader of Ilyria’s defense force. She’s been trying to quell the rise in violence between the guilds, often working with mercenaries to keep the peace.
    - **Elandor Sagewind:** A mysterious, reclusive scholar who lives on the outskirts of the city and is known to study ancient maritime lore, including rumored sunken cities and legendary sea creatures.
8. **Noteworthy Places:**
    - **The Silver Gull Tavern:** A lively tavern by the canal’s edge, popular among sailors, pirates, and adventurers. It’s known for its strong rum, fresh oysters, and occasional brawls between rival factions.
    - **The Tidesmith’s Forge:** A famous smithy where some of the finest ship parts are made, from anchors to hull reinforcements. The forge also crafts exquisite weaponry for Ilyria’s sailors.
    - **The Floating Market:** A vibrant, bustling market that drifts along the canals on barges, offering everything from exotic spices and trinkets to freshly caught fish and rare seaweed.
    - **The Wavecrest Academy:** A prestigious school of shipbuilding and maritime magic, where the best shipwrights and navigators are trained. The academy is known for its cutting-edge research in magical navigation and weather manipulation.
    - **The Coral Tomb:** A hidden underground crypt beneath the city, said to house ancient relics of a civilization that once ruled the island. The tomb is only accessible by boat through secret passages in the canals.
9. **Most Expensive Item for Sale:** A rare, enchanted **Deep Sea Diver's Helm** valued at 150,000 GP. This helm, said to be from an ancient sunken city, grants the wearer the ability to breathe underwater and communicate with sea creatures.
10. **Historical Importance:** Ilyria was founded centuries ago by explorers seeking to capitalize on the rich marine resources of the Bay of Trelas. Over the years, it grew into a key naval city and trading hub. It is built upon the ruins of an ancient, long-forgotten civilization that once thrived in the region, with remnants of their technology still hidden beneath the city.
11. **Magical Elements:** The waters surrounding Ilyria are imbued with minor arcane energies, which have been harnessed by some of the city’s most talented shipbuilders and mages. The **Wavecrest Academy** is a center for maritime magic, specializing in spells to calm storms, navigate the seas, and protect ships. There are also rumors of a hidden **Leyline Nexus** beneath the Bay of Trelas that amplifies magical abilities related to water and the ocean.
12. **Community Atmosphere:** Ilyria is a city of contrasts. It is a vibrant and lively place during the day, full of activity and commerce. However, there is an undercurrent of tension due to the guild conflict, and while most citizens are friendly and welcoming, there is a sense of unease about the future.
13. **Iconic Landmarks:**
	- **The Bridge of Tides:** A grand, arched bridge that spans across the main canal in the city. It is adorned with intricate carvings of sea creatures and is considered a symbol of Ilyria’s connection to the ocean.
	- **The Great Lighthouse of Ilyria:** Standing tall on the western edge of the island, this massive lighthouse is visible for miles and has guided countless ships safely through the Bay of Trelas. It is also rumored to be enchanted, with a mystical light that has kept Ilyria safe from storms for centuries.
	- **The Waterfall Gardens:** A serene, tranquil garden located on a small island just off the coast of Ilyria. Its cascading waterfalls are said to have healing properties, and it is a popular spot for meditation.
1. **Geographical Surroundings:** Ilyria is situated on a lush, mountainous island in the Bay of Trelas. The island is surrounded by clear, rich waters teeming with fish, while its interior is covered in dense forests that provide timber for the shipbuilding industry. The surrounding bay is known for sudden, violent storms, which add an element of danger to the city’s maritime endeavors. The island's strategic location makes it a prime target for both trade and conflict.
2. **City Districts:**
	- **The Canal District:** The heart of Ilyria, where the city’s canals are most densely packed. It is full of markets, shipyards, and the city’s working-class population.
	- **The Merchant District:** A wealthy area near the main docks where the elite conduct business. It is home to luxurious villas, upscale shops, and the headquarters of the merchant guilds.
	- **The Old Quarter:** A historic district where the ancient ruins of past civilizations can be found. This area is quieter and often visited by scholars and those looking to explore Ilyria’s past.
	- **The Lighthouse District:** Home to the Great Lighthouse and the city’s military garrison. This district is dedicated to protecting the city from external threats and overseeing maritime defenses.
