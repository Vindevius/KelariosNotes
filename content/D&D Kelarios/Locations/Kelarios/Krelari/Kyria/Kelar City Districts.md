### 1. Old Kelar – **Aeontas, God of Eternity**

**Description:**  
Old Kelar is the historic heart of the city, with structures dating back centuries. The architecture is largely untouched by time, with well-preserved stone buildings and ancient inscriptions honoring heroes of the past. A subtle, eternal enchantment preserves the buildings, making them immune to decay.

**Vibe and Population:**  
Residents here tend to be traditionalists and scholars, upholding the legacies of past eras. Many families have lived in Old Kelar for generations, and they maintain ancestral records, believing it is their duty to remember and respect those who came before.

---

### 2. White Forge – **Numeda, Goddess of Strength**

**Description:**  
This district is known for its forges, where blacksmiths work tirelessly at anvils and furnaces. The clang of metal echoes through the streets, and the air smells of smoke and molten metal. Weapons, armor, and intricate metal sculptures are produced here.

**Vibe and Population:**  
The district draws artisans, warriors, and laborers. People here are practical, industrious, and loyal, embodying the strength that Numeda values. It’s not uncommon to see sparring sessions and competitions of physical prowess, paying homage to the goddess of strength.

---

### 3. Skylight Market – **Sovenia, Goddess of Wisdom**

**Description:**  
Skylight Market is bustling with intellectuals, traders, and collectors of rare knowledge. Booksellers, magical artifact vendors, and herbalists are common. The market has a reputation for housing rare and esoteric goods, often imbued with wisdom or enchantment.

**Vibe and Population:**  
Merchants, scholars, and curious minds gather here, making it a lively place for conversation and trade. The air buzzes with debates on philosophy, politics, and magic. Sovenia’s influence makes this district a melting pot of ideas.

---

### 4. Mistlight Gate – **Efimera, Goddess of the Ephemeral**

**Description:**  
Mistlight Gate is shrouded in mist, giving it a mystical and eerie atmosphere. It has narrow alleys and hidden alcoves, and there are shrines to lost loved ones in several places. Efimera’s followers honor the transient nature of life here.

**Vibe and Population:**  
This district attracts poets, artists, and mourners. People come to meditate on life’s fleeting moments, leaving offerings to remember those who have passed. The area has a quiet, introspective feel, where whispers seem to carry in the mist.

---

### 5. Pearl Hall – **Medicarma, Goddess of Healing**

**Description:**  
Pearl Hall is a serene district known for its healing centers, herbalist shops, and alchemical apothecaries. The streets are lined with blossoming plants, and fountains are common, symbolizing the restorative power of Medicarma.

**Vibe and Population:**  
Healers, clerics, and patients in need of physical or spiritual healing frequent this area. It has a calm, soothing atmosphere, and the people here tend to be compassionate and service-oriented. Festivals of renewal and health are often celebrated here.

---

### 6. Clay Shore – **Vizoia, Goddess of Life**

**Description:**  
Located near the city’s river, Clay Shore is home to Kelar’s potters, ceramic artists, and farmers. The district’s lush, green spaces and flowing water make it feel alive and vibrant.

**Vibe and Population:**  
Clay Shore attracts artists, gardeners, and people who celebrate the beauty of life. Vizoia’s followers often hold community events and feasts, emphasizing the joy of living. It’s a lively area filled with color, creativity, and laughter.

---

### 7. Dock Ward – **Vecmetus, God of Travel**

**Description:**  
The Dock Ward is Kelar’s waterfront, bustling with ships, merchants, and travelers. It’s filled with the sounds of waves, seagulls, and dockworkers calling out to one another.

**Vibe and Population:**  
Sailors, merchants, and adventurers gather here. There’s a sense of adventure and wanderlust, with people sharing stories of far-off lands. Vecmetus’s followers often give blessings to those about to embark on journeys.

---

### 8. Grimbloom – **Zehir, God of Affliction (formerly Marcaith)**

**Description:**  
This district was once dedicated to Marcaith, god of affliction, but since his fall, Zehir has taken over. It’s a shadowy area where poisons, curses, and afflictions are studied and understood.

**Vibe and Population:**  
Alchemists, warlocks, and those seeking forbidden knowledge often gather here. There’s a sinister edge to Grimbloom, with an undercurrent of secrecy and fear. It’s known as a place to seek out remedies for curses, though some are wary of its reputation.

---

### 9. Darkwater – **Asmodeus, God of Dominion (formerly Domexius)**

**Description:**  
This district, originally the stronghold of Domexius, is now dedicated to Asmodeus. It has a regal but oppressive atmosphere, with grand buildings and strict order.

**Vibe and Population:**  
Merchants, bureaucrats, and those seeking power congregate here. The people are organized and efficient, and Asmodeus’s influence is seen in the rigid hierarchy. There’s a sense of ambition, with everyone seeking to assert their dominance.

---

### 10. Greenfall Steps – **Neglamel, God of Neglect**

**Description:**  
Greenfall Steps is a more neglected area of Kelar, where abandoned buildings and overgrown vegetation are common. However, there’s beauty in the decay, with wildflowers and ivy reclaiming the stone structures.

**Vibe and Population:**  
Those who are overlooked or forgotten find solace here. It’s a quiet, melancholic district that draws artists and loners. Neglamel’s influence fosters a sense of acceptance of abandonment, creating a bittersweet atmosphere.

---

### 11. Ashbloom End – **Ruichasm, God of Destruction**

**Description:**  
Ashbloom End is known for its rubble-strewn streets and burnt-out structures, remnants of past conflicts. It’s a place of ruin but also rebirth, where people rebuild what’s been destroyed.

**Vibe and Population:**  
This district attracts revolutionaries, artisans, and visionaries who believe in creation through destruction. Ruichasm’s followers often hold rituals to destroy old relics or symbols, believing in the transformative power of destruction.

---

### 12. Fenwind – **Signosia, Goddess of Knowledge**

**Description:**  
Fenwind is filled with libraries, schools, and magical academies. The architecture is grand, with towers and spires, and the streets are often crowded with scholars and apprentices.

**Vibe and Population:**  
Students, mages, and philosophers reside here, making it a hub for learning and intellectual pursuit. Signosia’s influence inspires curiosity and a love of knowledge, and people here are always eager to learn and share ideas.

---

### 13. Stonecrest – **Zorvia, Goddess of Force**

**Description:**  
Stonecrest is a district of towering stone fortresses and training grounds. It’s known for producing Kelar’s finest soldiers and is home to the city’s military academy.

**Vibe and Population:**  
Warriors and tacticians train here, embodying the unyielding strength of Zorvia. The atmosphere is disciplined and intense, with drills and sparring matches a common sight.

---

### 14. Stone Court – **Seravius, God of Energy**

**Description:**  
Stone Court is alive with vibrant energy, filled with enchanters, elementalists, and inventors. The district pulses with magic, and experiments with different types of energy are commonplace.

**Vibe and Population:**  
Mages, artificers, and energetic personalities are drawn here. The district has a lively, frenetic atmosphere where new inventions and spells are constantly being tested.

---

### 15. Pale Gate Market – **Topolus, God of Location**

**Description:**  
A market district that acts as a nexus of trade and information. It’s where travelers first arrive, and it serves as a gateway to the rest of the city.

**Vibe and Population:**  
Merchants, guides, and newcomers populate this district. There’s a sense of transience, with people always coming and going, guided by Topolus’s influence.

---

### 16. New Hearth – **Valeia, God of Health**

**Description:**  
New Hearth is a comfortable residential area with clean streets, herbal gardens, and medical facilities. It’s where many of the city’s families reside, and Valeia’s influence fosters community wellness.

**Vibe and Population:**  
Families, caretakers, and medics live here, giving it a warm, nurturing atmosphere. People are friendly and community-focused, hosting gatherings to celebrate health and life.

---

### 17. Grimbark Ward – **The Raven Queen, God of Death (formerly Morthadius)**

**Description:**  
Grimbark Ward has taken on a somber, respectful air after Morthadius’s fall. It’s home to Kelar’s graveyards and mausoleums, where followers of the Raven Queen honor the dead.

**Vibe and Population:**  
Mourners, clerics of death, and those who seek to understand mortality gather here. The district is quiet and reflective, with a reverence for death as part of the cycle of life.

---

### 18. New Tower – **Tempronus, God of Time**

**Description:**  
New Tower is dominated by a grand clocktower, symbolizing the passage of time. Timekeepers, astrologers, and chronomancers work here, studying and recording the flow of time.

**Vibe and Population:**  
Historians, fortune-tellers, and those curious about time’s mysteries are drawn here. The atmosphere is contemplative, with an air of patience and foresight.

---

### 19. Timber Gate – **Eriseris, God of Wilderness**

**Description:**  
This district is closer to nature than most, with gardens, parks, and wooden structures. Timber Gate serves as a meeting ground for hunters, rangers, and druids.

**Vibe and Population:**  
Nature lovers, druids, and scouts reside here, respecting Eriseris’s call to live in harmony with the wild. The district has a rugged charm, with open spaces and natural beauty.

---

### 20. Clear Valley – **Nafursa, Goddess of Nature**

**Description:**  
Clear Valley is lush and green, with fields of wildflowers, orchards, and small ponds. It’s a serene escape within the city, emphasizing the abundance and tranquility of nature.

**Vibe and Population:**  
Farmers, herbalists, and those who worship nature’s beauty live here. It’s a calm district, where the people are harmonious and connected to the land.

---

### 21. Grimcrest – **Exsoria, Goddess of Exile**

**Description:**  
Grimcrest is where Kelar’s outcasts reside, a place for those shunned or unwanted. It’s a gritty district, with makeshift homes and secret meeting places for those who live on society’s fringes.

**Vibe and Population:**  
Outcasts, rebels, and those seeking freedom from society’s constraints gather here. Exsoria’s influence gives the district a sense of defiance and resilience, where the exiled create their own sense of belonging.
