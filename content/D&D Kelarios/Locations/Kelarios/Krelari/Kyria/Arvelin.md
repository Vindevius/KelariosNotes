### City Template: **Arvelin**

---

#### **1. Settlement Name:**

**Arvelin**

---

#### **2. Settlement Type:**

**City** (Population: 7,800)

---

#### **3. Defining Trait:**

Arvelin boasts a **dual-river citadel**, a massive stone fortress built where the two rivers converge, serving as a trade hub and defensive stronghold.

---

#### **4. Claim to Fame:**

Arvelin is famous for its **glassmaking industry**, producing exquisite stained glass and mirrors prized across Kyria and Veteria.

---

#### **5. Current Calamity:**

The recent annexation by Kyria has left the city divided. **Loyalists to Veteria** and Kyria's new administration clash in secret, threatening to ignite outright rebellion.

---

#### **6. Local Leaders:**

- **Governor Elthira Voswyn:** A shrewd but beleaguered Kyrian appointee attempting to enforce Kyria's rule.
- **Dovran Harel:** A former Veterian knight turned underground resistance leader.
- **Archcleric Varros Pelnis:** A neutral religious figure from the Temple of the Twin Rivers, mediating between the factions.

---

#### **7. Noteworthy People:**

- **Tallis Wrenwright:** A master glassmaker experimenting with infused magical glass.
- **Iven Mossglen:** A merchant dealing in river trade, rumored to fund the resistance.
- **Lirya Fenshart:** An exiled Veterian diplomat gathering information for her homeland.
- **Korath Ironjaw:** A Kyrian captain charged with maintaining order, known for his harsh methods.
- **Elya “Shimmerblade” Vorath:** A mercenary hired by the city council to protect trade caravans.

---

#### **8. Noteworthy Places:**

- **The Riverview Rest:** A popular riverside tavern known for its smoked fish and nightly performances.
- **Crimson Glassworks:** A workshop producing the city’s famed stained glass, now guarded due to sabotage attempts.
- **The Conflux Bazaar:** A sprawling open market at the river confluence, hosting traders from both Kyria and Veteria.
- **Twin Rivers Temple:** A grand structure with a domed ceiling of enchanted stained glass depicting the gods of water and commerce.
- **The Iron Lock Armory:** A forge and armory supplying weapons and tools for both civilians and guards.

---

#### **9. GP Value of Most Expensive Item for Sale:**

**200,000 GP** (A set of enchanted stained-glass armor that enhances stealth and reflects light-based spells.)

---

#### **10. Historical Importance:**

Arvelin was a **key military outpost** during the war of the Vile Ones, holding off invasions from both the north and south.

---

#### **11. Magical Elements:**

- **Glass of Memories:** Experimental enchanted glass that can replay past events when light passes through it.
- **River Conflux Aura:** A natural phenomenon at the river junction that amplifies water-based magic.
- **Leyline Nexus:** The rivers converge over a leyline, subtly enhancing the magical craft in the city.

---

#### **12. Community Atmosphere:**

**Tense and divided** – While trade still flows, the city’s residents are uneasy, with quiet suspicions festering on all sides.

---

#### **13. Iconic Landmarks:**

- **The Dual-River Citadel:** A fortress straddling the rivers, offering both protection and an impressive administrative hub.
- **Conflux Arch:** A grand stone archway at the rivers’ meeting point, etched with ancient Veterian and Kyrian runes.
- **The Starlit Dome:** The stained glass ceiling of the Twin Rivers Temple, which sparkles at night as if imbued with starlight.
- **Veil Falls:** A small but picturesque waterfall just north of the city, rumored to conceal an entrance to a forgotten cave system.

---

#### **14. Geographical Surroundings:**

Arvelin lies at the **convergence of two rivers**, surrounded by **fertile floodplains** that produce crops and support fishing. To the east are **low, forested hills**, offering timber and wild game. The rivers provide robust trade routes leading to Tha Ethela and other key settlements.

---

#### **15. City Districts:**

1. **Citadel Ward:** Administrative and military hub, home to the Dual-River Citadel.
2. **Glassmaker’s Quarter:** Center of the glassmaking industry.
3. **Conflux Market District:** Bustling area with shops, traders, and inns near the river junction.
4. **Temple District:** Dominated by the Twin Rivers Temple and surrounding clergy housing.
5. **Lower Dockside:** Residential and working-class area along the rivers, housing many fishermen and laborers.
6. **Old Veterian Quarter:** The former political center, now a hotbed for resistance activity.

---

**Arvelin** stands at the crossroads of rivers and empires, a city caught between past allegiances and an uncertain future.
