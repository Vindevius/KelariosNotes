### Town Template: **Sevrenthall**

---

#### **1. Settlement Name:**

**Sevrenthall**

---

#### **2. Settlement Type:**

**Town** (Population: 4,200)

---

#### **3. Defining Trait:**

Sevrenthall is surrounded by a **perimeter of watchtowers**, built to monitor the nearby lake (Lake Lirienne) and Tha Ethela across its waters.

---

#### **4. Claim to Fame:**

The town is known for its **expert shipbuilding**, crafting swift and durable lake vessels used for both trade and military patrols.

---

#### **5. Current Calamity:**

Tensions between Kyrian troops stationed in Sevrenthall and the local Veterian population have reached a boiling point, leading to **riots and acts of sabotage** against the outpost.

---

#### **6. Local Leaders:**

- **Captain Halrek Droslin:** The Kyrian military officer in charge of the outpost, stern and pragmatic.
- **Mayor Yelvara Aetherwind:** A Veterian-born leader trying to balance local concerns with Kyria’s demands.
- **Miren Solcrest:** A charismatic rabble-rouser, leading clandestine resistance efforts against Kyrian rule.

---

#### **7. Noteworthy People:**

- **Toran Myleth:** A Veterian shipwright whose vessels are secretly equipped with hidden compartments for smuggling.
- **Priestess Lienna Morveil:** Overseer of the local temple, advocating for peace through diplomacy.
- **Brennik Vosh:** A Kyrian quartermaster, known for running a black-market trade in rare goods.
- **Elois “Flicker” Darnes:** A former spy who acts as an informant for both sides, manipulating events for personal gain.

---

#### **8. Noteworthy Places:**

- **The Drifting Lantern:** A lakeside tavern where locals, soldiers, and merchants mingle uneasily.
- **Myleth Shipworks:** The town’s primary shipyard, essential to both trade and military operations.
- **The Emberlight Watchtower:** The largest and most fortified of Sevrenthall’s watchtowers, overlooking the lake.
- **Silverlake Market:** A bustling marketplace known for fresh fish, exotic spices, and black-market dealings.
- **Temple of Harmonious Waters:** A serene temple dedicated to gods of peace and balance, with an enchanted reflecting pool.

---

#### **9. GP Value of Most Expensive Item for Sale:**

**2,000 GP** (A finely crafted Kyrian warship fitted for speed and stealth.)

---

#### **10. Historical Importance:**

Sevrenthall served as a **key harbor** during the War of the Vile Ones, enabling Veterian troops to launch surprise attacks across the lake.

---

#### **11. Magical Elements:**

- **Lake Spirits:** The waters around Sevrenthall are believed to house benevolent spirits that bless sailors.
- **Magical Drydock:** Part of the shipyard is enchanted to speed up repairs and construction.
- **Warded Watchtowers:** The perimeter towers are reinforced with magical glyphs to detect intruders.

---

#### **12. Community Atmosphere:**

**Wary and secretive** – The locals resent Kyria’s occupation but fear open rebellion, creating an air of distrust and unease.

---

#### **13. Iconic Landmarks:**

- **The Sevrenthall Beacon:** A massive lighthouse marking the town’s harbor, said to be powered by ancient Veterian magic.
- **The Mirror Gate:** An arched stone bridge reflecting perfectly in the lake’s waters, rumored to grant visions of the past.
- **The Anchor’s Stand:** A rusted iron statue of a ship’s anchor, commemorating those who died defending the town in past wars.

---

#### **14. Geographical Surroundings:**

Sevrenthall lies on the **southern shores of Lake Lirienne**, surrounded by lush forests to the south and rolling plains to the east. The lake provides plentiful fish, while the forests offer timber for shipbuilding.

---

#### **15. City Districts:**

1. **Harbor District:** Home to docks, the shipyard, and the Drifting Lantern tavern.
2. **Market Square:** A lively center of trade, legal and illicit alike.
3. **Watchtower Perimeter:** Encircles the town, manned by Kyrian guards.
4. **Temple Quarter:** Houses the Temple of Harmonious Waters and residences for clergy.
5. **Residential Core:** A mix of Veterian and Kyrian homes, with a palpable sense of tension.

**Lake Lirienne** – A vast and tranquil body of water separating Sevrenthall from Tha Ethela.

Sevrenthall stands as both a beacon of trade and a reminder of the fragile peace between Kyria and Veteria, its serene surroundings hiding deep unrest.
