### Kelar: Overview of the Oldest City in Kelarios

**City Vibe:**  
Kelar exudes a sense of history and resilience, having weathered ancient wars, powerful gods, and the wrath of dragon deities. The architecture is a blend of ancient stone structures and modern repairs, with a range of influences from its diverse districts. Due to its past encounters with divine and draconic forces, the residents of Kelar often hold a quiet reverence for the gods and the legendary heroes who defended the city.

**Climate and Daily Life:**  
Located on the western coast of Krelari, Kelar experiences cool, misty mornings with clear skies later in the day. Nighttime still carries an eerie chill, a memory of the mist that once plagued the city during the War of the Vile Ones. Streets are lined with vendors from Skylight Market hawking wares, citizens sharing tales of the Slayers of the Vile Ones, and guards stationed around Cloudkey Grove to protect the Council of the Stars.

### Key Locations

1. **Cloudkey Grove and the Council of the Stars:**  
    This sacred center is where the government operates. The Council of the Stars, a council of ten members, leads from here. Beneath the grove lies Eradas, the last living androsphinx, guarding the Forge of Verithinus. Only the council and those deemed worthy may approach Eradas, who will test any intruders mercilessly.
2. **Temple of the Seven-Pointed Star:**  
    Located on White Hill near Old Kelar, this grand temple to Verithinus serves as a place of worship, drawing clerics, scholars, and those seeking divine guidance. The temple’s architecture mirrors the shape of the seven-pointed star in Kelarios stars, reinforcing its connection to Verithinus.
3. **Thingamabobs (Skylight Market):**  
    This quirky shop, run by Varian Darwin Ravensilence and his partner, specializes in magical and mechanical curiosities. Their adopted tiefling twins, Jessica and Jeremy, assist around the shop, giving it a familial and vibrant atmosphere. The shop is particularly popular among young artificers and curious travelers.
4. **The Weary Pirate (Dock Ward):**  
    A lively tavern where locals and sailors gather. Jacob, the bartender, has a reputation for handling unruly patrons with his invisible shotgun. The place is a popular haunt for adventurers and storytellers who regale patrons with tales of the Slayers and battles against the Vile Ones.
5. **Tahiti Senior Center (Clear Valley):**  
    Known as "a wonderful place," this center provides care for the elderly, including many retired adventurers and scholars. It's well-respected, and Orion Ravensilence once lived here before joining the fight against Domexius. The center often hosts historical storytelling nights, where residents recount tales of Kelar’s past.

### Districts Overview

Each district in Kelar holds distinct cultural ties to a specific deity and has its own unique characteristics and traditions:

1. **Old Kelar (Aeontas - God of Eternity):**  
    The historic heart of the city, filled with ancient buildings and narrow cobblestone streets. It’s revered as a symbol of Kelar’s endurance. Residents are known for their steadfast loyalty and often venerate Aeontas.
    
2. **White Forge (Numeda - Goddess of Strength):**  
    This district is home to Kelar’s most skilled blacksmiths and artisans. The forges here never sleep, producing arms and armor revered across Kyria. Numeda’s followers often perform strength contests in her honor.
    
3. **Skylight Market (Sovenia - Goddess of Wisdom):**  
    A bustling trade hub where exotic goods, mystical relics, and intellectual exchanges occur. Scholars and inventors flock to this area, seeking inspiration from Sovenia’s blessings.
    
4. **Mistlight Gate (Efimera - Goddess of the Ephemeral):**  
    Known for its ghostly atmosphere and mist-shrouded alleys, Mistlight Gate is a place of reflection. Followers of Efimera often perform rituals here to honor memories and transient experiences.
    
5. **Pearl Hall (Medicarma - Goddess of Healing):**  
    A serene district focused on healing arts, herbalism, and alchemical remedies. Pearl Hall’s central hospice offers care to wounded and sick adventurers from across Krelari.
    
6. **Dock Ward (Vecmetus - God of Travel):**  
    The bustling waterfront, filled with ships and sea merchants. Vecmetus’s followers often gather here, blessing travelers and bidding them safe journeys.
    
7. **Grimbloom (Zehir - God of Affliction):**  
    A darker area formerly dedicated to Marcaith, Grimbloom is now shrouded in mystery. Zehir’s influence has given rise to shadowy gatherings and alchemical experiments on curses and afflictions.
    
8. **Darkwater (Asmodeus - God of Dominion):**  
    Once Domexius’s domain, this district has taken on a regal yet oppressive atmosphere. Asmodeus’s followers here are often well-organized and run a powerful mercantile guild.
    
9. **Clear Valley (Nafursa - Goddess of Nature):**  
    A peaceful farming district on Kelar’s outskirts, Clear Valley is blessed by Nafursa. It provides much of the city’s fresh produce and holds seasonal celebrations for planting and harvest.

### Significant NPCs Post-War

1. **Nexus Sparrow (Council Leader):**  
    A warforged bard who has led the council through many conflicts. Nexus’s logical yet artistic approach brings a balanced vision to Kelar’s governance.
2. **Edith “Eddy” Drakemantle (Replacing Bultrak):**  
    A dragonborn paladin, deeply committed to the city’s safety, often patrolling the streets to assure citizens of their protection.
3. **Zyra Windstep (Replacing Bernard):**  
    A halfling monk with unparalleled agility. Her street performances in Skylight Market also serve as a reminder of her physical prowess.
4. **Arah Mornlight (Replacing Kix Zodrak):**  
    A half-elven ranger with a deep connection to the spirits of nature, Arah often acts as a mediator between Kelar and the neighboring forest communities.
5. **Arvyn Frosthollow (Replacing Thidbral):**  
    A stoic, half-orc druid who took Kix Zodrak’s place, Arvyn is known for his earthy wisdom and is beloved by the people of Clear Valley.

### Cultural and Historical Significance

Kelar’s past is woven with the tales of gods and dragons, and its residents honor the memory of the Slayers of the Vile Ones through yearly festivals, reenactments, and statues around the Cloudkey Grove. On the anniversary of the Siege of Kelar, citizens hold a candlelit procession through the Cloudkey Grove to honor Orion Ravensilence and all those who fell in battle.

The **Echo Festival**, held in Mistlight Gate, celebrates the city’s endurance by honoring departed spirits and recalling the memories of those lost to the Mist. People share stories, dress in costumes that represent the Umbraki, and thank Charadraak, the copper dragon, for his Wish that dispelled the Mist.
